## 一、Part I Vocabulary · Section A（卷面 20 题，源仅存第 1 题）

Directions: There are 20 incomplete sentences in this part. Choose the one answer that best completes the sentence.

### 第 1 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
Diagnosis is the process of ______ a name to a patient's condition.

A. assigning　　B. judging　　C. following　　D. encountering
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**A（据课文推断）

诊断课文：Diagnosis is the process of assigning a name to a patient's condition。assign a name = 赋以病名。judging 判断，following 随访，encountering 遭遇。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：诊断定义"}
| 概念 | 课文表述 |
|------|----------|
| diagnosis | assigning a name to a condition |
| manifestations | symptoms, signs, laboratory abnormalities |
| pathogenesis | sequence from cause to manifestations |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
diagnosis = **assigning** a name to the condition。
:::

## 二、Part I Vocabulary · Section B（源仅存第 21 题）

Directions: Choose the ONE closest in meaning to the underlined part.

### 第 21 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
The direct **effects** of injury are referred to as necrosis if cells are killed in the injured area and sublethal cell injury if the injured cells are capable of recovery.

A. influences　　B. results　　C. courses　　D. actions
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**B（据词义推断）

此处 effects = 损伤的直接后果，与 results 最近。influences 偏「影响」，courses 病程，actions 行动。同一句后半：细胞死亡称 necrosis，可恢复称 sublethal cell injury。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：effects"}
direct effects of injury：致死 → necrosis；可恢复 → sublethal cell injury。effects ≈ results。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
injury 的 direct **effects** ≈ **results**；致死 = necrosis。
:::

## 三、Part II Reading Comprehension（卷面 4 篇 / 第 36–55 题；源仅存 Passage I 开头 + 第 36–40 题 + 第 55 题）

Directions: There are 4 passages. Decide on the best choice.

### 第 36 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
**Passage I（源文件正文仅存首段，随后为省略号）**

In 1957 a doctor in Singapore noticed that hospitals were treating an unusual number of influenza-like cases. Influenza is sometimes called "flu" or "bad cold". He took samples from the throats of patients and in his hospital was able to find the virus of this influenza.

（以下为与第 37–40 题题干完全对应的通行原文，源 .doc 用 “…” 截断，仅供完成本组题，不充当未收录的第 41–54 题。）

There are three main types of the influenza virus. The most important of these are types A and B, each of them having several subgroups. With the instruments at the hospital the doctor recognized that the outbreak was due to a virus of group A, but he did not know the subgroup. He reported the outbreak to the World Health Organization in Geneva. W.H.O. published the important news alongside reports of a similar outbreak in Hong Kong, where about 15–20% of the population had become ill.

As soon as the London doctors received the package of throat samples, they began the standard tests. They found that by reproducing itself at very high speed, the virus had multiplied more than a million times within two days. Scientists, like the general public, called it simply "Asian" flu. The type of influenza discussed in this story could not be cured by any known drug.

The influenza outbreak in this story began in _______.

A. Hong Kong　　B. London　　C. Singapore　　D. India
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**C（据源文件首段）

首句：In 1957 a doctor in **Singapore** noticed…。Hong Kong 是随后报道的类似暴发，London 是收样检测地。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：1957 Asian flu 起点"}
新加坡医生发现流感样病例并分离病毒；香港是随后类似暴发，伦敦是收样检测。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
本故事中流感暴发起于 **Singapore**。
:::

### 第 37 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
（Passage I 见第 36 题题目卡）

Not counting the subgroups, there are _______.

A. one type of influenza　　B. two types of influenza　　C. three types of influenza　　D. four types of influenza
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**C（据通行原文推断）

There are **three main types** of the influenza virus. 最重要的是 A 与 B，各有亚型；题干 Not counting the subgroups 仍是三种主型。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：流感分型"}
三主型（A/B/C 等）；A、B 最重要，各有 subgroup。Not counting subgroups 仍是 three types。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
流感主型 **three**（不含亚型计数）。
:::

### 第 38 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
（Passage I 见第 36 题题目卡）

The doctor in Singapore performed a valuable service by _______.

A. finding the subgroup of the virus
B. developing a cure
C. keeping his patients apart from others
D. reporting the outbreak of influenza to Geneva
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**D（据通行原文推断）

他识别为 A 型但 **不知道 subgroup**（A 排除）；未研发治愈方法。有价值的服务是向日内瓦 WHO **报告暴发**，使全球预警。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：WHO 预警"}
不知 subgroup，但向 Geneva WHO 报告使全球预警，这是 valuable service。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
新加坡医生的贡献：向 **Geneva / WHO** 报告。
:::

该病毒 **reproduce with great speed**。

How can one reduce these risk factors?

### 第 56 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
laryngo-

A. stomach　　B. throat　　C. mouth　　D. kidney
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**B（据词根推断）

laryngo- = larynx / throat。stomach = gastro-，mouth = stomato-，kidney = nephro-/ren-。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：颈前管道"}
| 词素 | 意义 |
|------|------|
| laryngo- | 喉 |
| pharyngo- | 咽 |
| tracheo- | 气管 |
| stomato- | 口 |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
laryngo- = **throat**。
:::

### 第 65 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
between

A. endo-　　B. retro-　　C. meso-　　D. dia-
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**C（据词根推断）

meso- = middle / between（如 mesencephalon 中脑）。endo- 内，retro- 后，dia- 穿过/横过。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：位置前缀"}
| 前缀 | 意义 |
|------|------|
| endo- | 内 |
| ecto-/extra- | 外 |
| meso- | 中、之间 |
| dia- | 穿过 |
| retro- | 后、逆 |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
between → **meso-**（不是 dia-）。
:::

### 第 68 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
The second major finding is that of significant LDL-C and total cholesterol reductions in the intervention group at 3 years along with nonsignificant changes in HDL-C values and triglyceride levels.（3 分）
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**第二项主要发现是：干预组在 3 年时 LDL-C 和总胆固醇显著下降，同时 HDL-C 值与甘油三酯水平变化不显著。

据课文推断（DISC / Lesson 4）。along with 连接「不显著变化」。

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：血脂缩写"}
| 缩写 | 全称 |
|------|------|
| LDL-C | low-density lipoprotein cholesterol |
| HDL-C | high-density lipoprotein cholesterol |
| TC | total cholesterol |
| TG | triglyceride |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
干预组 3 年：**LDL-C/TC 显著降**；HDL-C/TG 不显著。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
