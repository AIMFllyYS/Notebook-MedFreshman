## 一、Part I Terminology · Section A（10%；源仅存第 1 题）

Directions: Choose the correct root, prefix or suffix for each given word.

### 第 1 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
false

A. polio-　　B. pseudo-　　C. angio-　　D. para-
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**B（据词根推断）

pseudo- = false（假）。polio- 灰/脊髓灰质，angio- 血管，para- 旁、异常。例：pseudocyst 假囊肿，pseudopodia 伪足。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：假/旁/管"}
| 词素 | 意义 | 例词 |
|------|------|------|
| pseudo- | 假 | pseudotumor |
| para- | 旁、异常 | parathyroid, paralysis |
| angio- | 血管 | angiography |
| polio- | 灰（质） | poliomyelitis |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
false → **pseudo-**。
:::

## 二、Part I Terminology · Section B（10%；源仅存第 11 题）

Directions: Choose the correct meaning of the underlined part.

### 第 11 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
paracenthesis

A. fixation　　B. stoppage　　C. puncture　　D. suture
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**C（据词根推断）

源拼写 paracenthesis，规范作 **paracentesis**。-centesis = puncture（穿刺）。fixation = -pexy，stoppage = -stasis，suture = -rrhaphy。para- 此处为「旁/经」：腹腔穿刺 paracentesis。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：穿刺与缝合"}
| 后缀 | 意义 | 例词 |
|------|------|------|
| -centesis | 穿刺 | amniocentesis, thoracentesis |
| -pexy | 固定 | nephropexy |
| -rrhaphy | 缝合 | herniorrhaphy |
| -stasis | 停滞 | hemostasis |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
paracentesis 的 -centesis = **puncture**。
:::

## 三、Part II Vocabulary · Section A（10%；源仅存第 21 题）

Directions: Choose one word or phrase that best completes the sentence.

### 第 21 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
Computed _________ plays an important role in diagnosis of acute intestinal obstruction and planning of surgical treatment.

A. spirometry　　B. tomography　　C. meteorology　　D. numeracy
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**B（据词根/课文推断）

Computed tomography = CT（计算机体层摄影）。spirometry 肺量计，meteorology 气象，numeracy 计算能力。tom(o)- = cut / section，-graphy = recording。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：-graphy 检查"}
| 词 | 意义 |
|----|------|
| tomography | 体层成像 |
| radiography | X 线摄片 |
| spirometry | 肺通气测量 |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
Computed **tomography** = CT，用于急腹症/肠梗阻。
:::

## 四、Part II Vocabulary · Section B（10%；源仅存第 31 题）

Directions: Choose the word or phrase which would best keep the meaning of the underlined part.

### 第 31 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
Many saw these **disparities** as a call for social reform or revolution.

A. issues　　B. differences　　C. biases　　D. conflicts
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**B（据词义/课文推断）

disparity = difference / inequality（差异、不平等）。social medicine / 健康公平课文常用 health disparities。issues 议题，biases 偏倚，conflicts 冲突。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：disparity"}
disparity = difference / inequality。health disparities 健康不平等。bias 是偏倚，conflict 是冲突。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
disparities = **differences**（差距）。
:::

## 五、Part III Reading Comprehension（20%；四篇 41–60。源缺全部正文，仅存三道题干）

Directions: There are 4 passages. Blacken the corresponding letter. 下列三题保留源卷题干；因无原文，解析标明「源缺原文，据题干与公开通行主题推断」，不编造阅读篇章。

### 第 41 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
**Passage 1**（源文件正文为省略号）

The biggest advantage of Medopad lies in __________.

A. improving access to the Internet
B. reducing the medical cost at a hospital
C. effective use of information on patients
D. quick exchange of data between hospitals
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**C（源缺原文，据题干/公开 Medopad 报道推断）

Medopad 是床旁平板/移动病历类应用，核心卖点是把患者信息有效调到医师手边，而不是「上网」或「院际传数据」本身。B 降费通常不是最大优点表述。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：Medopad"}
床旁移动病历：把患者信息有效送到医师手边。不是「上网」或「院际传数据」本身。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
Medopad 最大优势：对 **患者信息的有效使用**。
:::

### 第 56 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
**Passage 4**（源文件正文为省略号）

From the first paragraph, we learn that __________.

A. scientists had understood what happened to normal cells that made them behave strangely
B. when a cell begins to copy itself without stopping, it becomes cancerous
C. normal cells do not copy themselves
D. the DNA inside a nucleus divides regularly
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**B（源缺原文，据题干推断）

癌症科普首段标准信息：细胞开始不停复制即癌变。A 时态 had understood 通常与「曾不明白」相反；C 否定正常细胞也会复制，为假；D 未点出癌变机制。
:::

:::callout{kind="pitfall" label="易错警示"}
正常细胞**会**复制；癌细胞是**失控、不停**复制。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：癌变一句"}
正常细胞会复制；开始 **不停** 复制即癌变。科学家起初并不明白正常细胞如何变「奇怪」。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
细胞开始 **copy itself without stopping** → cancerous。
:::

## 六、IV. Translation（20%；源仅存第 61、64 题）

Directions: Translate the following English sentences into Chinese and Chinese sentences into English.

### 第 61 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
This code of ethics, which provided a gold stand for professional physicians, dictated the moral authority and independence of physicians in service to others and their responsibility towards the sick, as well as the physician's individual honour.（3 分）
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**这一伦理准则为职业医师提供了金标准，规定了医师服务于他人时的道德权威与独立性、对患者的责任，以及医师个人的荣誉。

据 Unit 1 Text A（Hippocratic Oath / Percival 1794 → AMA 1846）推断。源 gold stand = gold standard。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：医学伦理金标准"}
| 要点 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 伦理准则 | code of ethics |
| 金标准 | gold standard |
| 道德权威与独立 | moral authority and independence |
| 对患者的责任 | responsibility towards the sick |
| 个人荣誉 | individual honour |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
Percival/AMA code = 医师的权威、独立、责任与 **honour**。
:::

### 第 64 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
不出所料，在所有这些受直接影响的人群中，创伤后应激障碍的广泛程度和记录到的情绪低落情况高于普通人群；研究还描述了慢性 PTSD 及其延迟发作的情况。（4 分）
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**Not surprisingly, among all these directly affected groups, the prevalence of post-traumatic stress disorder and documented depression was higher than in the general population; studies have also described chronic PTSD and its delayed onset.

据 Unit 8 Text A（9/11 身心效应）推断。OLE 中文已清洗。PTSD = post-traumatic stress disorder；delayed onset 延迟发作。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：9/11 精神结局"}
| 中文 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 创伤后应激障碍 | post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) |
| 情绪低落/抑郁 | depression |
| 直接暴露人群 | directly affected groups |
| 延迟发作 | delayed onset |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
直接暴露人群：PTSD/抑郁 **高于** 普通人群；可慢性、可延迟发作。
:::
