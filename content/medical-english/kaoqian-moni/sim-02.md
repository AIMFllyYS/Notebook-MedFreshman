## 一、I. Prefix / suffix（每题 1 分，共 8 分）

Choose the correct meaning for each prefix or suffix.

### 第 1 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
masto–

A. breast　　B. womb　　C. menses　　D. ovary
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**A（源文件标注）

masto- / mamm(o)- = breast。womb = hystero-/metro-，menses = meno-，ovary = oophoro-/ovario-。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：乳房与生殖词根"}
| 词素 | 意义 |
|------|------|
| masto-, mammo- | 乳房 |
| hystero-, metro- | 子宫 |
| meno- | 月经 |
| oophoro- | 卵巢 |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
masto- = **breast**。
:::

## 二、II. Filling in the blanks（每题 1 分，共 8 分）

源样题把答案写进句中；对照历年真题空白版，填空位置如下。拼写以规范英文为准（源卷有 menifestations / Structral 等笔误）。

### 第 9 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
We will use the term ________ to refer to the data that can be gathered about an individual patient, namely, symptoms, signs, and laboratory abnormalities.
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**manifestations

源卷写作 menifestations（漏 a）。课文定义：个体患者可收集的资料 = 症状 + 体征 + 化验异常，统称 manifestations。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：三组资料"}
manifestations = symptoms（主观）+ signs（客观）+ laboratory abnormalities（化验/影像/活检）。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
symptoms + signs + lab abnormalities = **manifestations**。
:::

### 第 19 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
Which of the following statements are not true?

A. Structural diseases sometimes fall into more than one category.
B. Structural diseases are not always to classify.
C. Structural diseases usually fall into more of the three broad categories.
D. Structural diseases are called lesions that may be biochemical or morphologic.
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**C（据课文推断）

结构性疾病通常归入三大类之一，而不是「more of the three」。A、D 符合课文；B 英语别扭但意思接近「并非总是容易分类」。C 语义错误，选 not true。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：分类交叉"}
结构性疾病有时可归入不止一类；通常仍落入三大桶之一，不是「more of the three」。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
结构性疾病可跨类，但不是「usually fall into more of the three」→ 选 **C**（not true）。
:::

## 四、IV. Reading comprehension（共 30 分）

### 第 25 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
HIV is spread or transmitted from one person to another by bodily fluids such as blood, semen and vaginal fluid. This happens in two main ways:

1. by sexual intercourse with an infected person.

The virus can be transmitted when an infected individual has sexual intercourse with another person. The larger the number of sexual partners an individual has, the more likely he or she is to have a partner who has HIV infection, thereby increasing the chances of becoming infected.

The virus is passed on more easily by anal intercourse than by vaginal intercourse. The infection can be transmitted not only from man to man and man to woman, but also from woman to man.

Although transmission is more likely to occur from males to females than from females to males, the risk of female-to-male transmission is significant.

2. by infected blood and blood products.

Although transmission of HIV has occurred through transfusions of infected blood and blood products in the past, donations to the Red Cross Blood Transfusion Service are now routinely tested for HIV antibodies. This screening and other measures introduced by the Red Cross Blood Transfusion Service make the chances of anyone being infected through this route very, very slim.

- Transmission through blood from a person infected with virus occurs when infected needles and/or syringes are shared by people using illicit drugs.
- There is absolutely no risk of blood donors acquiring HIV infection by giving blood to the Red Cross Transfusion Service.

3. from mother to baby during pregnancy.

The virus is very likely to be passed from an infected mother to her child during pregnancy. There is also a possibility of the virus being transmitted through breast milk. Women who intend to become pregnant should consider having an HIV antibody test.

We do know how the virus is NOT spread:

It is NOT spread through the air. You cannot become infected by breathing the air breathed out by an infected person or the air from an air conditioner.

It is NOT spread through people swallowing infected liquids. HIV has been found in saliva, but infection does NOT take place through using cups, glasses, plates, other crockery or cutlery which has been used by people with AIDS. There is no evidence that HIV can be acquired by intimate kissing.

It is NOT spread by mosquitoes. Studies in the USA have NOT linked the spread of HIV with mosquitoes.

HIV cannot be spread.

A. by semen　　B. by food　　C. by vaginal fluid　　D. by breast milk
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】**B

:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：spare no one"}
spare no one = 不放过任何人；irrespective of age or sex = 不论年龄性别。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
cancer **spares no one**, irrespective of age or sex.
:::
