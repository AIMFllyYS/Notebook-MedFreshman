## 二、核心知识点

### 2.1 Paraphrasing（释义改写）

**定义**：Paraphrasing refers to restating others' written or spoken content with different words, with the core requirement of fully retaining the original semantic meaning without distortion.

**三大价值**：
1. 帮助更好地理解原文（better original text comprehension）
2. 支持高效笔记和总结（efficient note-taking and summary）
3. 避免抄袭，使论文查重率保持在合格阈值内（avoid plagiarism）

**四大常用技巧**：

| 技巧 | 说明 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 同义词替换 | 用同义词替换原词和短语 | important → crucial / significant |
| 词性转换 | 名词↔动词、形容词↔副词等 | analysis (n.) → analyze (v.) |
| 主被动转换 | 主动语态↔被动语态 | The doctor diagnosed → It was diagnosed |
| 句型改写 | 调整句子结构和模式 | 简单句↔复合句 |

**标准操作步骤**：
1. First read and fully understand the original text（通读理解）
2. Identify core meaningful keywords and expressions（识别核心词）
3. Replace eligible content and rearrange sentences（替换+重组）
4. Compare the rewritten version with the original to confirm consistent meaning（对照确认）

**特殊规则**：
- **专业术语不可替换**：Fixed proper nouns including professional terms such as photosynthesis and drug names cannot be replaced. Other content should be fully paraphrased to reduce duplicate checking rates.
- **谚语/习语**：Must first fully grasp the core implied meaning of the idiom, then restate the connotation in plain language instead of translating the literal content directly.

**场景注意**：
- **语境匹配**：Words with abstract contextual meanings cannot be replaced with literal corresponding synonyms; replacement must match the specific semantic scenario.
- **正式性**：Replacing Latin/Greek derived formal professional terms with common English will reduce the formality of the original academic text — not recommended for formal paper writing.
- **医学英语特征**：Medical academic texts often use simple sentences with rich modifiers, or compound sentences with simple main clauses and complex subordinate clauses.

### 2.2 AI医学诊断文本结构分析

**文本三段式结构**：

| 部分 | 段落 | 核心内容 |
|---|---|---|
| Section 1 | Para 1-3 | 人类医学诊断现存问题 + 误诊两大核心原因 |
| Section 2 | Para 4-9 | AI应用于医学诊断的核心优势（准确性+时效性提升） |
| Section 3 | Para 10-14 | AI诊断现存挑战 + 未来发展方向与优化路径 |

**Illness Script（疾病脚本）**：
- 医生通常依靠记忆疾病脚本来识别疾病
- 这种方法容易出错的两大原因：
  1. **忽略患者关键特异因素**（some patients' critical specific factors are neglected）
  2. **非典型疾病表现不符合标准模式**（atypical disease presentations do not conform to standard patterns）

**AI的核心优势**：
- 海量数据支撑（supported by massive data）
- 提高疾病识别的准确性和时效性（improve accuracy and timeliness）
- 能发现新的、创造性的模式（spot new, creative patterns）
- 例如：快速识别缺血性中风（ischemic stroke）

**AI面临的挑战**：

| 挑战 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| 高开发成本 | High development cost |
| 潜在错误信息 | Potential misinformation output |
| 患者信任度低 | Low patient trust in AI diagnosis without sufficient valid data |
| 伦理与就业 | Over-reliance may lead to large-scale unemployment; Europe adopts conservative R&D strategies for ethical and employment protection |

**核心发展原则**：AI technology in the medical field should **complement rather than replace** human professional expertise, and sufficient capital investment is required for stable technology iteration.

**行业趋势**：AI和机器人技术的快速发展使电气工程重新成为热门专业，因为电力供应是所有智能运行的核心基础。

### 2.3 学术写作段落展开方法

**8种常用段落展开方法**：

| 方法 | 英文 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 时间顺序 | time order / time sequence | 按时间先后组织内容 |
| 过程描述 | process | 描述步骤和流程 |
| 空间描述 | space description | 按空间位置组织 |
| 举例+概括 | example and generalization | 先举例后归纳，或先概括后举例 |
| 比较与对比 | comparison and contrast | 比较异同 |
| 因果关系 | cause and effect | 分析原因与结果 |
| 分类与划分 | classification and division | 将整体分类或划分 |
| 定义法 | definition | 先定义概念再展开 |

**应用规则**：Most formal academic paragraphs do not use a single development method; multiple methods are often combined to present content and support core arguments.

**修辞手法**：Even professional medical academic texts use rhetorical devices such as **metaphor** and **irony** in titles and key sentences to enhance readability and attract readers.

**段落分析实例**：

## 三、例题/强调点

1. **Paraphrasing的核心要求**：fully retaining the original semantic meaning without distortion — 保留原意是第一原则。
2. **专业术语不可替换**：photosynthesis、drug names等fixed proper nouns必须保留，替换会导致术语错误。
3. **谚语释义≠字面翻译**：必须先理解隐含意义（core implied meaning），再用平实语言重述。
4. **误诊两大原因**：①忽略患者关键特异因素 ②非典型表现不符合标准模式 — 这两点正好对应AI能解决的优势。
5. **AI的定位**：complement rather than replace — 补充而非替代人类专业知识。
6. **段落展开方法**：正式学术段落通常多方法组合，不只用单一方法。
7. **医学文本也用修辞**：metaphor（如"history is a march"）和irony用于标题和关键句增强可读性。
8. **Para 2长句特征**：simple main clause + complex subordinate clause — 先找主句抓核心意思。
9. **小组展示四维度**：main idea / writing style / rhetorical devices / paraphrasing & translation — 缺一不可。

## 五、主动回忆题

**Q1：What is paraphrasing and what are its four common techniques?**

A: Paraphrasing refers to restating others' written or spoken content with different words while fully retaining the original semantic meaning without distortion. The four common techniques are: (1) synonym replacement — replacing words with synonyms; (2) part of speech conversion — changing nouns to verbs, adjectives to adverbs, etc.; (3) active-passive voice switching; (4) sentence pattern rewriting — adjusting sentence structures. The standard workflow is: read and understand → identify core keywords → apply techniques → compare with original to confirm consistent meaning.

**Q2：Why can't professional terms be replaced in paraphrasing? How should proverbs/idioms be paraphrased?**

A: Fixed proper nouns including professional terms (such as photosynthesis and drug names) cannot be replaced because they have specific, standardized meanings that synonyms cannot accurately convey. Replacing them would introduce terminology errors. For proverbs and idioms, one must first fully grasp the core implied meaning of the idiom, then restate the connotation in plain language — rather than translating the literal content directly, which would lose the figurative meaning.

**Q3：What is the three-part structure of the AI medical diagnosis text? What are the two core causes of human misdiagnosis?**

A: The text is divided into three sections: Section 1 (Paragraphs 1-3) focuses on existing problems of human medical diagnosis and the two core causes of frequent misdiagnosis; Section 2 (Paragraphs 4-9) elaborates the core advantages of AI in medical diagnosis, covering accuracy and timeliness improvement; Section 3 (Paragraphs 10-14) lists existing challenges of AI medical diagnosis and future development directions. The two core causes of human misdiagnosis are: (1) some patients' critical specific factors are neglected, and (2) atypical disease presentations do not conform to standard patterns. Doctors rely on memorizing "illness scripts" to identify diseases, which leads to these two types of errors.

**Q4：What are the eight common paragraph development methods in academic writing? Why are multiple methods usually combined?**

A: The eight common paragraph development methods are: time order, process description, space description, example and generalization, comparison and contrast, cause and effect, classification and division, and definition. Most formal academic paragraphs do not use a single development method because complex arguments require multiple organizational approaches to present content comprehensively and support core arguments effectively. Even professional medical academic texts use rhetorical devices such as metaphor and irony in titles and key sentences to enhance readability.
