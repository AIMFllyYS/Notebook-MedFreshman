# Lesson 8 · Short-term and medium-term health effects of 9/11

本课 Text A 是 *The Lancet* 2011 年综述：Sharon E. Perlman 等 *Short-term and medium-term health effects of 9/11*。它不是新闻特写，而是把 2001 年 9 月 11 日世界贸易中心（WTC）袭击后的**精神健康**与**呼吸健康**证据收成短、中期账本，并指出还需要继续研究的空白。数字来自教材译文与英文连续段落：44% 的美国成人在 3–5 天内感到实质压力；纽约学生样本中 29% 有至少一种焦虑或抑郁障碍；10378 名消防员第一年每日咳嗽 54%（灾前 4%）；灾后 3 个月 FEV1 平均下降 372 mL。

:::insight{label="先分人群，再谈症状"}
综述把研究对象切成三组：直接暴露（救援人员、志愿者、下曼哈顿居民与上班族、师生、路人）；纽约市内间接暴露；全美及国际间接暴露。PTSD 筛查高分只能叫 **probable PTSD**，因为清单不是诊断工具。把电视观众的 1% 风险和尘云里的消防员写成同一句话，是这课最常见的错账。
:::

:::definition{label="三组暴露人群"}
Studies of the health effects of 9/11 have generally focused on three distinct populations: (1) directly exposed groups — rescue and recovery workers, volunteers, lower Manhattan residents, people who worked in the area, school staff and students, and passers-by on 9/11; (2) those indirectly exposed in New York City — not near the WTC site on 9/11 or during the weeks afterwards, and who did not lose a loved one or a job as a result of the attacks; (3) those indirectly exposed throughout the USA and internationally.
:::

## 二、核心词汇

:::derivation{label="exposure /ɪkˈspəʊʒə/ n."}
**书本义：** [C, U] 暴露；接触（有害因子、创伤事件）

**词形：** expose（v.）→ exposed（a.）→ exposure；dose of exposure 暴露剂量。

**常用搭配：**
- dust-cloud exposure 尘云暴露
- directly / indirectly exposed groups 直接/间接暴露组
- prolonged, intense exposure 长时间、高强度暴露

**例句：**
1. Individuals with more intense, prolonged WTC exposures generally experienced more severe and persistent respiratory illness.（暴露更强、更久的人，呼吸系统疾病通常更重、更持久。）
2. Exposure predicted initial illness, but individual host factors affected the long-term course.（暴露预示初始疾病，但个体宿主因素影响长期病程。）

✍️ In this review exposure is a measured contact with dust, death and media, not a synonym for "experience".（本综述里 exposure 是与尘、死亡和媒体的可测量接触，不是「经历」的同义词。）
:::

:::derivation{label="permeate /ˈpɜːmieɪt/ vt. vi."}
**书本义：** 弥漫；渗透；充满

**词形：** permeation（n.）；pervasive 是形容词近义。

**常用搭配：**
- toxic substances that permeated the clean-up site 弥漫于清理现场的有毒物质
- be permeated with dust 充满灰尘

**例句：**
1. Routine monitors were destroyed, which made it difficult to quantify exposure to the massive dust cloud and to toxic substances that permeated residences and workplaces.（常规监测设备被毁，难以量化巨大尘云以及弥漫于住宅与工作场所的有毒物质暴露。）
2. Alkaline dust permeated lower Manhattan in the days after the collapse.（倒塌后的几天里，碱性粉尘弥漫下曼哈顿。）

✍️ Permeate takes a space as object: dust permeates a site; people are exposed to dust.（permeate 的宾语是空间：尘弥漫现场；人被暴露于尘。）
:::

:::derivation{label="alkaline /ˈælkəlaɪn/ a."}
**书本义：** 碱性的

**词形：** alkali（n. 碱）；alkalinity（n. 碱度）。课文：The dust was highly alkaline (pH up to ~11 in related reports) and had high concentrations of particulate matter.

**常用搭配：**
- highly alkaline dust 强碱性粉尘
- alkaline pH 碱性 pH

**例句：**
1. The dust was highly alkaline and capable of causing respiratory irritation and damage.（粉尘强碱，足以刺激并损伤呼吸道。）
2. Powdered concrete and gypsum help explain why the dust was caustic as well as alkaline.（粉末状混凝土和石膏有助于解释粉尘为何既苛性又呈碱性。）

✍️ Alkaline dust irritates upper airways first; do not jump to "all particles cause cancer" from pH alone.（碱性粉尘先刺激上气道；不要单凭 pH 跳到「所有颗粒致癌」。）
:::

:::derivation{label="psychopathology /ˌsaɪkəʊpəˈθɒlədʒi/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 精神病理学；（本文）精神病理状态/障碍

**词形：** patho- 病 + -logy；pre-event psychopathology 事件前已有的精神病理。

**常用搭配：**
- stress reactions and psychopathology nationwide 全国范围的应激反应与精神病理
- pre-event psychopathology 事件前精神病理（PTSD 风险因素）

**例句：**
1. Reports documented stress reactions and psychopathology nationwide, and psychopathology in the New York metropolitan area.（报道记录了全国的应激与精神病理，以及纽约大都市区的精神病理。）
2. Pre-event psychopathology, female sex, recent immigration and more hours of event-related media were risk factors up to four years later.（事件前精神病理、女性、新近移民、观看事件相关媒体时间更长，是四年内的风险因素。）

✍️ Psychopathology here is a research category, not an insult. Screening is not diagnosis.（这里的 psychopathology 是研究类别，不是骂人。筛查不是诊断。）
:::

:::derivation{label="prevalence /ˈprevələns/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 患病率；流行率（某一时点有病的比例）

**词形：** prevalent（a.）；与 incidence（新发病）对举。

**常用搭配：**
- prevalence of PTSD and depression PTSD 与抑郁的患病率
- high prevalence in directly exposed groups 直接暴露组中的高患病率

**例句：**
1. One to two months after 9/11, the prevalence of PTSD among 988 residents of Manhattan south of 110th Street was 7.5%, and that for depression was about 9%.（9/11 后 1–2 个月，110 街以南 988 名曼哈顿居民中 PTSD 患病率 7.5%，抑郁约 9%。）
2. Prevalence of both PTSD and depression had decreased by six months after the attacks.（袭击后 6 个月，PTSD 与抑郁的患病率均已下降。）

✍️ Prevalence is "how many have it now"; incidence is "how many get it newly". Firefighters' 54% is a period rate of daily cough, reported against a pre-9/11 baseline.（prevalence 是现患，incidence 是新发。消防员 54% 是对照灾前基线的阶段率。）
:::

:::callout{kind="pitfall" label="prevalent 不是 epidemic"}
Unit 6 课件已辨：prevalent = 普遍存在；epidemic = 短时间突然大量发生。本课 prevalence of PTSD 是现患比例。不要写成 an epidemic of 7.5%。
:::

:::derivation{label="sequela /sɪˈkwiːlə/ n."}
**书本义：** [C] 后遗症（复数为 sequelae）

**词形：** 医学正式用语；mental health sequelae 精神健康后遗症。

**常用搭配：**
- long-term sequelae 长期后遗症
- prevent sequelae 预防后遗症

**例句：**
1. Screening and treatment of high-risk populations may prevent long-term mental health sequelae.（对高危人群筛查和治疗，可能预防长期精神健康后遗症。）
2. Respiratory sequelae persisted in thousands even after most people improved.（即使大多数人有所改善，仍有数千人残留呼吸后遗症。）

✍️ Sequela is the after-disease, not the original blast injury.（sequela 是病后账，不是爆炸当场的伤。）
:::

:::derivation{label="spirometry /spaɪˈrɒmətri/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 肺活量测定；肺量计检查

**词形：** spir/o 呼吸 + -metry 测量；spirometer 肺量计。

**常用搭配：**
- spirometry done a median of 3 months after 9/11 9/11 后中位 3 个月的肺量检查
- decline in FEV1 on spirometry 肺量计上 FEV1 下降

**例句：**
1. Spirometry in 12 079 New York City Fire Department responders showed an average decrease of 372 mL in FEV1.（对 12079 名纽约消防部门应急人员的肺量检查显示 FEV1 平均下降 372 mL。）
2. After the first-year drop, spirometry showed a stabilisation of function, with most having normal, albeit diminished, FEV1.（第一年大幅下降后，肺量检查显示功能趋稳，多数人 FEV1 仍正常但低于灾前。）

✍️ Spirometry is the objective counterpart of "I have a cough"; the review insists on both.（肺量计是「我咳嗽」的客观对照；综述坚持两本账都要。）
:::

:::derivation{label="albeit /ɔːlˈbiːɪt/ conj."}
**书本义：** 尽管；虽然（后接形容词或短语，不接完整从句）

**词形：** 正式书面语；= although。

**常用搭配：**
- normal, albeit diminished FEV1 正常但有所降低的 FEV1
- useful, albeit incomplete, data 有用但不完整的数据

**例句：**
1. Most had normal, albeit diminished, FEV1 values compared with pre-disaster levels.（与灾前相比，多数人 FEV1 仍正常，尽管有所降低。）
2. The mental health of children has been studied, albeit less thoroughly than that of adults.（儿童精神健康已被研究，尽管不如成人充分。）

✍️ Albeit diminished still means inside the normal range for many firefighters; it does not mean "recovered to baseline".（albeit diminished 对许多消防员仍在正常范围内；不等于回到基线。）
:::

:::derivation{label="outreach /ˈaʊtriːtʃ/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 外展服务；主动接触高危人群

**词形：** 名词为主；immediate outreach 立即外展。

**常用搭配：**
- immediate outreach after disasters 灾后立即外展
- outreach, screening and treatment 外展、筛查与治疗

**例句：**
1. Interventions that might reduce short- and medium-term effects include immediate outreach.（可能减少短中期影响的干预包括立即外展。）
2. Outreach without respirators and shift rotation is only half a plan.（只有外展、没有呼吸器与轮班，只是半套方案。）

✍️ Outreach is going out to people, not waiting for them to find the clinic.（outreach 是走出去找人，不是等他们找到诊室。）
:::

## 三、课文精讲 · Text A

### Introduction · 看见什么、吸入什么

> The September 11, 2001 (9/11) terrorist attacks exposed many people to a wide range of horrific events: they saw planes crashing into buildings, people falling from buildings, and the collapse of the World Trade Center (WTC) towers; they were trapped in the dust cloud; and they witnessed injury and death. Many lost relatives, friends, and colleagues. Rescue and recovery workers found body remains and personal effects of the deceased. Many people experienced stress, fear, and uncertainty. In the weeks after 9/11, public areas in New York City were covered with pictures of missing people, many of whom had died in the attacks. Furthermore, thousands of people were temporarily or permanently unable to return to their homes, workplaces, or schools.

> Additionally, the collapse of the WTC towers exposed many to substances that have been shown to adversely affect health. Routine environmental monitoring equipment in lower Manhattan was damaged and destroyed in the aftermath of the attacks, which made it difficult to quantify exposure to the massive dust cloud generated by the collapsing towers and potentially to toxic substances that permeated the clean-up site, residences, and workplaces in the area. Sampling for asbestos, organochlorine compounds, volatile organic compounds, and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons began between September 14 and September 21, 2001. The dust and debris contained powdered concrete, gypsum, glass fibres, chrysotile asbestos, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons, steel, and several metals such as lead, aluminum, antimony, chromium, molybdenum, and barium. The dust was highly alkaline and had high concentrations of particulate matter capable of causing respiratory irritation and damage. The destroyed buildings also contained electrical cable coated in polyvinyl chloride, some of which was incinerated during and after the collapse of the towers, which caused the release of dioxins and other chlorinated organic compounds into the atmosphere.

**中文翻译：** 2001 年 9 月 11 日的恐怖袭击使许多人暴露于一系列可怕事件：他们看见飞机撞进建筑物、人们从楼坠下、世贸双塔倒塌；他们困在尘云中；他们目睹受伤与死亡。许多人失去亲人、朋友和同事。救援人员发现人体遗骸和死者遗物。许多人经历压力、恐惧和不确定。9/11 之后数周，纽约公共场所贴满失踪者照片，其中许多人已在袭击中死亡。此外，成千上万的人暂时或永久无法返回家园、工作场所或学校。

同时，双塔倒塌使许多人暴露于已被证明有害健康的物质。曼哈顿下城的常规环境监测设备在袭击余波中被损坏、摧毁，因而难以量化倒塌产生的巨大尘云，以及弥漫于清理现场、住宅和该地区工作场所的潜在有毒物质。石棉、有机氯、挥发性有机化合物和多环芳烃的采样在 2001 年 9 月 14 日至 21 日之间开始。尘土与残骸含有粉末状混凝土、石膏、玻璃纤维、温石棉、多环芳烃、钢，以及铅、铝、锑、铬、钼、钡等金属。粉尘强碱，并含高浓度足以刺激、损伤呼吸道的颗粒物。被毁建筑中还有聚氯乙烯包裹的电缆，其中一些在倒塌中或之后被焚烧，导致二噁英及其他氯化有机物释放到大气中。

**讲解：** 精神暴露（看见、失去、不确定）和化学暴露（碱性尘、石棉、二噁英）从第一页就并列。监测设备被毁，所以定量暴露困难——后文所有「到达时间」都是暴露的代理指标，不是实验室里的毫克数。Sampling 从 9 月 14 日才开始，空白的是最浓的那几天。

:::keypoint{label="尘的成分要能分类"}
- 碱性刺激：concrete、gypsum → 上气道咳嗽、咽痛、鼻窦炎
- 纤维与颗粒：glass fibres、particulate matter
- 已知毒物：chrysotile asbestos、PAHs、金属
- 燃烧产物：PVC 电缆 → dioxins、chlorinated organics
:::

> This review investigates the health effects of 9/11 terrorist attacks in the short and medium terms to establish what has been learned, and to identify areas in which further research is needed.

**中文翻译：** 本综述考察 9/11 恐怖袭击在短期和中期的健康影响，以明确已知什么，并指出仍需进一步研究的领域。

**讲解：** short and medium terms 是标题里的时间窗，不是「已经全部结束」。

### Mental health · 全国应激与纽约病理

> More than 150 studies have documented the mental health effects of 9/11. These reports can be divided into two groups: those that documented stress reactions and psychopathology nationwide, and those that focused on psychopathology in the New York City metropolitan area. Most WTC investigators used screening instruments to assess post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) such as the PTSD checklist. High scores on the checklist are often referred to as probable PTSD because these instruments are not diagnostic for the disorder. Other studies assessed post-traumatic stress symptoms, indicating that respondents endorsed symptoms of stress but did not meet PTSD criteria.

**中文翻译：** 超过 150 项研究记录了 9/11 对心理健康的影响。这些报告可分为两类：记录全国范围应激反应与精神病理的，以及聚焦纽约大都市区精神病理的。多数世贸中心研究者使用 PTSD 清单一类筛查工具。清单高分通常称为「可能的 PTSD」，因为这些工具并不能诊断该障碍。其他研究评估创伤后应激症状，表明受访者承认有压力症状，但未达到 PTSD 标准。

**讲解：** 150+ studies 是规模。probable PTSD ≠ PTSD。endorse symptoms 是问卷用语：勾选症状。

> Measurements taken 3–5 days after 9/11 suggested that 44% of the adult US population experienced substantial stress. Findings from subsequent national studies also showed that individuals across the country experienced fear and insecurity, and had increased rates of post-traumatic stress symptoms two months later. Research into the potential causes of these symptoms among people with no direct exposure, or to other disasters, could be beneficial in view of the broad reach of mass media compared with direct exposure after a disaster. Risk factors associated with post-traumatic stress symptoms up to four years after 9/11 included pre-event psychopathology, female sex, recent immigration to the USA, and increased hours of viewing event-related media coverage. Overall, individuals who lived outside the New York City metropolitan area were significantly less likely to have probable PTSD than were those inside the area two months after 9/11. A 2004–05 survey of nearly 35 000 US adults showed that those who had indirect experience of 9/11 (e.g., witnessed the attack on television) had the lowest risk of PTSD (1%) of 32 traumatic events listed.

**中文翻译：** 9/11 后 3–5 天的测量表明，44% 的美国成年人口经历了实质压力。随后的全国研究也显示，全国各地的人感到恐惧与不安全，并在两个月后出现更高的创伤后应激症状率。鉴于大众媒体的覆盖远广于灾后的直接暴露，研究无直接暴露者出现这些症状的潜在原因是有益的。与 9/11 后四年内创伤后应激症状相关的风险因素包括：事件前精神病理、女性、新近移民美国、观看事件相关媒体时间增加。总的来说，住在纽约大都市区以外的人在两个月后发生可能 PTSD 的可能性显著低于区内的人。2004–05 年近 3.5 万名美国成人调查显示，间接经历 9/11（例如在电视上目睹袭击）者，在列出的 32 种创伤事件中 PTSD 风险最低（1%）。

**讲解：** 44% 是全国应激，不是全国 PTSD。电视间接暴露 1% 是 32 种创伤里最低——用来打「看了电视就会得 PTSD」的夸张。媒体小时数仍是风险因素，两句话不矛盾：看很多是风险，但相对其他创伤，这一条的绝对风险仍低。

> Studies done in the New York City metropolitan area focused on psychopathology, particularly PTSD and depression. Several studies based on a series of cross-sectional surveys and subsequent longitudinal work led by New York Academy of Medicine researchers showed that 1–2 months after 9/11, the prevalence of PTSD in 988 residents of Manhattan south of 110th Street was 7.5%, and that for depression was about 9%. Peri-event panic attacks, loss of possessions, and job loss were risk factors for the development of PTSD, and low social support was a risk factor for depression. Prevalence of both PTSD and depression had decreased by 6 months after the terrorist attacks. Other investigations have focused on rescue and recovery workers, lower Manhattan residents, students, workers, and passers-by on the day of the attack. The prevalence of PTSD and depression documented was, as expected, higher in all these directly exposed groups than in the general population, and studies describe chronic and delayed-onset PTSD.

**中文翻译：** 在纽约大都市区进行的研究聚焦精神病理，尤其是 PTSD 与抑郁。纽约医学科学院研究者主导的一系列横断面调查及后续纵向工作显示：9/11 后 1–2 个月，110 街以南 988 名曼哈顿居民中 PTSD 患病率 7.5%，抑郁约 9%。事件期间惊恐发作、财产损失和失业是 PTSD 的风险因素，低社会支持是抑郁的风险因素。袭击后 6 个月，两者患病率均下降。其他研究关注救援人员、下曼哈顿居民、学生、上班族和当天路人。这些直接暴露组的 PTSD 与抑郁患病率一如预期高于一般人群，研究也描述了慢性与迟发性 PTSD。

**讲解：** 7.5% / ~9% 是曼哈顿南段一般居民，不是消防员。peri-event panic、丢东西、丢工作 → PTSD；low social support → 抑郁。6 个月下降说明一部分是急性反应。chronic and delayed-onset 说明下降不是「全部好了」。

### Suicide · 未必上升

> Although some studies have shown no significant change in the risk of suicide at the population level in the month after 9/11 compared with previous years, others note a significant decrease in suicide both in the New York City area and internationally. Greater communal support and unity in the aftermath of the 9/11 attacks might have decreased the risk of suicide.

**中文翻译：** 虽然一些研究显示，与往年相比，9/11 后一个月人群水平的自杀风险无显著变化，另一些则注意到纽约地区以及国际上自杀显著减少。袭击之后更强的公共支持与团结，可能降低了自杀风险。

**讲解：** 不要把灾难自动写成「自杀上升」。课文给的机制是 communal support。

### Children

> The mental health effects of 9/11 have not been as well studied in children as in adults. In a sample of 8236 New York City students aged 9–21 years taken 6 months after 9/11, 29% of children had at least one of six anxiety or depressive disorders assessed. The most prevalent were probable agoraphobia (15%), separation anxiety (12%), and PTSD (11%). Higher rates were associated with greater exposure (a composite variable including proximity to the attack, injury, dust-cloud exposure, and death or injury of a family member), female sex, a younger age (9–10 years), and a history of previous trauma. The severity of children's reactions has been positively correlated with parental distress (parental post-traumatic stress and crying in front of the child) and with the number of graphic images seen on television. In New York City, children who had probable peri-event panic attacks were more likely to have probable PTSD 6–9 months later than were those who had not. A study of 116 children aged 5 years and younger in lower Manhattan showed that children with a history of other trauma were at greater risk of anxiety and depression symptoms, and sleep problems, 2–4 years after the attacks.

**中文翻译：** 9/11 对儿童心理健康的影响，研究得还不如成人充分。9/11 六个月后，8236 名 9–21 岁纽约学生样本中，29% 的儿童有所评估的六种焦虑或抑郁障碍中的至少一种。最常见的是可能的广场恐怖症（15%）、分离焦虑（12%）和 PTSD（11%）。更高比率与更大暴露（临近袭击点、受伤、尘云暴露、家人伤亡的复合变量）、女性、低龄（9–10 岁）以及既往创伤史有关。孩子反应的严重程度与父母悲痛（父母的创伤后应激、在子女面前哭泣）以及电视上看到的惨烈画面数量呈正相关。在纽约市，经历过事件相关惊恐发作的小孩，比没有过的小孩更可能在 6–9 个月后出现可能的 PTSD。下曼哈顿一项针对 116 名 5 岁及以下儿童的研究显示：有其他创伤史的儿童，更可能在袭击 2–4 年后出现焦虑、抑郁症状和睡眠问题。

**讲解：** 29% 是「至少一种」，不是人人 PTSD。儿童最常见的甚至不是 PTSD 而是 agoraphobia。parental distress 把家庭做成第二暴露源。5 岁以下样本只有 116 人，课文自己在说儿童研究不足。

### Respiratory health · 消防员队列

> Respiratory illnesses in people exposed to the WTC attack began on a large scale immediately after 9/11. Despite improvement in most individuals since 9/11, thousands continue to have long-term symptomatic and functional problems. Individuals with more intense, prolonged WTC exposures generally experienced more severe and persistent respiratory illness. Clinician-reported or self-reported diagnoses of upper-respiratory symptoms (such as nasal or sinus congestion, sore throat, and laryngitis) and, at lower prevalence, lower-respiratory symptoms (wheezing, tightness of the chest, dyspnoea, asthma and reactive airways dysfunction syndrome) were documented in all exposure groups. However, the overall incidence of respiratory illness is difficult to assess because most study populations contain self-selected subsets of exposed individuals.

> Firefighters provide a more complete cohort, because there is a documented roster of employees that undergo routine mandatory medical examinations approximately every 18 months. The longitudinal follow-up of this cohort allows the detection of immediate and longer-term patterns of changes in lung function within individuals.

**中文翻译：** 暴露于世贸袭击的人群在 9/11 后立即大规模出现呼吸系统疾病。尽管此后多数人有所改善，仍有数千人存在长期症状和功能问题。暴露更强、更久的人，呼吸系统疾病通常更重、更持久。所有暴露组都记录了临床或自我报告的上呼吸道症状（鼻或鼻窦充血、咽痛、喉炎），以及患病率较低的下呼吸道症状（喘息、胸闷、呼吸困难、哮喘和反应性气道功能障碍综合征）。然而总发病率难以评估，因为多数研究人群包含自我选择的暴露者子集。

消防员提供了更完整的队列：有登记在册的雇员，大约每 18 个月接受强制体检。该队列的纵向随访使得监测个体肺功能的即时与长期变化成为可能。

**讲解：** self-selected 是方法学警告：志愿者登记会高估发病率。消防员的价值是 roster + mandatory exams。upper vs lower respiratory 要能分开写。

> For many affected firefighters, signs of WTC cough appeared immediately after 9/11, with a shift to lower-respiratory problems over the next year. In the first year after 9/11, 54% of 10 378 firefighters had a daily cough (vs 4% in the year before 9/11), and it persisted in 17% in the second year. The rates of upper-respiratory symptoms far exceeded those from before 9/11: sore throats (62% vs 3%) and rhinosinusitis (45% vs 4%) were frequent. Sore-throat symptoms generally subsided for most, but reported rhinosinusitis remained at more than 40% by the fourth year. Lower-respiratory symptoms of dyspnoea (40% vs 3%) and wheeze (34% vs 1%) were increased in the year after 9/11 compared with before the attack. Dyspnoea and wheezing were reported more often in the second year and rates remained high through to the fourth year of follow-up. In firefighters, arrival time, months of working at the WTC site, presence of respiratory symptoms in the first year, current smoking, and older age on 9/11 were significant predictors of lower-respiratory symptoms four years after 9/11.

**中文翻译：** 对多数受影响的消防员来说，与世贸相关的咳嗽在 9/11 后立即出现，随后一年转向下呼吸道问题。9/11 后第一年，10378 名消防员中 54% 有每日咳嗽（灾前一年为 4%），第二年仍有 17% 持续。上呼吸道症状远超灾前：咽痛 62% 对 3%，鼻窦炎 45% 对 4%。咽痛对多数人逐渐消退，但报告的鼻窦炎到第四年仍高于 40%。下呼吸道的呼吸困难（40% 对 3%）和喘息（34% 对 1%）在此后一年升高；第二年报告更多，并高位维持到第四年。对消防员而言，到达时间、在 WTC 现场工作月数、第一年即有呼吸症状、当前吸烟、以及 9/11 当日年龄，是四年后下呼吸道症状的显著预测因素。

**讲解：** WTC cough 上→下的时间迁移是叙事主轴。54/62/45/40/34 这组对比数字是完形和细节题的矿。arrival time 是暴露代理。

> Pulmonary function data provide objective physiological evidence of abnormality and complement symptom reporting. Spirometry done a median of 3 months after 9/11 in 12 079 New York City Fire Department responders showed an average decrease of 372 mL in forced expiratory volume in 1 s (FEV1) from their last pre-9/11 measurement, which is equal to the loss expected in 12 years of normal ageing. A higher rate of decline was correlated with the degree of exposure, as estimated by date of arrival at the WTC site. After the substantial drop in the first year post-9/11, spirometry in firefighters and emergency medical service workers showed a stabilisation of function, with most having normal, albeit diminished, FEV1 values compared with pre-disaster levels. During the subsequent 6 years of follow-up, FEV1 adjusted for race, sex, height, and age on 9/11 in firefighters who had never smoked decreased by 26 mL per year, and the average percentage of predicted FEV1 did not change. Smokers had a similar pattern but at a steeper rate of decline than did non-smokers. Although an earlier arrival on 9/11 predicted a decrease in adjusted FEV1 in the first year, it did not predict adjusted FEV1 at the conclusion of follow-up. This finding suggests that exposure predicted initial illness, but that individual host factors such as genomics or non-WTC exposures affected the long-term course.

**中文翻译：** 肺功能数据提供异常的客观生理证据，并补充症状报告。对 12079 名纽约消防部门应急人员在 9/11 后中位 3 个月进行的肺量检查显示，FEV1 较其灾前最后一次测量平均下降 372 mL，相当于正常老化 12 年的损失。下降速率与按到达 WTC 现场日期估计的暴露程度相关。第一年大幅下降之后，消防员和急救人员的肺量检查显示功能趋稳，多数人 FEV1 仍正常，尽管低于灾前。在随后 6 年随访中，从不吸烟的消防员在校正种族、性别、身高和 9/11 当日年龄后，FEV1 每年下降 26 mL，预测 FEV1 平均百分比没有变化。吸烟者模式相似，但下降更陡。尽管 9/11 当天更早到达能预示第一年校正 FEV1 的下降，却并不能预示随访结束时的校正 FEV1。这一发现提示：暴露预示初始疾病，但基因组学或非 WTC 暴露等个体宿主因素影响长期病程。

**讲解：** 372 mL = 12 years ageing 是比喻，不是真的老了 12 岁。26 mL/年接近普通老化斜率，说明第一刀已经砍完，后面走宿主因素。arrival time 只管第一年。

### Interventions

> Interventions that might reduce physical and mental health effects in the short and medium terms after disasters are immediate outreach: screening and treatment of high-risk populations with targeted, exposure-specific interventions to prevent long-term mental health sequelae; early and consistent use of appropriate respirators when environmental hazards cannot be controlled; and disaster preparedness training and shift rotations to enable shorter work duration of rescue workers.

**中文翻译：** 在灾害后的短期和中期，可能减少身心健康影响的干预是：立即外展；用有针对性、暴露特异的干预对高危人群进行筛查和治疗，以防止长期精神健康后遗症；当环境危害无法控制时，及早并持续使用适当的呼吸器；以及备灾培训与轮班，以缩短救援人员的工作时间。

**讲解：** 三件套：外展筛查治疗 / 呼吸器 / 轮班减工时。没有「坚强一点」。

## 四、长难句

:::derivation{label="长难句 1 · 监测设备被毁"}
Routine environmental monitoring equipment in lower Manhattan was damaged and destroyed in the aftermath of the attacks, which made it difficult to quantify exposure to the massive dust cloud generated by the collapsing towers and potentially to toxic substances that permeated the clean-up site, residences, and workplaces in the area.

**结构：** 主句 equipment was damaged and destroyed；which 指整个事件，made it difficult to quantify exposure to A and potentially to B；B 再带 that permeated 三个并列地点。
:::

:::derivation{label="长难句 2 · 暴露管一年，宿主管以后"}
Although an earlier arrival on 9/11 predicted a decrease in adjusted FEV1 in the first year, it did not predict adjusted FEV1 at the conclusion of follow-up. This finding suggests that exposure predicted initial illness, but that individual host factors such as genomics or non-WTC exposures affected the long-term course.

**结构：** Although 让步 + 主句否定；suggests that A, but that B。两个 that 从句并列，是综述的收口判断。
:::

:::keypoint{label="本课必须能默写的账"}
- 三组人群：直接 / 纽约间接 / 全美与国际间接。
- 筛查高分 = probable PTSD，不是诊断。
- 全国 3–5 天：44% 实质压力；电视间接 1%（32 种创伤最低）。
- 曼哈顿 110 街以南 1–2 月：PTSD 7.5%，抑郁约 9%；6 月下降。
- 儿童 8236 人：29% 至少一种；广场恐怖 15% > 分离焦虑 12% > PTSD 11%。
- 消防员：咳嗽 54% vs 4%；FEV1 −372 mL = 12 年老化；此后从不吸烟者 −26 mL/年。
- 干预：外展 + 呼吸器 + 轮班。
:::
