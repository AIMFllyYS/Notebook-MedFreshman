# Lesson 12 · Medical Ethics

本课 Text A 是 *Withholding Information from Patients — When Less Is More*（对患者隐瞒信息——少即是多），Ronald M. Epstein、David N. Korones、Timothy E. Quill，*New England Journal of Medicine*，2010。它不是鼓励撒谎，而是把「知情同意」从「信息越多越好」改写成一套**可操作的临床判断**：何时必须告知，何时可以少说，何时必须回头重说。伦理三原则在课文里同时在场：autonomy（自主）、beneficence（有利）、non-maleficence（不伤害）。

:::insight{label="少即是多，不是家长制作废知情"}
作者自称「极为重视对患者实情相告」。他们要限制的是 **information dump**（信息倾倒）：把无关、不可消化、只会制造恐惧的细节一股脑交给患者，反而削弱自主。Less is more 的主语是「无关或有害的信息」，不是「诊断本身」。
:::

:::definition{label="三原则在本课的位置"}
- **Autonomy 自主**：患者应能知情并按自己的价值观做决定。
- **Beneficence 有利**：告知应帮助决策、打开资源、帮助赋予疾病意义。
- **Non-maleficence 不伤害**：告知不应造成无法承受的认知/情感负荷，也不应挤掉真正重要的对话。

自主权必须与有利、不伤害**均衡**，不是把后两项开除。
:::

## 二、核心词汇

:::derivation{label="withhold /wɪðˈhəʊld/ vt."}
**书本义：** 拒绝给予；隐瞒；扣留（信息、待遇）

**词形：** withhold → withheld → withholding；withholding information from patients 对患者隐瞒信息。

**常用搭配：**
- withhold information / a diagnosis 隐瞒信息/诊断
- withhold consent 不予同意（另一搭配，本课不是这个）

**例句：**
1. The authors reflect on circumstances in which physicians withhold from patients information about conditions, treatments, and outcomes.（作者反思医师对患者隐瞒病情、治疗与结局信息的情形。）
2. Withholding pertinent information without the patient's knowledge is ethically tense even when intended as kindness.（即使出于善意，不告知患者而隐瞒相关信息，在伦理上仍然紧张。）

✍️ Withhold is an action with a reason; forgetting to mention is not withholding in this essay's sense.（withhold 是有理由的行动；忘了说在本文意义上还不算隐瞒。）
:::

:::derivation{label="autonomy /ɔːˈtɒnəmi/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 自主；自主权（患者按自身价值观做决定的权利）

**词形：** autonomous（a.）；relational autonomy 关系性自主（课文理想目标）。

**常用搭配：**
- the ethical principle of autonomy 自主的伦理原则
- enhance or diminish a patient's sense of autonomy 增强或削弱患者的自主意识
- relational autonomy 关系性自主

**例句：**
1. The ethical principle of autonomy would suggest that patients should always be fully informed.（自主原则似乎提示患者应始终被充分告知。）
2. Dumping all available information on patients may overwhelm them and paradoxically weaken their ability to make wise decisions.（把所有现有信息一股脑交给患者，可能使其不堪重负，从而适得其反地削弱其明智决策的能力。）

✍️ Autonomy is capacity-in-relationship here, not a pile of printouts.（这里的自主是关系中的能力，不是一摞打印单。）
:::

:::derivation{label="beneficence /bɪˈnefɪsns/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 有利；行善（促进患者福利的义务）

**词形：** 与 benefit 同源；课文与 non-maleficence 成对。

**常用搭配：**
- the principle of beneficence 有利原则
- balance autonomy with beneficence 在自主与有利之间取得平衡

**例句：**
1. Autonomy needs to be balanced against the moral duties of beneficence and non-maleficence.（自主权需要与有利、不伤害的道德责任均衡考虑。）
2. Instrumental benefits of information — aiding decisions, opening options, giving access to resources — belong under beneficence.（信息的辅助性益处——帮助决策、打开选择、提供资源——属于有利。）

✍️ Beneficence is not "the doctor knows best" as a blank cheque; it is a duty that must still face autonomy.（有利不是医生说了算的空白支票；它仍须面对自主。）
:::

:::derivation{label="surrogate /ˈsʌrəɡət/ n. a."}
**书本义：** [C] 代理人；替代者（本课：代为知情、代为决策的人）

**词形：** surrogate decision-maker；课文亦用 proxy。

**常用搭配：**
- a surrogate / health-care proxy 医护代理人
- disclose information to a surrogate 向代理人告知

**例句：**
1. When patients lack cognitive capacity to understand, someone else should be informed as surrogate.（当患者缺乏理解病情的认知能力时，应由他人作为代理人知情。）
2. If a patient clearly or tacitly refuses important clinical information, the facts may be given to a proxy while the team explores why the patient refuses.（若患者明确或暗示拒绝重要临床信息，可将实情告知代理人，同时探究拒绝背后的原因。）

✍️ A surrogate is not a convenient relative who prefers silence; the essay revisits withholding when family and patient disagree.（代理人不是图省事、喜欢沉默的亲戚；当家属与患者意见相左，课文要求重新考虑隐瞒。）
:::

:::derivation{label="incidental /ˌɪnsɪˈdentl/ a."}
**书本义：** 附带的；非主要目的而出现的

**词形：** incidental finding 附带发现；incidentally（ad.）

**常用搭配：**
- an incidental finding on CT 扫描中的附带发现
- incidental hepatic cyst 附带的肝囊肿

**例句：**
1. Discussing at length an incidental benign liver cyst can crowd out the conversation about a serious pulmonary nodule.（对附带的良性肝囊肿谈论过久，会挤掉关于严重肺结节的对话。）
2. Incidental findings are not false; they are often the wrong agenda.（附带发现不是假的；它们常常是错误的议程。）

✍️ Incidental means "not why we ordered the test", not "unimportant forever".（incidental 指「不是这次检查的目的」，不是「永远不重要」。）
:::

:::derivation{label="overwhelm /ˌəʊvəˈwelm/ vt."}
**书本义：** 使不堪重负；压垮（认知或情感）

**词形：** overwhelming（a.）；overwhelmed（a.）

**常用搭配：**
- overwhelm the patient with information 用信息压垮患者
- cognitive overload 认知超负荷（课文近义表达）

**例句：**
1. Too much information can produce cognitive overload that impairs rather than aids understanding and choice, especially when patients and families are already under strain.（信息过多会造成认知超负荷，有损而非有助于理解与选择，尤其当病人与家人已在巨大压力下。）
2. Dumping every available fact may overwhelm patients and weaken decision-making.（倾倒每一条现成事实可能压垮患者并削弱决策。）

✍️ Overload is a clinical harm in this essay, listed beside pain and fear.（在本文里，超负荷是临床伤害，与疼痛、恐惧并列。）
:::

:::derivation{label="bias /ˈbaɪəs/ n. vt."}
**书本义：** [C, U] 偏颇；成见；使有偏见

**词形：** biased（a.）；unconscious bias 无意识偏颇。

**常用搭配：**
- conscious or unconscious bias 有意识或无意识的偏颇
- racial bias in information-giving 告知中的种族偏颇

**例句：**
1. Even after accounting for education and socio-economic status, physicians tend to give less information to Black patients than to white patients.（即使考虑到教育水平和社会经济地位，相对于白人患者，医生仍倾向于给黑人患者较少信息。）
2. Individualised judgment is the ideal; bias is why that ideal needs cross-checks.（个别化判断是理想；偏颇是这个理想需要交叉核对的原因。）

✍️ The essay's warning is empirical, not decorative: less information to Black patients is documented, not a hypothetical slur.（课文的警告是经验性的，不是装饰：给黑人患者更少信息是被记录的，不是假想的骂名。）
:::

:::derivation{label="disclose /dɪsˈkləʊz/ vt."}
**书本义：** 披露；告知；公开（信息）

**词形：** disclosure（n.）；full disclosure 充分披露；delayed disclosure 延迟披露。

**常用搭配：**
- disclose a diagnosis / prognosis 告知诊断/预后
- delay disclosure 延迟披露

**例句：**
1. In some special cases, withholding or delaying disclosure of potentially important information may be appropriate.（在某些特例中，对潜在重要信息隐瞒不报或延迟披露可能更合适。）
2. If the patient asks, the physician should disclose the facts.（如果患者要求，医生应该告以实情。）

✍️ Delay is still a form of withholding and must remain an exception, not a habit.（延迟仍是隐瞒的一种形式，必须保持为特例，不能养成习惯。）
:::

:::derivation{label="adherence /ədˈhɪərəns/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 依从性（按方案服药、就诊）

**词形：** adhere to a regimen；课文用 promote adherence to the treatment plan。

**常用搭配：**
- adherence to therapy / a plan 对治疗/方案的依从
- time spent informing vs. promoting adherence 花在告知上的时间 vs. 促进依从的时间

**例句：**
1. Physicians routinely judge whether time spent giving facts will interfere with other important work such as decision-making or promoting adherence.（医生会常规判断：花时间提供实情，是否会干扰决策或促进依从等其他重要工作。）
2. Information that buries the purpose of treatment under rare side-effects can damage adherence.（让罕见副作用淹没治疗目的的信息，会损害依从。）

✍️ Adherence is not obedience; it is the practical side of a shared plan.（依从不是服从；它是共同方案的实践面。）
:::

:::derivation{label="prognosis /prɒɡˈnəʊsɪs/ n."}
**书本义：** [C] 预后（对病程与结局的判断）

**词形：** 复数为 prognoses；形容词 prognostic。词根 gnosis 知，pro- 向前。

**常用搭配：**
- information unrelated to prognosis or treatment choice 与预后或治疗选择无关的信息
- a poor / favourable prognosis 不良/良好预后

**例句：**
1. Some information is unrelated to prognosis or treatment choice, yet its emotional charge causes needless distress.（有些信息与预后或治疗选择无关，但其情感特性会导致不必要的苦恼。）
2. A single blast cell on a routine blood count may have no prognostic meaning and still terrify parents.（常规血常规上的单个胚细胞可能毫无预后意义，却仍使父母惊恐。）

✍️ If it does not change prognosis or choice, ask what emotion it will buy before you speak.（若它不改变预后或选择，开口前先问它会买来什么情绪。）
:::

## 三、课文精讲 · Text A Withholding Information from Patients

课文逻辑是：先承认自主原则 → 指出信息也可能伤害 → 列出伦理上较易接受的三种隐瞒 → 进入灰色地带 → 用益处/负担两条尺子 → 举临床例子 → 指出偏颇与制度激励 → 给出可操作原则。

### 开篇 · 自主不是唯一尺子

> As clinicians who strongly value truth telling and active patient involvement in medical decision making, we have lately been reflecting on the circumstances in which physicians consciously (and sometimes unconsciously) withhold from patients information about their conditions, treatments, and outcomes. The ethical principle of autonomy would suggest that patients should always be fully informed, not only so that they can make the best possible decisions, but also because information helps them to make sense of and cope with illness. However, since information can sometimes increase patients' cognitive and emotional burden and lead to greater confusion rather than clarity, the right of autonomy needs to be balanced against the moral responsibilities of beneficence (doing good) and non-maleficence (doing no harm). Physicians also make routine judgments about whether time spent providing facts will interfere with other important work, such as making decisions or promoting adherence to a treatment plan.

**中文翻译：** 作为极为重视对患者实情相告、以便患者积极参与医疗决策的临床医师，我们最近一直在反思那些医生有意识地（有时无意识地）对患者隐瞒病情、治疗方案和治疗结果的情形。患者自主原则总在提示我们：患者应该一直有完全的知情权，这样不仅有助于他们做出可能最佳的选择，也有助于他们了解和应对疾病。然而，知情有时会增加患者的认知和情感负担，不仅无助于了解病情，反而导致更大的困惑，因而自主权需要均衡考虑有利于患者（有利原则）和不危害患者（不伤害原则）的道德责任。医生还会做出常规判断：花费时间提供实情，是否会干扰制定决策或促进治疗方案依从性等其他重要工作。

**讲解：** 第一人称 we 把作者放进诊所，不是法庭。always be fully informed 是自主原则的「看起来如此」，however 立刻限制。cognitive and emotional burden 是后文所有例子的总名。time 本身被写成一种伦理资源：说次要细节会挤掉依从与决策。

### 较易接受的三种情形

> In general, clinicians and ethicists readily accept several circumstances in which information is withheld from patients. When patients lack the cognitive capacity to understand information about their illness, someone else should be informed in their place. When urgent intervention is needed — for example, a ruptured aneurysm — the potential benefits of early action outweigh incomplete or delayed disclosure. When patients explicitly or implicitly (as by remaining silent or repeatedly changing the subject) refuse important clinical information, the information may be given to a health-care proxy, while the team infers the reasons behind the refusal. But when patients directly request information, or when patients and families disagree about the clinical situation, the decision to withhold must be reconsidered.

**中文翻译：** 一般而言，临床医师和伦理学家容易接受以下几种对患者隐瞒病情的情形。当患者缺乏理解疾病信息的认知能力，应由其他人代为知情。在需要紧急干预（比如动脉瘤破裂）时，相对于不完全告知或延迟告知，早期干预潜在的利大于弊。当患者明确或非明确（如沉默不语或反复转移话题）拒绝获知重要临床信息，可将病情告知医护代理人，同时推测拒绝背后的原因。但是，当患者直接要求知情，或当患者与家属对临床情况意见相左，隐瞒实情的决定需要重新考虑。

**讲解：** 三许可 + 两刹车。许可：无认知能力 / 急症（动脉瘤）/ 拒绝知情。刹车：患者直接要求；患者与家属意见冲突。implicit refusal 的行为证据是沉默和转移话题，仍要探原因，不是一默认可骗。

:::compare{title="可接受的少说 vs 必须回头"}
| 可接受（一般） | 必须重新考虑 |
|----------------|----------------|
| 无认知能力 → 代理人知情 | 患者直接要求知情 |
| 急症，延迟告知的代价大于早期行动 | 患者与家属意见相左 |
| 患者明确或暗示拒绝 → 告知代理人并探究原因 | 发现隐瞒可能被拆穿且后果严重 |
:::

### 灰色地带 · 两条尺子

> There is, however, a grey area: patients who have the capacity to understand and have not clearly said they do not want to know, yet physicians — intentionally or not — withhold information. In such cases, the choice to give or withhold should aim at maximising the patient's benefit and minimising psychological, cognitive and emotional burden. Physicians should consider whether disclosure would matter to the patient in two ways: instrumental benefits (aiding decision-making, offering meaningful options, providing access to resources) and emotional or relational benefits (helping the patient cope, find meaning, quiet guilt or stop blame, and thereby build trust).

**中文翻译：** 然而这一问题也存在灰色地带：患者有能力理解，也并未明确表达不想知情，医生却（有意或无意地）隐瞒实情。这类情况下，选择提供或隐瞒应本着患者利益最大化、心理—认知—情感负担最小化的原则。医生应从两方面考虑告知是否对患者意义重大：辅助性益处（有助于决策、提供多种有意义的选择、提供获取资源的途径），以及情感或关系性益处（帮助患者应对疾病、发现生命意义、平息愧疚或停止责备，进而建立信任）。

**讲解：** grey area 是本课真正要训练的判断。两条尺子：instrumental / emotional-relational。不是「我忙」或「怕吵架」。

### 负担的例子

> The burdens of informing require more complex thought. Especially when patients and families are already under great strain, too much information can produce cognitive overload that impairs rather than aids understanding and choice. A rambling recitation of improbable side-effects of a drug can blur more relevant facts and keep both parties from discussing the purpose of treatment and the practical details. If a patient being scanned for a suspected pulmonary nodule is found to have a benign liver cyst, dwelling on the incidental finding can obstruct discussion of a serious disease and its options. Telling a patient with panic disorder that the QT interval looks possibly prolonged only heightens fear of heart disease and interferes with training and initial treatment for panic. Some information is unrelated to prognosis or treatment choice, yet its emotional charge causes needless distress. A single blast cell found on a routine blood count in a child with leukemia may have no diagnostic meaning and change no decision, yet the information can devastate the parents. Providing exhaustive clinical detail also takes time and therefore steals time from what matters. Spending that time explaining to a hospice patient that she has five rather than three sites of lung-cancer metastasis is inferior to discussing how to relieve symptoms and how the family should take part in care. If a breast-cancer screening report notes a technical defect in the images that are still usable, with no need to repeat the films before the next scheduled scan, rehearsing this fact is a poorer use of time than reinforcing other health behaviours.

**中文翻译：** 对患者知情可能带来的负担，需要更复杂的考虑。尤其当病人和家人已在巨大压力下，过多信息带来的认知超负荷可能有损而非有助于了解病情和治疗抉择。对某一药物不大可能出现的副作用作喋喋不休的陈述，不仅会模糊更相关的信息，也会妨碍双方专心讨论治疗目的与细节处理。若一位因怀疑肺结节接受扫描的病人被发现良性肝囊肿，过多探讨附带结果会妨碍对一种严重疾病及其治疗选择的讨论。把「疑似 QT 间期延长」告知一位恐慌症患者，只会加重他对心脏病的恐惧，妨碍恐慌症的训练和初始治疗。有些信息与预后或治疗选择并不相关，但其情感特性会导致不必要的苦恼。白血病患儿在常规血常规复查中出现一个单个胚细胞，本身无诊断意义也不影响任何决策，却令父母痛苦万分。提供详尽临床信息也要花费时间，因而占用讨论重要事宜的时间。耗费时间向临终关怀医院的患者解释她有五处而非三处肺癌转移，不如讨论如何减轻症状以及家属应如何配合。若乳腺筛查报告提示扫描图像有技术缺陷但尚可使用、下次预定扫描前无需重拍，把这一情况转述一遍，不如多努力强化其他健康行为。

**讲解：** 五个例子要能对上「为什么少说」：

:::keypoint{label="五个少说的临床例子"}
- 罕见副作用清单 → 淹没治疗目的
- 良性肝囊肿（附带发现）→ 挤掉肺结节
- 疑似 QT 延长告诉恐慌症患者 → 强化心脏病恐惧
- 无意义的单个胚细胞 → 父母崩溃、不改决策
- 临终患者 5 处 vs 3 处转移 / 尚可使用的「技术缺陷」胶片 → 偷走症状控制与健康行为的时间
:::

### 信息与自主意识，关系性自主

> Finally, the physician should consider whether a given piece of information will enhance or diminish the patient's sense of autonomy. If information makes the illness clearer or more controllable, the patient can better decide according to his or her own values. Yet more information is not always better. Pouring out every available fact may overwhelm patients and, paradoxically, weaken their ability to make informed decisions. Moreover, the capacity to obtain and use information, and to take part fully in care, depends not only on the quantity of information received but also on the quality of relationships with physicians, family and others. If patients do not trust the physician, they often seek still more information. If they must sort complex facts alone, they may feel abandoned and unable to exercise control. The ideal may be "relational autonomy": patients, those close to them, and physicians work together to find information, interpret its context and meaning, and act on it.

**中文翻译：** 最后，医师还应考虑某一疾病信息将会增强还是降低患者的自主意识。如果信息使病情更明确或更有掌控力，患者就能更好地依照自己的价值观做出决策。然而病情信息并非越多越好。把所有现有信息一股脑透露给患者，可能使其不堪重负，从而适得其反地削弱明智决策的能力。此外，患者获取和利用信息、全力投入疾病护理的能力，不单纯依赖信息量，也取决于他们与医师、家属及其他人关系的性质。如果患者不信任医师，他们往往会进一步寻求信息。如果患者只能独自整理复杂病情，他们可能感到被遗弃而无法发挥掌控力。理想的目标或许是「自治关系」（关系性自主）：病人、亲人和医生一起寻找信息，解释其来龙去脉及含义，并按信息采取行动。

**讲解：** paradoxically 是关键词：更多信息可以减少自主。distrust → 更多搜寻；孤立解读 → 被遗弃。relational autonomy 把自主从个人英雄主义改成三人小组。

> Physicians often make tacit judgments about how much information a patient can reasonably digest, how to interpret it without distortion, how particular facts will affect the patient and family's ability to cope and to decide, and how to avoid frightening or bewildering the patient with excess detail. Ideally, each patient is considered as an individual — cognitive capacity, education, values, and wish to know medical details. In practice, however, when deciding whether to withhold information that may be clinically relevant, physicians must watch for conscious or unconscious bias.

**中文翻译：** 对于患者可合理消化多少信息、如何合理解读以免断章取义、特定信息将如何影响患者及家属应对与决策的能力、如何避免因过多细节使患者惊恐无所适从，医生常常有心照不宣的判断。理想状态下，医生应把每个病人当作个体来考虑：认知能力、教育水平、价值观、以及详细了解医疗信息的意愿。但在临床判断的实践中，对可能与临床相关的信息是否隐瞒时，医生们需要提防有意识或无意识的偏颇。

**讲解：** tacit judgments 不是禁止，是必须拿出来审查。individualise 是理想；bias 是实践里的蛀虫。

### 偏颇与制度

> Although most people deny their own prejudices, physicians, like other human beings, show discrimination in their behaviour. For example, even after accounting for patients' education and socio-economic status, physicians tend to give less information to Black patients than to white patients. In time-pressured health care, clinical judgment can be used as a reason to withhold information in order to avoid time-consuming or burdensome elements of care. Current economic incentives generally reward intervention and discourage conversation about the limits of treatment. Moreover, some physicians may withhold information — omitting alternatives or controversial elements of care — simply to avoid conflict with a possibly argumentative patient or family. Everyday decisions about how much to share require great flexibility and sufficient candour.

**中文翻译：** 尽管大多数人不承认自己带有成见，医生的行为也像所有普通人一样表现出一定的歧视。例如，即使考虑到患者的教育水平和社会经济地位，相对于白人患者，医生仍倾向于给黑人患者较少信息。在时间紧迫的医疗环境中，为了避免费时或繁重的护理环节，临床判断可以成为是否隐瞒的一个借口。当前的经济奖励规则总体上提倡干预行为，不鼓励关于治疗方案局限性的对话。而且，某些医生可能隐瞒某些信息——不提及治疗的备选方案或有争议的护理环节——纯粹为了避免与或许喜欢争辩的患者或家属发生冲突。究竟分享多少信息，这些日常决定需要极强的应变能力和足够的真诚。

**讲解：** 三种坏的少说：种族偏颇；用「临床判断」逃避费时护理；怕吵架而藏备选方案。economic incentives reward intervention 把制度写进伦理，不是个人品德课。

### 收束原则

> We believe that when information offers no clear benefit and the patient has not asked to know, physicians should not disclose information likely to be unbearable or grievously distressing. Excess information, especially when it is not clinically relevant, can crowd out important discussions. In some circumstances it may even be appropriate to withhold or delay disclosure of potentially important information — but only as a special case. We suggest these simple principles. If the patient asks, the physician should tell the truth. If the physician is anxious about the consequences of the patient discovering that information was withheld, the decision to withhold should be reconsidered. Physicians should examine their tacit judgments by asking, for example: What would a trusted colleague think? Am I uneasy? Am I assuming that the patient shares my own values? They should correct possible bias by knowing the patient as a person. When uncertain whether to withhold, the physician should discuss the decision with a trusted person in the patient's circle, or with an experienced colleague, or with both.

**中文翻译：** 我们认为，在获得信息不会带来任何明显益处、而且患者并未要求知情的情况下，医生应该对可能引起患者难以承受或忧伤的信息知情不报。过度知情，尤其当信息与临床治疗并不相关时，可能使很多重要讨论无法展开。甚至在某些情况下，对潜在重要信息隐瞒不报或延迟披露更为合适——但这仅限于特例。我们提出以下简单原则：如果患者要求，医生应该告以实情。如果对患者发现被隐瞒实情的后果感到焦虑，医生应该重新考虑隐瞒的决定。医生应该审查自己某些心照不宣的判断，问自己：我信任的同仁会怎么看？我是否心里不安？我是否臆想患者跟我有同样的价值观念？医生应该通过对患者个人的了解克服可能的偏颇。对隐瞒举棋不定时，应与患者人际圈里值得信赖的核心人物讨论，或与一位经验丰富的同事讨论，或两者一起讨论。

**讲解：** 最后不是口号，是清单。If the patient asks → tell。Anxiety that the secret will explode → reconsider。Three questions to a tacit judgment。Uncertainty → 不独断。

:::callout{kind="pitfall" label="不要把课文读成「可以骗」"}
三条刹车始终在：患者要求必须说；怕被拆穿就要重想；拿不准就找人商量。灰色地带的少说，对象是无益处、未请求、且会造成难以承受痛苦的信息，不是诊断本身。
:::

## 四、长难句

:::derivation{label="长难句 1 · 自主须与有利、不伤害均衡"}
However, since information can sometimes increase patients' cognitive and emotional burden and lead to greater confusion rather than clarity, the right of autonomy needs to be balanced against the moral responsibilities of beneficence (doing good) and non-maleficence (doing no harm).

**结构：** since 原因从句（increase A and lead to B rather than C）；主句 needs to be balanced against + 两个名词，括号内英文释义。rather than 否定的是 clarity，不是 information itself。
:::

:::derivation{label="长难句 2 · 关系性自主"}
The ideal may be "relational autonomy": patients, those close to them, and physicians work together to find information, interpret its context and meaning, and act on it.

**结构：** 冒号后三个主语并列 + 三个动词 find / interpret / act on。自主的单位从「一个人拥有全部事实」变成「一组人一起解释事实」。
:::

:::keypoint{label="本课必须能默写的账"}
- 三原则：autonomy / beneficence / non-maleficence。
- 三许可：无能力、急症、拒绝知情（告知代理人）。
- 两刹车：患者要求说；患者与家属冲突。
- 两条尺子：辅助性益处 vs 情感/关系益处；负担 = 超负荷、恐惧、偷时间。
- 坏的少说：种族偏颇、逃费时工作、怕吵架藏备选。
- 原则：问则告；怕拆穿则重想；拿不准则商量。
:::
