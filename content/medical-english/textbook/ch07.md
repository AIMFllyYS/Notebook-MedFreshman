# Lesson 7 · Social Medicine

本课 Text A 是 Matthew R. Anderson、Lanny Smith 与 Victor W. Sidel 的 *What is Social Medicine?*（社会医学是什么）。张梅课件把 19 段收成三块：美国医疗的公司化与社会医学三条原则（段 1–3）→ 原则的历史起源（段 4–15）→ 原则与当下执业（段 16–19）。词根表走循环/呼吸：angi/o、vas/o、phleb/o、thromb/o、lymph/o、bronch/o、laryng/o、pulmon/o、spir/o。

:::insight{label="社会医学不是「在医院门口做公益」"}
课文三条原则要一起记：社会经济条件塑造疾病；人群健康是社会事务；社会须用个体手段**和**社会手段促进健康。少任何一条，都会把 Virchow 读成只会切细胞的病理学家，或把 Bethune 读成只会做手术的英雄传。
:::

:::definition{label="社会医学的三条共同原则"}
Over time the term "social medicine" took on varied meanings as it was adapted to differing societies and diverse social conditions. Nonetheless, certain common principles underlie the term:

1. Social and economic conditions profoundly impact health, disease, and the practice of medicine.
2. The health of the population is a matter of social concern.
3. Society should promote health through both individual and social means.
:::

## 二、核心词汇

:::derivation{label="prescient /ˈpresiənt/ a."}
**书本义：** 有先见之明的；预见的

**词形：** pre-（before）+ scient（know）；名词 prescience。课件对照：prescribe 事先写、开方；preserve 保存；preview 预习。

**常用搭配：**
- a prescient warning 有先见的警告
- look prescient 显得有先见

**例句：**
1. When we consider the AIDS pandemic, his words look so prescient.（当我们考虑艾滋病大流行时，他的话显得如此有先见之明。）
2. These workers were more prescient than their superiors about the economic situation.（这些工人比上级更有远见，领悟到了经济形势。——课件填空）

✍️ A prescient diagnosis in social medicine is often a reading of poverty, not a lucky guess about a microbe.（社会医学里有先见的诊断，常常是读懂贫困，而不是碰巧猜中一种微生物。）
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="pre- 家族"}
课件把 prescient 放进 pre- 家族：presage 预感、prescribe 开方、preserve 保存、preview 预习。反义前缀 post- / retro-。不要把 prescient 写成 \*precient（课件一处拼写错误）。
:::

:::derivation{label="epidemic /ˌepɪˈdemɪk/ n. a."}
**书本义：** [C] 流行病；（某地某时新发病例远超预期）疫情；a. 流行性的

**词形：** epi-（upon）+ dem（people，同 democracy）；epidemiology 流行病学；pandemic 大流行。

**常用搭配：**
- an influenza epidemic 流感疫情
- epidemic typhus 流行性斑疹伤寒
- epidemic of strikes 突发大罢工潮（引申）

**例句：**
1. An epidemic occurs when the incidence rate substantially exceeds what is expected.（当发病率显著超过基于近期经验的「预期」时，就构成 epidemic。）
2. Epidemic typhus is passed from human to human by the body louse.（流行性斑疹伤寒由体虱在人与人之间传播。）

✍️ Use epidemic for a regional outbreak, pandemic when the same fire crosses continents.（地区暴发用 epidemic，跨洲且比例极高用 pandemic。）
:::

:::compare{title="academic / epidemic / pandemic / epidemiology"}
| 词 | 词源要点 | 课文/课件用法 |
|----|----------|----------------|
| academic | academy 学院 | 也可指「纸上谈兵、不导向决定」 |
| epidemic | epi- + dem 民 | 某地区、某时段超出预期的暴发 |
| pandemic | pan- 全部 + dem | 跨大区域、影响极高比例人口 |
| pathology | patho- 病 + -logy | 病因、发病机制、形态、临床表现四件 |
| epidemiology | epidemi- + -logy | 人群中疾病分布与决定因素 |
:::

:::derivation{label="germane /dʒɜːˈmeɪn/ a."}
**书本义：** 有关的；切题的（正式）

**词形：** germanely；germaneness。固定 germane to sth。

**常用搭配：**
- germane to the issue / case 与议题/案件有关
- questions that are germane and central 切题且关键的问题

**例句：**
1. Advances in pathology and microbiology made social factors seem less germane in the etiology of disease.（病理学与微生物学的巨大进步，让社会因素看起来与疾病病因不那么相关。）
2. A number of documents which were germane to the case were suppressed.（许多与案件密切相关的文件被查禁。——课件）

✍️ Germane is "relevant to this argument", not "German".（germane 是切题，不是「日耳曼的」。）
:::

:::derivation{label="trump /trʌmp/ vt. n."}
**书本义：** vt. 胜过；压过；（牌戏）出王牌；n. 王牌

**词形：** trump sth. up 捏造罪名（与课文义不同）。

**常用搭配：**
- trump any argument 压倒任何论点
- rights have trumped public health 权利压过了公共卫生

**例句：**
1. If the fetus is human life, that trumps any argument about the freedom of the mother.（课件例：若胚胎即生命，则压倒关于母亲自由权的论点。）
2. The rights of pharmaceutical corporations to their "intellectual property" have trumped public health.（制药公司的「知识产权」压过了公共卫生。）

✍️ In this essay trump is a verb of power, not a proper name.（本课 trump 是权力动词，不是专有名词。）
:::

:::derivation{label="disparity /dɪˈspærəti/ n."}
**书本义：** [C, U]（尤指不公正造成的）悬殊；差距

**词形：** 反义 parity；近义 inequality / discrepancy。

**常用搭配：**
- health disparities 健康不平等
- marked disparity among age groups 年龄组间的明显差异
- disparity between two figures 两个数字之间的悬殊

**例句：**
1. The disparity between them is enormous.（两者差距相当之大。——课件）
2. Early social medicine was inspired by huge mortality disparities among social classes in European health statistics.（社会医学的早期灵感，来自欧洲卫生统计中不同阶层间巨大的死亡率差异。）

✍️ Disparity names an unfair gap; difference can be neutral.（disparity 点名不公正的缺口；difference 可以中性。）
:::

:::derivation{label="hierarchical /ˌhaɪəˈrɑːkɪkl/ a."}
**书本义：** 等级体系的；分层的

**词形：** hierarchy（n.）；反义 nonhierarchical。课件：Congress is collegial and not a hierarchical body.

**常用搭配：**
- a hierarchical body / database / relationship 等级机构/分层数据库/等级关系
- Maslow's hierarchy of human needs 马斯洛需求层次

**例句：**
1. All state judiciaries are hierarchical, like the federal system.（各州司法机构都像联邦体系一样分层。）
2. Community-oriented primary care tries to step out of a purely hierarchical clinic.（社区导向的初级保健试图走出纯粹等级化的诊室。）

✍️ Hierarchical describes a ladder of command, not "advanced" or "scientific".（hierarchical 是命令梯子，不是「高级」或「科学」。）
:::

:::derivation{label="ramification /ˌræmɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/ n."}
**书本义：** [C, 常复数] 衍生后果；派生影响；也可指分支结构

**词形：** ramify（v. 分枝）。课件：Electronic information crimes are a ramification of modern high-technology development.

**常用搭配：**
- legal / political ramifications 法律/政治后果
- ramifications of neighbouring in-laws 姻亲的旁支（课件家族例）

**例句：**
1. Any change is bound to have legal ramifications.（任何变动都必定有法律上的衍生后果。）
2. The way clinical care is delivered has remarkable political ramifications.（提供临床治疗的方式带有显著的政治延伸效应。）

✍️ Ramification in academic prose is usually "downstream consequence", not a tree drawing.（学术散文里 ramification 多半是下游后果，不是画一棵树。）
:::

:::derivation{label="dubious /ˈdjuːbiəs/ a."}
**书本义：** 可疑的；未必可靠的；名声不好的

**词形：** 近义 doubtful / suspicious / skeptical，但 dubious 可贬指人「背景靠不住」。

**常用搭配：**
- drugs of dubious efficacy 疗效可疑的药物
- a dubious reputation 可疑名声

**例句：**
1. Advertising campaigns promote expensive drugs of dubious efficacy.（广告活动在推销疗效可疑的昂贵药物。）
2. His background is a trifle dubious.（他的背景不大可靠。——课件）

✍️ Dubious efficacy means we should not trust the claim of benefit; it does not mean the drug is a placebo by definition.（疗效可疑是不该轻信获益声明，不等于定义上就是安慰剂。）
:::

:::derivation{label="vex /veks/ vt."}
**书本义：** 使烦恼；使生气；使焦虑（常为琐事）

**词形：** vexing（a. 恼人的）；the most vexing questions 最棘手的问题。

**常用搭配：**
- vexing problems in modern health care 现代卫生保健中最令人烦恼的问题
- the memory still vexed him 那次交谈的记忆仍使他气愤

**例句：**
1. We hope social medicine can shed light on some of the most vexing problems in modern health and health care.（我们希望社会医学能阐明现代健康与卫生保健中一些最棘手的问题。）
2. I ain't vex with you.（课件口语例：我不生你的气。）

✍️ Vexing marks the author's negative attitude toward commercialism, not a medical diagnosis.（vexing 标记作者对公司化医疗的否定态度，不是一个诊断名。）
:::

:::derivation{label="etiology /ˌiːtiˈɒlədʒi/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 病因学；病因

**词形：** 课件填空：Pathology addresses four components of disease: cause/etiology, pathogenesis, morphologic changes, clinical manifestations.

**常用搭配：**
- etiology of disease 疾病的病因
- social factors in the etiology 病因中的社会因素

**例句：**
1. Social factors seemed less germane in the etiology of disease after the rise of microbiology.（微生物学兴起后，社会因素在病因里显得不那么相关。）
2. Virchow treated poverty and lack of education as etiologic, not decorative.（魏尔肖把贫困和缺乏教育当作病因，而不是装饰。）

✍️ Etiology is "why the disease occurs", pathogenesis is "how it develops".（etiology 是为何发生，pathogenesis 是如何展开。）
:::

## 三、课文精讲 · Text A What is Social Medicine?

:::compare{title="张梅课件的篇章结构"}

### 段落 1 · 公司议程

> The past two decades have seen a rapid expansion of the corporate agenda in the field of health and health care. Rather than moving toward a system of universal access to medical care in the United States, the access to and quality of clinical services is being turned over increasingly to the insurance industry. Patients are now "customers" and health care a "product line". Clinical research is now funded more by the pharmaceutical industry than by the National Institutes of Health; companies pay the salaries of leading academic experts and set the national research agenda. Clinicians and patients alike are wooed by sophisticated advertising campaigns (often disguised as education) that promote expensive drugs of dubious efficacy. The insertion of "market rationality" into health care has not brought the hoped-for curbing of health care costs. Although the United States spends more per capita on health care than any other country, it still ranks poorly on many health indicators — 27th in the world in life expectancy at birth.

**中文翻译：** 过去二十年，健康与卫生保健领域的企业议程急速扩张。美国并未走向普遍可及的医疗体系，临床服务的可及性与质量正越来越多地被交给保险业。病人成了「客户」，医疗服务成了「生产线」。现在制药产业对临床研究的投资比美国国立卫生研究院还多；它们支付顶级学术专家的薪水并设定国家研究议程。精致的广告活动（常常伪装成教育）在推销疗效可疑的昂贵药物时，同时讨好临床医生和病人。把「市场理性」插入卫生保健，并没有带来所希望的费用遏制。尽管美国是世界上人均医疗花费最高的国家，在许多健康指标上仍表现欠佳，出生时预期寿命仅列全球第 27 位。

**讲解：** The past two decades have seen 是拟人：时间「看见」扩张。turned over to the insurance industry 是权交接，不是技术进步。disguised as education 与 dubious efficacy 是作者态度词。27th in life expectancy 用来打「花得最多 = 最好」的常识。

:::callout{kind="note" label="access to 的介词"}
课件强调 access to sth 中 to 是介词：have access to records。不是 access to do 的不定式（课件另举的 have access to deliver 是另一结构，本课锁介词 to）。
:::

### 段落 2–3 · 未被放过的挑战，与三条原则

> This corporate agenda, however, has not gone unchallenged.

> This history goes back at least to the early nineteenth century, when the relationships among society, disease and medicine began to be studied systematically. This study — and the forms of medical practice derived from it — became known as "social medicine". Over time the term took on varied meanings as it was adapted to differing societies and diverse social conditions. Nonetheless, certain common principles underlie the term: (1) social and economic conditions profoundly impact health, disease, and the practice of medicine; (2) the health of the population is a matter of social concern; (3) society should promote health through both individual and social means.

> In this essay we explore the origins of these concepts in nineteenth-century Europe and their subsequent development in Latin America, South Africa, and the United States. While this brief essay cannot provide a comprehensive examination of social medicine, we hope it will suggest ways in which the historical experience of social medicine can shed light on some of the most vexing problems in modern health and health care.

**中文翻译：** 然而，这一企业议程并非没有受到挑战。

这段历史至少可追溯到十九世纪早期，当时社会、疾病与医学的关系开始被系统研究。这项研究以及由此产生的医学实践形式，被称为「社会医学」。随着时间推移，该术语因适应不同社会与多样社会条件而获得不同含义。尽管如此，某些共同原则构成这个术语的基础：（1）社会经济条件深刻影响健康、疾病与医疗实践；（2）人群健康是社会事务；（3）社会应通过个体手段和社会手段促进健康。

在这篇文章中，我们探索这些概念在十九世纪欧洲的起源及其随后在拉丁美洲、南非和美国的发展。这篇短文无法提供对社会医学的全面考察，但希望能提示：社会医学的历史经验可以如何阐明现代健康与卫生保健中一些最棘手的问题。

**讲解：** has not gone unchallenged 是双重否定 = 已被挑战，课件标为过渡句。underlie = be the basis of。中文对照把 South Africa 写成「南美洲」是笔误，英文是 South Africa。shed light on 是「照亮/阐明」，后面宾语是 vexing problems，即第一段的商业化医疗。

:::callout{kind="pitfall" label="varied / various / variable / variant"}
课件辨析：various 强调许多、各种各样；varied 强调丰富多彩、五花八门；variable 易变；variant 变异体。课文 took on varied meanings = 含义变得多样，不是「几个各种含义」的流水账。
:::

### 段落 4–6 · Virchow 与「人为流行病」

> Although the German physician Rudolf Virchow was not the first to point to the links between society and health, he is considered by many to be the founder of social medicine. He was one of the greatest pathologists of the nineteenth century and contributed notably to the understanding of disease at the cellular level. He was also keenly aware of the social origins of disease. In 1848, while working as a physician at the Berlin hospital, he investigated a typhus epidemic. He argued that social factors such as poverty, lack of education and lack of democracy were key to the development of the epidemic. This experience produced his concept of "artificial epidemics" during social unrest.

> When we consider the AIDS pandemic, his words look so prescient. Social inequalities and disruptions have been central to the spread of HIV. The fight against AIDS is not only a fight against an infectious disease; it is also a fight for women and children, sex workers, and sexual minorities. It is also a struggle to bring clinical care to some of the poorest people in the world. We can truly say that we live in the best of times and the worst of times. Rapid identification of the pathogen and development of highly effective treatments are a miracle of modern biomedicine. In the United States, AIDS is largely treatable, though still not curable. But it is a great outrage — and also characteristic of modern medicine — that most people who need the medications are denied access to them. Of an estimated six million poor people in urgent need of AIDS medicines, only about 400,000 could actually obtain them. Why were those who desperately needed treatment shut out? The answer is not simply the price of the drugs. The "cocktail" of AIDS medicines cost about US$10,000 a year, but the U.S. government long used the World Trade Organisation to limit poor countries' ability to produce and buy generic drugs. The rights of pharmaceutical corporations to their "intellectual property" have trumped public health.

**中文翻译：** 虽然德国医师 Rudolf Virchow 不是第一个指出社会与健康联系的人，但许多人视他为社会医学的奠基人。他被看作十九世纪最伟大的病理学家之一，对在细胞水平理解疾病作出了突出贡献。他也敏锐意识到疾病的社会起源。1848 年，他在柏林医院任内科医生时调查斑疹伤寒流行。他认为贫困、缺乏教育与缺乏民主等社会因素是流行病发展的关键。这一经历使他提出社会动乱期间「人为流行病」的概念。

当我们考虑艾滋病大流行时，他的话显得如此有先见之明。社会不平等与动荡对 HIV 的传播至关重要。对抗艾滋病的斗争不仅是对传染病的斗争，也是为妇女儿童、性工作者、性少数群体的斗争，也是一次把临床护理带给世界上某些最贫困人群的斗争。我们真正处于最好也最坏的时代：迅速确认病原体并发展高效治疗，确是现代生物医学的奇迹。在美国，艾滋病在很大程度上可治疗，但仍不能治愈。但同样令人愤慨——这也是现代医学的特征——大多数需要这些药物的人无法获得它们。在估计急需艾滋病药物的六百万贫困人口中，只有大约 40 万人真正得到。为什么这些迫切需要治疗的人被拒之门外？答案并不完全是药价。艾滋病「鸡尾酒」疗法大约每年一万美元，但美国政府长期通过世贸组织限制贫穷国家生产和购买仿制药的能力。制药公司对其「知识产权」的权利压过了公共卫生。

**讲解：** cellular level 与 social origins 必须两本账一起写，这是 Virchow 的形状。artificial epidemics 不是「假的流行病」，是社会制造的流行。AIDS cocktail = combination therapy，课件用 CD4 与 viral load 解释「是否起作用」。intellectual property 在课文里被放在引号里，是批判对象。中文数字「万人」对应英文约 400,000；写作时用课文的「六百万中约四十万」。

:::callout{kind="note" label="Hodgkin lymphoma 只是专名零件"}
课文后文举 Thomas Hodgkin 为医生中的激进主义者。lymph-o-ma：淋巴 + 瘤。课件补充 Hodgkin 与 non-Hodgkin 的区别、无痛淋巴结肿大等，是词根练习，不是要把课文读成肿瘤学各论。
:::

### 段落 7–8 · 医学为何慢半拍

> Yet organised medicine has traditionally been slow to accept that social factors play an important role in disease. In the late nineteenth century, striking advances in pathology and microbiology made social factors seem less germane in the etiology of disease.

> Much of the early inspiration of social medicine came from European health statistics that documented huge mortality disparities among social classes. Health and disease, wealth and poverty, were correlated. Unfortunately, this remains true today, and health inequalities are an active area of research and activism.

**中文翻译：** 然而，建制医学传统上迟迟不愿接受社会因素在疾病中起重要作用这一事实。十九世纪后期，病理学与微生物学的巨大进步，让社会因素看起来与疾病病因不那么相关。

社会医学的大部分早期灵感，来自证实不同阶层间存在巨大死亡率差异的欧洲卫生统计。健康与疾病、富裕与贫困相互关联。不幸的是，这话在今天仍正确，健康不平等仍是研究与行动的活跃领域。

**讲解：** less germane 是反讽：显微镜越亮，社会越像「不相关」。this remains true today 把 19 世纪统计接到现在。

### 段落 9–11 · 激进医师、拉丁美洲、小写的政治

> There are various explanations for the fact that the rich are healthier than the poor. Perhaps the rich have better genes, or better lifestyles. Many have seen such disparities as a call for social change or revolution. Thomas Hodgkin, famous for describing Hodgkin's lymphoma, and Norman Bethune, the Canadian surgeon who worked for the Spanish Republic and died aiding the Chinese revolution, are two examples of physicians as activists.

> During the twentieth century, Latin America became one of the most active centres of social medicine. After rich theoretical and practical work on the relation between health and society, it insisted on a tight link between theory and practice through praxis. Practitioners have been involved with community organisations, unions, and political movements; many fell victim to political repression.

> The desire for a new model of the health system — more democratic, less authoritarian — did not fall only on Latin America. Indeed, if politics is medicine writ large, it is also apparent that medicine is politics writ small. The way clinical care is delivered has remarkable political ramifications. Socially conscious physicians began to look for forms of clinical practice that would reflect different social values.

**中文翻译：** 对于富人比穷人更健康这一事实，有各种各样的解释。或许富人有更好的基因，或许有更好的生活方式。许多人把这种不一致看作社会变革或革命的号召。因发现霍奇金淋巴瘤而闻名的 Thomas Hodgkin，以及在西班牙内战中为共和派工作、最后因救助中国革命者而死的加拿大外科医生 Norman Bethune，正是医生中的行动者的两个例子。

二十世纪，拉丁美洲成为社会医学最活跃的中心之一。在调查健康与社会关系方面有了丰富的理论与实际工作后，它强调通过实践在理论与操作之间建立紧密联系。实践者投入社区组织、工会和政治运动；许多人成为政治压迫的受害者。

想要一个更新——即更民主、更少专制——的保健系统模型的愿望，不只落在拉丁美洲。事实上，如果政治是大写的医疗，那么很明显医疗是小写的政治。提供临床治疗的方式体现着显著的政治延伸效应。有社会意识的医生开始寻找能反映不同社会价值的临床实践方案。

**讲解：** better genes / better lifestyles 是作者列出的常见开脱，不是作者立场。fell victim to 课件释义 to suffer from。writ large / writ small 是「放大/缩小地写」：政治是放大的医学，医学是缩小的政治。中文翻译「不对等」是在说规模，不是说两者无关。

### 段落 12–15 · 社区卫生、阿拉木图、Montefiore 网站

> Community-oriented primary care therefore included innovations based on the principles of social medicine. Clinical care became a team responsibility: a primary-care doctor, a community nurse, and a health educator (a representative from the community). The team served neighbouring families they knew, treating "patients" as families rather than isolated individuals. Unlike the traditional family doctor, the team systematically considered the broader social context of the individual patient and the epidemiologic meaning of a new diagnosis.

> In the 1960s the U.S. Office of Economic Opportunity first established two community health centres in the United States: one at Columbia Point in Boston, and one at Mound Bayou, Mississippi. The former was founded by Jack Geiger — who had worked with Sidney Kark in South Africa — and his colleagues. Congress eventually created a nationwide network of community health centres that still serve the "third of the nation" that needs them. Two of the present authors (Matt Anderson and Lanny Smith) have practised in these centres. Many ideas of the community-health movement are embodied in the Declaration of Alma-Ata issued by the WHO International Conference on Primary Health Care in 1978. The declaration reaffirmed the WHO's holistic definition of health as "a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being, and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity." It followed that "existing gross inequality in the health status of the people" is unacceptable, that people have the right to participate in the organisation and implementation of health care, and that primary care should be universal.

> The first hospital-based Department of Social Medicine in the United States was founded at Montefiore in 1950. Martin Cherkasky was its first chair, later succeeded by George Silver and one of the authors of this essay. Members of Montefiore's Department of Family and Social Medicine and of the Albert Einstein College of Medicine — including two authors of this essay, Matt Anderson and Lanny Smith — established a website that offers information on social medicine and links to related sites.

**中文翻译：** 因此，社区导向的初级保健包含了基于社会医学准则的革新。临床保健是一个团队的责任：一名基层医生、一名社区护士、一名健康教育者（社区代表）。这个团队服务于他们熟悉的邻近家庭，把「病人」当成家人而不是孤立个体。此外，不像传统家庭医生，这个团队会系统考虑个体患者更广的社会环境意义以及新诊断的流行病学含义。

二十世纪六十年代，美国经济机会办公室首先在美国建立了两个社区健康中心：一个在波士顿哥伦比亚角，一个在密西西比河畔的 Mound Bayou。前者由曾在南非与 Sidney Kark 合作过的 Jack Geiger 及其同僚建立。议会最终建立了全国社区卫生服务中心网络，至今仍为美国「三分天下」中需要服务的人群提供健康服务。本文两位作者（马特·安德森和兰尼·史密斯）即在这些中心执业。社区健康运动的许多理念体现于 1978 年世界卫生组织国际初级卫生保健会议发出的《阿拉木图宣言》。该宣言重申世卫组织对健康的整体定义：「一种身体上、精神上和社会适应上的完好状态，而不仅仅是没有疾病和虚弱。」由此可推出：现有卫生上的严重不平等不可接受，人人有权参与卫生保健的组织与实施，初级保健应当全民普及。

美国第一个基于医院的社会医学部于 1950 年成立于蒙蒂菲奥里，Martin Cherkasky 任首任主席，其后是 George Silver 和本文作者之一。蒙蒂菲奥里家庭与社会医学部以及爱因斯坦医学院的成员——包括本文两位作者 Matt Anderson 与 Lanny Smith——建立了一个提供社会医学信息及链接的网站。

**讲解：** 社会手段的具体例子，课件 after-reading 答案就写：两个社区健康中心、社区健康运动、医院社会医学部、网站。Alma-Ata 1978 与 WHO 健康定义是翻译题常客。not merely the absence of disease or infirmity 要能默写。

### 段落 16–19 · 皇帝的新衣，与上西里西亚的药方

> Does social medicine affect medical practice today? Sometimes it is useful to state the obvious. Two decades of "market reform" in U.S. health care have not given all Americans affordable, quality health care, nor is it likely to do so. This emperor has no clothes. HIV treatment for all who need it could be supplied for a tiny fraction of what the U.S. government has spent to pursue Saddam Hussein and his nonexistent weapons of mass destruction. This is a political problem in essence. So what can we do? Clinicians know the lives of their patients intimately and thus are uniquely suited to understand the political and social dimensions of their patients' problems. Virchow put it compactly: physicians are the natural attorneys of the poor. We have contemporary examples of physicians taking up this challenge. It is this mission that has been recognised by the Nobel Peace Prize to Médecins Sans Frontières (Doctors without Borders) in 1999 and to International Physicians for the Prevention of Nuclear War in 1985.

> Those who know the history of social medicine know that for U.S. health problems, more doctors, more drugs, more quality measures, more computers, more audits, and faster discharges will not be enough. A fundamental rethinking of the social role of medicine is required. Progressive physicians who make the social nature of medicine explicit can serve as examples. Virchow's prescription for the typhus epidemic in Upper Silesia is more pertinent than ever. The logical answer to the question of how conditions similar to those unfolded before our eyes in Upper Silesia can be prevented in the future is very easy and simple: education, with its daughters liberty and prosperity.

**中文翻译：** 社会医学是否影响到现今的医疗实践？有时把显而易见的事情说出来是有用的。美国卫生保健二十年的「市场改革」并没有给所有美国人提供可负担、有质量的医疗，也不可能做到。这个皇帝没有穿衣服。若把美国政府花在追捕萨达姆·侯赛因及其并不存在的大规模杀伤性武器上的钱拿出一小部分，就足以供给所有需要者的 HIV 治疗。这本质上是政治问题。那么我们能做什么？临床医生更熟悉病人的生活，因而独特地适于理解病人问题的政治与社会维度。魏尔肖简洁地指出：医生是穷人天然的辩护人。当代也有医生接受这一挑战的例子。正是这一使命，在 1999 年被授予无国界医生组织、在 1985 年被授予国际防止核战争医生组织的诺贝尔和平奖所承认。

熟悉社会医学历史的人都知道，对于美国的医疗问题，再多的医生、药品、质控指标、计算机、审计、再快的出院，也解决不了。必须从根本上反思医学的社会角色。那些把医学的社会性说清楚的进步医生可以充当榜样。魏尔肖对上西里西亚斑疹伤寒流行的药方比以往更恰当。对于以后如何防止与我们眼前这幅「上西里西亚」相似的情形，一个符合逻辑而且简单的答案是：教育，以及她的女儿——自由与繁荣。

**讲解：** This emperor has no clothes 是 allegory，课件明确：借安徒生讽喻市场医疗之失败。It is this mission that... 是强调句。daughters 的先行词是 education：自由与繁荣是教育的女儿，不是「另外两味药」。上西里西亚 = Upper Silesia，Virchow 1848 调查地。

## 四、长难句

:::derivation{label="长难句 1 · 可及性交给保险业"}
Rather than moving toward a system of universal access to medical care in the United States, the access to and quality of clinical services is being turned over increasingly to the insurance industry.

**结构：** Rather than + doing 作状语，主句 access to and quality of ... is being turned over to ...。主语是复合名词短语，不是 the United States。
:::

:::derivation{label="长难句 2 · 大写的政治，小写的医学"}
Indeed, if politics is medicine writ large, it is also apparent that medicine is politics writ small.

**结构：** if 从句用 writ large（显而易见地放大）；主句 it is apparent that 用 writ small。课件译文：如果政治是大写的医疗，那么医疗则是小写的政治。
:::

:::keypoint{label="本课必须能默写的账"}
- 三条原则：社会条件；人群健康是社会事务；个体+社会手段。
- Virchow：细胞病理 + 社会起源；1848 斑疹伤寒；artificial epidemics；医生是穷人的辩护人。
- 专利 trump 公共卫生；鸡尾酒疗法约每年一万美元。
- Alma-Ata 1978：完好状态，不仅仅是没有疾病和虚弱。
- 皇帝没穿衣服 = 市场改革失败。
- 药方：education, with its daughters liberty and prosperity.
:::
