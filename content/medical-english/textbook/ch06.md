# Lesson 6 · Gender, climate change and health

本课 Text A 摘自世界卫生组织关于**性别、气候变化与健康**的报告。论点不是「气候有害健康」这一句空话，而是：极端天气对男女的负面健康后果**不成比例**，差异与灾害严重程度成正比，与妇女社会经济地位成反比。李梅英课件把课文分成四块：自然灾害的性别后果（段 1–2）→ 变暖与潮湿（段 3–5）→ 洪水暴雨（段 6–8）→ 干旱与公平（段 9–15）。词根表同时打开 schisto-、-iasis、gastr/o、enter/o、vaso- 这组「寄生虫病 + 胃肠 + 血管」零件。

:::insight{label="先锁性别，再锁气候"}
如果只读成「天热、发洪水、人会病」，男女性别差异、取水做饭拾柴的劳动分工、社会隔离的老年男性，全部会从账上消失。WHO 的分析单位是 **gendered health consequences**，不是气象站读数。
:::

:::definition{label="climate vs weather"}
课件强调：weather 是某一地点某一时刻的大气状况；climate 是通常以约 30 年为尺度的天气型式。热浪是天气事件，热浪变多变强才是气候变化的健康入口。
:::

## 二、核心词汇

:::derivation{label="aftermath /ˈɑːftəmæθ/ n."}
**书本义：** [C, 常 the aftermath]（战争、风暴、事故之后的）余波；善后时期

**词形：** 无动词用法作「余波」；immediate aftermath 指紧随其后的阶段。

**常用搭配：**
- in the aftermath of a disaster / flood 在灾害/洪水余波中
- immediate aftermath 直接余波
- typhoon aftermath 台风善后

**例句：**
1. The same study looked at mortality in natural disasters and their aftermath.（同一研究观察了自然灾害及其余波中的死亡率。）
2. Women often carry extra emotional labour in the aftermath of flooding.（洪水过后，妇女常常承担额外的情感劳动。）

✍️ Public-health planning that stops at the peak of a storm and ignores the aftermath will miss most of the gendered harm.（只规划风暴高峰、不管余波的公共卫生计划，会漏掉大部分有性别差异的伤害。）
:::

:::derivation{label="mortality /mɔːˈtæləti/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 死亡；死亡率；（某一群体在某时期的）死亡数

**词形：** mortal（a. 终有一死的）→ mortality；infant / neonatal / maternal mortality 婴儿/新生儿/孕产妇死亡率。

**常用搭配：**
- mortality rate 死亡率
- heatwave-related mortality 热浪相关死亡
- infant mortality 婴儿死亡

**例句：**
1. Natural disasters actually narrow the life-expectancy gap because they reduce women's life expectancy more than men's.（自然灾害实际上缩小预期寿命的性别差距，因为它们使女性预期寿命下降得比男性更多。）
2. Heatwave mortality among elderly men in Chicago was linked to social isolation.（芝加哥老年男性的热浪死亡与社会隔离有关。）

✍️ Mortality is a count of deaths; vulnerability is the reason some groups die first.（mortality 是死亡计数；vulnerability 才是某些群体先死的原因。）
:::

:::derivation{label="vulnerability /ˌvʌlnərəˈbɪləti/ n."}
**书本义：** [U, C] 脆弱性；易损性；无法承受敌对环境作用的程度

**词形：** vulnerable（a.）→ vulnerability；be vulnerable to 易受……伤害。

**常用搭配：**
- social vulnerability 社会脆弱性
- gender-specific vulnerability 性别特异性脆弱
- be vulnerable to diabetes / heatstroke 易患糖尿病/中暑

**例句：**
1. People with high blood pressure are especially vulnerable to diabetes.（高血压患者尤其易患糖尿病。——课件例）
2. Recognition of different vulnerabilities and strengths of both genders is indispensable for equity.（承认两性不同的脆弱与优势，是实现公平不可或缺的一步。）

✍️ Vulnerability is not a personality trait of women; it is produced by labour, mobility, water, fuel and political status.（脆弱不是女性的性格特征；它由劳动、行动能力、水、燃料和政治地位生产出来。）
:::

:::callout{kind="pitfall" label="vulnerable 后面是 to，不是 of"}
课件固定搭配 be vulnerable to。说 \*vulnerable of heat 是中式英语。名词 vulnerability analysis / scan 是公共卫生与信息安全共用的术语，课文里走健康账。
:::

:::derivation{label="contiguous /kənˈtɪɡjuəs/ a."}
**书本义：** 毗连的；相邻的（空间上挨着）

**词形：** contiguity（n.）；contiguously（ad.）；近义 adjacent，反义 distant / unconnected。

**常用搭配：**
- contiguous countries / areas 毗连的国家/地区
- spatial contiguity 空间毗连
- Viet Nam is contiguous to China 越南与中国接壤（课件例）

**例句：**
1. The resort's main claim to fame is that it is the largest contiguous ski area in Austria.（课件填空：最大的连片滑雪区。）
2. Appendicitis may spread by contiguity to adjacent organs.（阑尾炎可经毗连扩散到邻近器官。）

✍️ Contiguous describes space, not friendship: two wards can be contiguous and still unequal in water and fuel.（contiguous 描述空间不是友谊：两个社区可以毗连，水和燃料仍可不平等。）
:::

:::derivation{label="precipitation /prɪˌsɪpɪˈteɪʃn/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 降水（雨、雪等）；降水量

**词形：** 气象义与化学「沉淀」同形；课文是气象义。annual precipitation 年降水量。

**常用搭配：**
- high precipitation 强降水
- precipitation intensity 降水强度
- declining precipitation 降水减少

**例句：**
1. Incidence of eclampsia rose in weather characterised by low temperature, high humidity or high precipitation.（以低温、高湿或强降水为特征的气象条件下，子痫发病率升高。）
2. Many glaciers are retreating amid rising temperatures and declining precipitation.（气温上升、降水减少，许多冰川在退缩。——课件例）

✍️ In this unit precipitation is rain and snow, not a chemical solid at the bottom of a test tube.（本课 precipitation 是雨雪，不是试管底的沉淀。）
:::

:::derivation{label="eclampsia /ɪˈklæmpsiə/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 子痫（妊娠末三月可出现的中毒状态：高血压、异常体重增加、抽搐）

**词形：** pre-eclampsia 先兆子痫；病因课件写 unknown cause。

**常用搭配：**
- incidence of eclampsia in pregnancy 妊娠子痫发病率
- hypertensive complications during pregnancy 孕期高血压并发症

**例句：**
1. A Kuwait study found pregnancy-induced hypertension highest in June, when temperature was high and humidity lowest.（科威特研究发现妊娠高血压在气温高、湿度最低的六月最高。）
2. In contrast, true incidence of pre-eclampsia rose in November under low temperature and high humidity.（相反，先兆子痫真正发病率在低温高湿的十一月升高。）

✍️ Do not collapse eclampsia and pre-eclampsia into "pregnancy hypertension"; the textbook keeps them as related but timed differently against humidity.（不要把子痫和先兆子痫并成一句「怀孕高血压」；教材把它们与湿度的时间关系分开写。）
:::

:::derivation{label="exacerbate /ɪɡˈzæsəbeɪt/ vt."}
**书本义：** 使恶化；加剧

**词形：** exacerbation（n.）；近义 worsen，反义 alleviate。

**常用搭配：**
- exacerbate undernutrition / global warming 加剧营养不良/全球变暖
- exacerbate respiratory symptoms 加重呼吸道症状

**例句：**
1. A 2°C rise may cause 5–20% reductions of cereal grain yields, significantly exacerbating undernutrition.（气温升高 2°C 可能使谷物减产 5–20%，显著加剧营养不良。）
2. Biomass fuels increase COPD risk while simultaneously exacerbating global warming.（生物燃料增加 COPD 风险，同时加剧全球变暖。）

✍️ Exacerbate takes a problem as object, not a person: we exacerbate drought impacts, we do not "exacerbate women".（exacerbate 的宾语是问题不是人：我们加剧干旱影响，不说「加剧妇女」。）
:::

:::derivation{label="proliferate /prəˈlɪfəreɪt/ vi."}
**书本义：** 增殖；扩散；（细菌、细胞）大量繁殖

**词形：** proliferation（n.）；proliferative（a.）

**常用搭配：**
- bacterial proliferation in shellfish 贝类中的细菌增殖
- cells proliferate in vitro 细胞体外增殖（课件填空）

**例句：**
1. A 1°C rise in coastal Alaska water temperature allowed summer-long bacterial proliferation in shellfish and increased gastroenteritis among consumers.（阿拉斯加沿岸水温升高 1°C，使贝类整个夏天细菌增殖，食客肠胃炎增加。）
2. They can be isolated from bone marrow and have the capacity to extensively proliferate in vitro.（它们可从骨髓分离，并能在体外广泛增殖。）

✍️ In climate-health writing, proliferate is for organisms and risks, not for "ideas" unless you are being metaphorical on purpose.（气候—健康写作里，proliferate 给生物和风险用；给「想法」用要有明确比喻。）
:::

:::derivation{label="equity /ˈekwəti/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 公平；公正（本课与 social justice 并列，不是公司股权）

**词形：** equitable（a.）；gender equity 性别公平。

**常用搭配：**
- equity and social justice 公平与社会正义
- gender-responsive health systems 对性别有回应的卫生系统

**例句：**
1. Equity cannot be achieved without recognising different vulnerabilities and strengths of both genders.（不承认两性不同的脆弱与优势，就不可能实现公平。）
2. We need a gender-equal, accessible health system that reaches the poorest.（我们需要覆盖最贫困人群的、两性平等而可及的卫生系统。）

✍️ Equity here is not "treat everyone identically"; it is respond to different labour, water and political positions.（这里的 equity 不是人人同一套；而是回应不同的劳动、水和政治位置。）
:::

### 段落 1 · 潘基文题记

> "From the health of the global economy to the health of our children, climate change affects every aspect of society. It is about our wells and our taps, about the food on our tables, and about the core of the major challenges we face almost every day." — Ban Ki-moon, UN Secretary-General

**中文翻译：** 「从全球经济的健康到我们孩子的健康，气候变化影响着社会的各个方面。它关系到我们的井水和自来水，也关系到餐桌上的饭菜，以及我们几乎每天都要面对的主要挑战的核心。」——联合国秘书长潘基文

**讲解：** wells and taps 把气候做成饮用水问题，food on our tables 做成营养问题，后面减产、干旱、取水都从这里长出来。题记不是装饰。

### 段落 2 · 性别差异的两条比例

> There is evidence that men and women experience different negative health consequences when facing extreme events such as floods, droughts and heatwaves. While disasters bring suffering to everyone, natural disasters generally kill more women, or kill women at a younger age than men. These differences are in proportion to the severity of the disasters and are also related to women's relative socio-economic status in the affected countries. The effects are strongest in countries where women's socio-economic and political status is low. In countries where women and men have similar status, natural disasters affect men and women almost equally.

**中文翻译：** 有证据表明，面对洪水、干旱和热浪等极端事件时，男女受到的负面健康影响不同。灾难给每个人带来苦难，但自然灾害一般会造成更多女性死亡，或使女性的死亡年龄小于男性。这些差异与灾难的严重程度成比例，也与妇女在受灾国家相对的社会经济地位相关。在妇女社会经济和政治地位很低的国家，这种影响最强烈；在妇女与男子地位相近的国家，自然灾害对男女的影响几乎相同。

**讲解：** in proportion to severity / inversely related to socio-economic status 是全文定律。课件填空题反复打这条。almost equally 是对照实验：地位相近，死亡率性别差就收窄——所以差异不是「女人身体更弱」一句话能解释的。

### 段落 3 · 147 国研究：社会规范，不是生理差

> A study based on information from 147 countries has highlighted that physical differences between men and women are unlikely to explain these differences and social norms may provide some additional explanation. The same study, looking at mortality in natural disasters and their aftermath, found gender-specific vulnerabilities of girls and adult women: natural disasters reduced women's life expectancy more than men's. Because women generally live longer than men, disasters actually narrowed the life-expectancy gap in most countries. The effect on the current gender gap in life expectancy was proportional to disaster severity: major disasters hit women's life expectancy harder (relative to men). The study also confirmed that this effect was inversely related to women's socio-economic status. It emphasised the socially constructed, gender-specific vulnerability of women to natural disasters, which is part of everyday socio-economic patterns and produces higher disaster-related mortality in women than in men.

**中文翻译：** 一项基于 147 个国家资料的研究指出，男女生理差异无法解释上述不同，社会规范或可提供补充解释。同一研究通过观察自然灾害及其余波中的死亡率，发现女童和成年女性特有的弱势：自然灾害使女性预期寿命下降得比男性更多。由于女性预期寿命通常高于男性，自然灾害实际上缩小了大多数国家的男女预期寿命差距。对当前预期寿命性别差距的影响与灾难严重程度成比例：重大灾难对女性预期寿命的打击（相对男性）更重。该研究还证实，这一影响与妇女社会经济地位呈负相关。它强调女性对自然灾害的社会构建性、性别特异性脆弱，而这正是日常社会经济模式的一部分，并导致灾难相关的女性死亡率高于男性。

**讲解：** 147 countries 是可考细节。physical differences unlikely / social norms 是作者立场。narrowed the gap 容易读反：不是女性更长寿了，是女性多死、优势被削掉。socially constructed 把脆弱从生理搬到劳动与权利。

:::callout{kind="pitfall" label="缩小寿命差距不是好消息"}
寿命差距缩小，在这里是灾难杀死更多女性的数学结果，不是性别平等改善。选项若写成「自然灾害促进男女平等」，属于偷换概念。
:::

### 段落 4–5 · 变暖与潮湿：粮食、热浪、钉螺、肠胃炎

> Many specific impacts of a warmer and wetter climate can be anticipated or, in some cases, already observed. Modelling studies indicate that a temperature rise of 2°C may cause 5–20% reductions of cereal grain yields in South Asia, South-East Asia and sub-Saharan Africa, significantly exacerbating undernutrition and adverse health outcomes, especially in children's physical and mental development. In many urban populations, higher temperatures are estimated to raise the annual death rate from heatwaves about twofold. In China, a 2°C temperature rise would allow a 50–100% increase in the geographic distribution of water snails, and hence the potential transmission of schistosomiasis, endangering many tens of millions of people. Recent experience from coastal Alaska shows that a rise in water temperature beyond a critical threshold permits summer-long bacterial proliferation in shellfish and increases the incidence of gastroenteritis among consumers.

**中文翻译：** 更暖、更湿气候的许多具体影响可以被预期，某些情况下已经被观察到。模型研究指出，气温升高 2°C 可能使南亚、东南亚和撒哈拉以南非洲谷物产量减少 5–20%，显著加剧营养不良和不良健康结局（尤其是儿童身心发育）。在许多城市人口中，气温升高估计会使热浪造成的年死亡率大约升高两倍。在中国，气温升高 2°C 会使钉螺地理分布增加 50–100%，由此带来的血吸虫潜在传播将危及数千万人。阿拉斯加沿海的近期经验显示，水温升高超过关键阈值，会使贝类整个夏天细菌增殖，从而增加食客肠胃炎发生率。

**讲解：** 数字课要能对上：2°C ↔ 5–20% 减产；热浪死亡约两倍；中国钉螺分布 50–100%；阿拉斯加 1°C 水温（课件另条 modelling studies）。schistosomiasis 的中间宿主是 water snails，不是「中国人更容易得寄生虫」的种族叙事。endangering 是现在分词作结果状语。

:::keypoint{label="变暖三条健康链"}
- 热 → 谷物减产 → 儿童营养不良
- 热 → 城市热浪死亡约翻倍（后文再拆男女）
- 热 → 钉螺扩张 / 贝类细菌增殖 → 血吸虫病、肠胃炎
:::

### 段落 5 续 · 热浪里的女性和男性

> In the 2003 European heatwave, female mortality was higher than male mortality. Most European studies show that, both relatively and absolutely, women are at higher risk of dying in a heatwave than men. There may be physiological reasons why older women are at greater risk. Social factors, however, are important determinants of negative heatwave outcomes. In the United States, for example, elderly men appear more at risk than women — a pattern especially visible in the Chicago event of July 1995. This vulnerability may stem from the social isolation of elderly men. Evidence for social contact as a protective factor against heatwave mortality comes from case-control studies in the United States. In Paris, unmarried men had higher heatwave-related risk than married men. Because men are more likely to be active in hot weather, they may also face a greater risk of heatstroke mortality.

**中文翻译：** 2003 年欧洲热浪中女性死亡率高于男性。欧洲大部分研究显示，无论相对还是绝对，女性死于热浪的风险高于男性。可能有一些生理原因解释年纪较大的女性风险更大。但社会因素在热浪负面后果中起重要决定作用。例如在美国，年老男性在热浪中似乎比女性更危险，这一点在 1995 年 7 月芝加哥事件中尤为明显。这一弱势可能源于年老男性的社会隔离。社会联系作为降低热浪死亡率保护因素的证据，来自美国的病例对照研究。在法国巴黎，未婚男性的热浪相关风险高于已婚男性。由于男性更可能在炎热天气中活动，这也许使其中暑死亡风险更大。

**讲解：** 不要背成「热浪一定女死得多」。欧洲女高、芝加哥老年男高，开关是 isolation / marital status / activity，不是染色体。case-control study 是本课方法学术语。

### 段落 5 续 · 子痫与气象

> Some studies have examined the link between meteorological conditions and the incidence of eclampsia in pregnancy, and found higher incidence in weather characterised by low temperature, high humidity or high precipitation, especially in the first months of the rainy season. A study in Kuwait found the incidence of pregnancy-induced hypertension highest in June, when temperature was high and humidity lowest. In contrast, the true incidence of pre-eclampsia rose in November under low temperature and high humidity. Another study in a southern province of Zimbabwe observed a distinctive change in pre-eclampsia incidence that matched seasonal precipitation: higher at the end of the dry season and in the first months of the rainy season. The observed relation between climate and pre-eclampsia raises new questions about its pathophysiology. One explanation is the effect of humidity and temperature on the production of vasoactive substances. Dry and rainy seasons may also affect nutritional status through agricultural yields and thus play a role in women's pathophysiology.

**中文翻译：** 一些研究关注气象条件与妊娠子痫发病率的联系，发现在低温、高湿或强降水条件下发病率升高，雨季前几个月尤为明显。科威特一项研究发现妊娠高血压发病率在气温很高而湿度最低的六月最高。与之相反，先兆子痫真正发病率在低温高湿的十一月升高。津巴布韦南部省份的另一项研究评估孕期高血压并发症，并观察到那一年先兆子痫发病率的独特改变，与降水季节变化相符：旱季末期和雨季首月升高。气候变化与先兆子痫的关系引出病理生理的新问题。一种解释是湿度和气温作用于血管活性物质的生成。旱季和雨季还可能通过对农业产量的影响改变营养状况，从而进入女性的病理生理。

**讲解：** Kuwait June vs November 是对照，不要合成「热就子痫」。vasoactive substances 把 vas/o 词根用进病理。营养是第三条通路：气候 → 产量 → 孕妇病理。

### 段落 6–8 · 洪水：孟加拉与英格兰

> Flooding is one of the most widespread climatic hazards that pose multiple risks to human health, yet there has been only limited systematic research on the health outcomes of flooding. From the few available studies it is already clear that the indirect effects of flooding on women can have serious negative consequences for their health and social lives. Far less is known about the effects of flooding on men.

**中文翻译：** 洪水是最普遍的气候灾害之一，对人类健康构成多种威胁。然而目前关于洪水健康影响的系统研究仍然有限。从仅有的研究已可看出，洪水对妇女的间接影响会严重损害她们的健康与社会生活。人们对洪水给男性带来的影响还知之甚少。

**讲解：** pose risks to 是课件搭配。limited systematic research 与「更了解女性、更不了解男性」同时成立——不是数据已经够了。

> In southwestern Bangladesh, waterlogging (a rise in the local water table) has become an urgent health problem. Women are often the family's main caregivers, shouldering the work of managing and cooking food, collecting drinking water, and taking care of household members and livestock. Because of these duties they frequently spend time in flooded homesteads and other premises. Studies show that waterlogging seriously affects the health of women in inundated communities. Tube wells are often contaminated, and women are forced to remain in the affected community and drink unsafe water. Pregnant women, whose mobility is limited in isolated and slippery environments, are often forced to stay indoors. Local health workers have reported a rising trend of gynaecological problems linked to the use of unclean water. Because men often leave the area to look for work, they are usually not affected in the same way as their female relatives. Waterlogging has thus produced different health impacts for women and men in coastal Bangladesh.

**中文翻译：** 在孟加拉国西南地区，内涝（当地地下水位上升）因其健康后果已成为迫切问题。妇女往往是家庭的主要照顾者，肩负管理和烹饪食物、收集饮用水、照顾家庭成员和牲畜的重任。因为这些职责，她们经常花时间在被水浸的房舍和其他处所。研究表明，内涝严重影响受淹社区妇女的健康。管井常被污染，妇女被迫留在受灾社区饮用不卫生的水。孕妇在孤立、湿滑环境中行动不便，往往被迫留在室内。当地医护人员已报道，由于使用不洁水，妇科问题呈增多趋势。男性往往走出这片地区去寻找工作，通常并不像他们的女性家人那样受影响。因此内涝已在孟加拉沿海地区的男女之间造成不同的健康影响。

**讲解：** shouldering, collecting, taking care of 是课件点名的并列结构。premises 在本课是「处所、房舍」，不是「前提」（那个是单数 premise）。gynaecological problems 从 unclean water 来，不是「女性体质」。

> Research on the health effects of flooding in England found that women suffered significantly more than men during the worst period of flooding. Qualitative research suggested this was because women bear more responsibility for the whole household than men, may invest more emotionally, and usually have primary responsibility for children and older relatives, while also trying to restore the household to its pre-flood state. Women also experienced more stress, anxiety and depression than men, and were more likely to seek medical help after the disaster.

**中文翻译：** 关于英格兰洪水对健康影响的研究发现，妇女在洪水最严重时期承受的痛苦明显多于男性。定性研究表明，这是因为妇女对整个家庭比男性负有更主要的责任，可能投入更多感情，通常对照顾孩子和长辈负有更主要责任，同时还得设法让家庭回到洪水之前的正常状态。妇女还比男性承受更多紧张、焦虑和沮丧，并在灾后更多寻求医疗帮助。

**讲解：** qualitative research 是方法学标签。England 与 Bangladesh 不是重复例子：一个是低收入内涝与不洁水，一个是高收入国家里的照料负担与心理症状。共同点是劳动的性别分工。

### 段落 9–14 · 干旱、生物燃料、男性自杀

> The effects of climate change on water systems are already evident worldwide, and even after future agreements to cut greenhouse-gas emissions they will still intensify for several decades. Drought brings, in varying degrees, social unrest, food insecurity and lasting health problems; it can damage or destroy related livelihoods. In arid, semi-arid and sub-humid regions, drought already threatens well-being and health. It is also associated with shortages of fuel, food and water, with conflict, mass migration, deepening poverty, more fires, difficulty obtaining fuel, and restricted access to health care.

**中文翻译：** 气候变化对水循环系统的影响在世界各地已非常明显；即使将来达成控制温室气体排放的协议，仍会有几十年的加速影响。干旱在不同程度上带来社会动荡、食品不安全和长久的健康问题，损坏甚至摧毁相关生计。在干旱、半干旱、半湿润地区，干旱已威胁当地人口的幸福感与健康。此外，干旱还与燃料、食品、水资源短缺、冲突、大规模迁徙、贫困加剧、火灾增加、燃料获取困难及卫生保健受限有关。

**讲解：** 课件原句几乎原封：in spite of future agreements ... within several decades。协议不是开关，滞后几十年。

> Few available studies point to different impacts of drought on men and women. Water scarcity is associated with rising cholera and diarrhoea, especially among children and the elderly, because people often sacrifice hygiene to keep enough water for drinking and cooking. This includes a rise in diarrhoeal disease — a leading cause of child death in less developed regions. In cities of Africa, Asia and Latin America, nearly half of residents have become victims of diseases brought by lack of water and sanitation.

> A study of drought management in Ninh Thuan, Viet Nam, found that a large share of respondents believed recurrent disasters affected men and women differently, and that women were more severely affected because of different water needs. With each drought, women had to fetch water from farther sources. They also cooked, cleaned, cared for children and collected firewood, so that daily physical labour became enormous. Fuel shortages increase women's workload where they are responsible for its collection; an estimated 2.4 billion people currently rely on biomass fuels for cooking and heating, impacting health by increasing the risk of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) and increasing respiratory symptoms, as well as worsening lung function, while simultaneously exacerbating global warming.

**中文翻译：** 少数可及的研究指出了干旱对男女的不同影响。缺水与霍乱和腹泻发生率上升相关，在儿童与老年人中尤为突出，因为人们通常牺牲卫生习惯来保证饮用和烹饪用水。这包括腹泻疾病上升——欠发达地区儿童死亡的一个主要原因。在非洲、亚洲和拉丁美洲的城市，将近半数居民已成为水资源和公共卫生设施匮乏所带来疾病的受害者。

越南宁顺一项干旱管理研究显示，相当比例的受访者认为反复发生的灾难对男女影响有差别，女性因对水的不同需求而受影响更严重。每次干旱造成损失后，女性得从更远的水源取水。她们同时要做饭、打扫卫生、带孩子和拾柴，每天承受庞大的体力劳动。燃料短缺增加了负责拾柴妇女的工作量；目前约 24 亿人依赖生物燃料烹调与取暖，这在加剧全球变暖的同时，大大增加慢性阻塞性肺疾病（COPD）风险，增多呼吸道症状，并损害肺功能。

**讲解：** 2.4 billion 与 COPD 是翻译和完形的高频点。课件中文有一处写成「慢性阻塞性肺气肿」，英文是 COPD，考试写慢性阻塞性肺疾病。semicolon 连接「工作量」与「24 亿人」两层。

> The stress of lost income and related debt escalates into a range of mental-health problems, despair and suicide. Empirical evidence points to a link between drought and suicide among men in Australia. These negative health impacts on Australian farmers have been associated with rural masculine norms of stoicism and health-damaging behaviour. Similar patterns have been reported in India, with a rise in suicides after drought in persistently semi-arid regions.

**中文翻译：** 失去收入和相关债务的压力升级为一系列心理健康问题、绝望和自杀。有经验证据指出，澳大利亚男性中干旱与自杀之间存在联系。对澳大利亚农民的这种负面健康影响，被认为与乡村男性的隐忍（禁欲主义式的 stoicism）及损害健康的行为有关。印度也有同样情形：持续半干旱地区干旱期过后自杀案件增多。

**讲解：** 课文不是「只有女性受害」。干旱账上，取水拾柴走女性，自杀走部分农村男性。stoicism 在这里是有害的性别规范，不是美德贴纸。

### 段落 15 · 公平与社会正义

> Equity and social justice cannot be achieved without recognising the different vulnerabilities and strengths of women and men, and the many other factors that produce vulnerability. Such recognition is an indispensable part of any attempt to address the different health impacts of environmental change on women and men. Gender-sensitive research is necessary for a better overall understanding of climate change, especially the health effects of extreme events. We urgently need to collect, analyse and report data disaggregated by age and sex, and to use other stratifiers that allow thorough gender analysis. We need to build gender-equal, accessible health systems that reach the poorest, so as to address the particular health needs of women and men across the life course.

**中文翻译：** 公平和社会正义，在不承认男女之间强弱差别及其他造成弱势的众多因素的前提下，是不可能实现的。承认这些差别，是应对环境变化对男女不同健康影响时必不可少的组成。性别敏感的研究，对于从整体上理解气候变化、特别是极端事件的健康影响，十分必要。我们迫切需要收集、分析和报告按年龄和性别分列的数据，并采用允许彻底性别分析的其他分层。我们需要建立两性平等、便利、能够覆盖最穷人群的卫生系统，以解决妇女和男子整个生命周期中的特别健康需求。

**讲解：** disaggregated by age and sex 是政策句，翻译题常考。accessible 在这里是卫生系统可及，不是「容易懂的课文」。life course 对应「整个生命周期」。

## 四、长难句

:::derivation{label="长难句 1 · 钉螺与血吸虫"}
In China, a 2°C temperature rise would allow a 50–100% increase in the geographic distribution of water snails, and hence the potential transmission of schistosomiasis, endangering many tens of millions of people.

**结构：** 条件名词短语 a 2°C temperature rise 作主语；would allow A, and hence B；endangering 现在分词状语给出人群后果。many tens of millions of = 数以千万计。
:::

:::derivation{label="长难句 2 · 生物燃料与 COPD"}
Fuel shortages increase women's workload where they are responsible for its collection; an estimated 2.4 billion people currently rely on biomass fuels for cooking and heating, impacting health by increasing the risk of COPD and increasing respiratory symptoms, as well as worsening lung function while simultaneously exacerbating global warming.

**结构：** 分号连接两句。where 引导地点/情境状语从句；impacting ... by increasing A and increasing B, as well as worsening C while simultaneously exacerbating D。一条行为（烧生物燃料）同时打健康和气候两本账。
:::

:::keypoint{label="本课必须能默写的账"}
- 差异 ∝ 灾害严重程度；差异 ∝ 1/妇女社会经济地位。
- 147 国：生理差解释不了，社会规范可以。
- 2°C → 谷物 5–20% 减产；中国钉螺分布 50–100%；热浪死亡约×2。
- 欧洲热浪女高；芝加哥 1995 老年男高（隔离）。
- 孟加拉内涝：妇女取水做饭、不洁水、妇科问题；男人外出打工。
- 24 亿人生物燃料 → COPD；澳大利亚/印度干旱后男性自杀。
- 对策：分年龄性别数据 + 对性别有回应的可及卫生系统。
:::
