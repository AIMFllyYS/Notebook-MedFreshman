# Lesson 1 · Medical Professionalism / 词根入门

医学英语不是把普通英语再背一遍，而是两套并行的本领：一套是**词根构词**，让你看见 `peri/cardi/itis` 就能拆出「心包炎」；一套是**职业伦理话语**，让你读懂希波克拉底誓言如何从古代理想变成现代执业规范。本课 Text A 是 *Hippocratic Oath, The Medical Ideal*（希波克拉底誓言：医学的理想）。词根课同时打开人体器官 combining form 与 `-itis / -oma / -ectomy` 这组「炎症—肿瘤—切除」后缀。

:::insight{label="为什么医学英语从誓言和词根一起学"}
誓言教的是医师对谁负责、什么绝对不能做；词根教的是病名怎么长出来。两者都是「看见零件就能还原整词/整句」。把 oath 只听成毕业仪式、把 cardi/o 只听成「心的形容词」，后面的 Nuremberg Code、triage、euthanasia 和 endocarditis 都会对不上户头。
:::

---

## 一、医学英语词根入门

现行医学术语绝大多数由希腊、拉丁词素构成，同一词素在不同词里保持同一意义。学会词素及其拼接规则，等于拿到一把开整本医学词典的钥匙。

### （一）词的分析：prefix / root / combining vowel / suffix

课件用 `hypo/pharyng/o/scope` 演示拆词：

:::definition{label="hypopharyngoscope 的拆法"}
- **hypo-**：前缀，under / below / beneath（在下）
- **pharyng**：词根，pharynx（咽）
- **-o-**：combining vowel（连接元音），只起连音作用，本身无义
- **pharyng/o**：combining form（词根 + 连接元音）
- **-scope**：后缀，an instrument for viewing or observing（窥镜）

因而 hypopharyngoscope 指用来观察咽下部（hypopharynx）内部的器械。
:::

:::compare{title="八种基本构词模式"}
| Pattern | 公式 | 例词 |
|---------|------|------|
| 1 | Root | tendon 腱 |
| 2 | Root (+ combining vowel) + Suffix | hepat/ic 肝的；splen/o/pathy 脾病 |
| 3 | Root + combining vowel + Root | hemat/o/blast 成血细胞 |
| 4 | Root + combining vowel + Root + Suffix | ot/o/rhin/o/laryng/o/log/ist 耳鼻喉学家 |
| 5 | Prefix + Root (+ combining vowel) + Suffix | peri/cardi/um 心包；dys/men/o/rrhea 痛经 |
| 6 | Prefix + Root + combining vowel + Root + Suffix | intra/erythr/o/cyt/ic 红细胞内的 |
| 7 | Prefix + Prefix + Root (+ combining vowel) + Suffix | sub/peri/oste/al 骨膜下的；contra/in/dic/a/tion 禁忌证 |
| 8 | Prefix + Suffix | para/centesis 穿刺术 |
:::

:::keypoint{label="连接元音两条铁律"}
- 词根接到**以辅音开头的后缀**时保留连接元音：rhin/o/scopy 鼻镜检查。后缀若以**元音开头**，通常丢掉连接元音：mamm/itis 乳腺炎（不是 mammoitis）。
- **两个词根之间**即使第二个词根以元音开头，也常保留连接元音：gastr/o/enter/itis 胃肠炎；lymph/o/vascul/ar 淋巴管的。
- 连接元音以 **-o-** 最常见，**-i-** 次之（ven/i/suture 静脉缝合）。例外要记，不要用规则硬套：sial/aden 涎腺、enter/aden/itis 肠腺炎。
:::

:::callout{kind="pitfall" label="不要按零件重音去念整词"}
`peri/card/itis` 读 `/ˌperikɑːˈdaɪtɪs/`，重音在 `-itis`，不是把 peri、card、itis 三个零件各自重读再拼起来。课件强调：medical words 的重音和音节切分与词素切分不是同一本账。
:::

### （二）前缀、后缀与 competing forms

:::compare{title="否定/相反前缀"}
| 前缀 | 意义 | 例词 |
|------|------|------|
| a-, an- | not, without | amorphous 无定形的 |
| anti- | against | antimycotic 抗真菌的 |
| contra- | against, opposite | contraception 避孕 |
| de- | down, without | deoxygenate 脱氧 |
| dis- | absence, separation | dissect 解剖、分离 |
| in-/il-/im-/ir- | not | invertebrate 无脊椎的（课件拼作 invertibrate，考试写 invertebrate） |
| non- | not | noninfectious 非传染的 |
| un- | not | undifferentiated 未分化的 |
:::

:::compare{title="三类高频后缀"}
| 类别 | 后缀 | 例词 |
|------|------|------|
| 表示状态/病 | -ia, -iasis, -ism, -sis, -tion, -y | phobia；cholelithiasis 胆石症；embolism；genesis；digestion；tetany |
| 表示专科/专家 | -iatrics, -iatry, -ics, -logy, -er, -ian, -ist | geriatrics 老年医学；podiatry 足病学；orthopedics；psychology；radiographer；physician；dentist |
| 表示「……的」 | -ac, -al, -ar, -ary, -eal, -ic, -ical, -ory, -ous, -tic | cardiac；bronchial；tonsillar；ciliary；esophageal；hepatic；anatomical；respiratory；mucous；peptic |
:::

:::definition{label="competing forms 竞争形式"}
希腊、拉丁两套词源同时供词，加上构词容易，就会出现「同一意义、两种写法」：bronch/i/ectasis 与 bronch/o/dilator（同一 bronch-，连接元音不同）；sperm/i/cide 与 spermat/o/cele（同一词根的长短式）；dent/ist（拉丁）与 odont/o/logy（希腊）都指牙；uter/o/plasty 与 hyster/ectomy 都指子宫相关手术。看见两套不要当成两个器官。
:::

| 脑 | cerebr/o-；encephal/o- | cerebral；encephalitis |
:::

:::compare{title="-itis / -oma / -ectomy / -tomy"}
| 后缀 | 意义 | 读音（课件） | 例词 |
|------|------|--------------|------|
| -itis | 炎症 | 总是 /ˈaɪtɪs/ | endocarditis；enteritis；dermatitis |
| -oma | 肿瘤 | 总是 /ˈəʊmə/ | adenoma；encephaloma |
| -ectomy | 切除、摘除（excision） | — | nephrectomy；gastrectomy；appendectomy |
| -tomy | 切开（incision into） | — | cystotomy；laryngotomy |
| -stomy | 造口通到体外 | — | enterostomy；tracheostomy |
:::

:::callout{kind="pitfall" label="课件把 -ectomy 写成 Incision，那是笔误"}
词根 PPT 在 Digestive & Urinary 一页把 `-ectomy` 注释成 Incision，紧挨着的 `-tomy` 才是「切开」。户头要锁对：**-tomy = 切开，-ectomy = 切掉**。nephrectomy 是切肾，不是把肾切开看看。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="发音三点，考试会默写"}
- 复数词尾 **-i** 读 /aɪ/：alveoli、bronchi。
- 词首双辅音 **cn-/gn-/mn-/pn-/ps-/pt-** 第一个常不发音：pneumonia 读作 /njuː-/，不是 /pnjuː-/。
- **c** 在 e/i/y 前读 /s/（cervical、cyst），在 a/o/u 前读 /k/（catalyst、cuspid）。
:::

## 二、Text A 核心词汇

课文标题是 Hippocratic Oath, The Medical Ideal。下面按课文出场顺序收核心词。每个词卡只讲用法；易错点写在词卡**外面**，不要把 `:::` 再套进 derivation。

:::derivation{label="oath /oʊθ/ n."}
**书本义：** [C] 誓言；宣誓；正式承诺

**词形：** 复数 oaths /oʊðz/；动词 swear an oath（发誓），不说 \*make an oath 作法律/职业誓词。

**常用搭配：**
- Hippocratic Oath 希波克拉底誓言
- swear / take an oath 宣誓
- break / violate an oath 违背誓言
- an oath of confidentiality 保密誓言

**例句：**
1. Most graduating medical students swear to some form of the oath before they practise.（多数医学院毕业生在行医前会宣读某种形式的誓言。）
2. The original oath prohibits assisted euthanasia.（原始誓言禁止协助安乐死。）

✍️ Before I take the oath, I must understand that confidentiality is not a courtesy but a duty.（在宣誓之前，我必须明白保密不是礼貌，而是义务。）
:::

:::callout{kind="pitfall" label="oath 不是 slogan"}
oath 是有约束力的誓言，不是口号、也不是合同条款。课文后半强调现代版本往往不再「对着神灵发誓才准行医」，而变成 code of practice（执业守则）——约束力的形式变了，不是词义变成「建议」。
:::

:::derivation{label="enduring /ɪnˈdjʊərɪŋ/ a."}
**书本义：** 持久的；耐久的；经得起时间考验的

**词形：** endure（v. 忍受；持续）→ enduring（a.）→ endurance（n. 耐力）

**常用搭配：**
- the most enduring tradition 最持久的传统
- enduring influence / value 持久的影响/价值

**例句：**
1. Perhaps the most enduring tradition in the history of medicine is the Hippocratic Oath.（医学史上最持久的传统或许就是希波克拉底誓言。）
2. An enduring ethical standard is still needed in an age of technological enlightenment.（在技术启蒙的时代，仍然需要持久的伦理标准。）

✍️ An enduring professional identity is built less on prestige than on the daily decision not to cause intentional harm.（持久的职业认同，靠的不是声望，而是每天决定不故意伤害。）
:::

:::derivation{label="impart /ɪmˈpɑːt/ vt."}
**书本义：** 传授（知识、技能）；告知；赋予

**词形：** impart → imparted → imparting；名词 impartation 罕用，课文语境用 knowledge 作宾语即可。

**常用搭配：**
- impart knowledge / skills / wisdom to sb. 向某人传授知识/技能/智慧
- those who have imparted their knowledge upon the science of medicine 把知识赋予医学科学的人（课文原搭配）

**例句：**
1. The oath is a promise to respect those who have imparted their knowledge upon the science of medicine.（誓言承诺尊敬那些把知识赋予医学科学的人。）
2. A good teacher imparts not only facts but a method of observing disease.（好老师传授的不只是事实，还有观察疾病的方法。）

✍️ Clinical teachers impart judgment as well as information; the latter can be googled, the former cannot.（临床老师传授的是判断力而不只是信息；后者可以搜索，前者不能。）
:::

:::derivation{label="treatise /ˈtriːtɪs/ n."}
**书本义：** [C] 专著；论文（尤指系统论述某一学科）

**词形：** 复数 treatises；勿与 treaty（条约）混淆。

**常用搭配：**
- a treatise on ethics / anatomy 伦理学/解剖学专著
- over 60 treatises of medicine 六十余篇医学论著

**例句：**
1. Over 60 treatises of medicine, called the Hippocratic Corpus, have been attributed to him.（超过六十篇医学论著——希波克拉底文集——被归于他的名下。）
2. Conflicting contents in those treatises suggest more than one author.（这些论著内容互相冲突，说明作者不止一人。）

✍️ A treatise is not a blog post: it claims a systematic account, and therefore invites systematic doubt.（专著不是博文：它声称系统论述，因而也邀请系统性质疑。）
:::

:::derivation{label="tenet /ˈtenɪt/ n."}
**书本义：** [C] 信条；原则；核心主张（可数，常复数 tenets）

**词形：** 复数 tenets；近义 principle / doctrine，但 tenet 更强调「被某一群体持守的基本条款」。

**常用搭配：**
- the basic / main tenets of the oath 誓言的基本信条
- hold true to the tenets 坚守信条
- tenets of Hippocratic medicine 希波克拉底医学的信条

**例句：**
1. The main tenets are the same: treat patients to the best of one's abilities, never cause intentional harm, and maintain patient confidentiality.（主要信条相同：尽己所能治疗病人、永不故意伤害、保守病人秘密。）
2. As long as practitioners hold true to the basic tenets, the oath continues to serve mankind.（只要从业者坚守基本信条，誓言就会继续服务人类。）

✍️ Write the three tenets from memory before you argue whether the original oath is "invalid".（先默写三条信条，再争论原始誓言是否「无效」。）
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="课文把 tenets 收成三条"}
后文现代版本措辞不同，但主轴只有三句：best of one's abilities / never intentional harm / confidentiality。答辩、写作、翻译都先锁这三条，再谈 placebo、triage、euthanasia 为什么会和原始誓言打架。
:::

:::derivation{label="placebo /pləˈsiːbəʊ/ n."}
**书本义：** [C] 安慰剂；（双盲试验中的）对照剂

**词形：** 复数 placebos 或 placeboes；placebo effect 安慰剂效应。

**常用搭配：**
- use placebos in double-blind trials 在双盲试验中使用安慰剂
- placebo-controlled study 安慰剂对照研究

**例句：**
1. Using placebos in double-blind trials means doctors do not attempt a cure in every subject.（在双盲试验中使用安慰剂，意味着医生并非对每一名受试者都尝试治愈。）
2. The original oath required patients to be cured regardless of circumstances, which would forbid placebo controls.（原始誓言要求无论情形如何都要治愈病人，这会禁止安慰剂对照。）

✍️ A placebo is ethically tense precisely because it withholds active treatment in order to produce knowledge.（安慰剂之所以伦理上紧张，正因为它为了产生知识而暂不给予活性治疗。）
:::

:::derivation{label="triage /ˈtriːɑːʒ/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 检伤分类；分诊（按生存机会或紧迫程度决定治疗优先次序）

**词形：** 亦可作动词 triage patients；源于法语 trier「挑选」。

**常用搭配：**
- triage in war or disasters 战争或灾害中的检伤分类
- emergency triage 急诊分诊
- triage based on survival chances 按生存机会分诊

**例句：**
1. The original oath would also forbid triage, which is used during war or disasters when treatment is prioritised based on patients' survival chances.（原始誓言也会禁止检伤分类——战争或灾害中按生存机会决定治疗优先次序。）
2. Different medical care for patients with or without health insurance would be impossible under a literal reading of the original oath.（若按字面执行原始誓言，有无医疗保险的病人就不可能接受不同的医疗。）

✍️ Triage is not cruelty dressed as efficiency; it is the admission that resources are finite and delay itself can kill.（分诊不是披着效率外衣的残忍；它承认资源有限，拖延本身也会致命。）
:::

:::derivation{label="euthanasia /ˌjuːθəˈneɪziə/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 安乐死；为解除不治之症患者痛苦而有意结束生命

**词形：** assisted euthanasia 协助安乐死；词源 eu-（好）+ thanatos（死）。

**常用搭配：**
- assisted euthanasia 协助安乐死
- prohibit euthanasia 禁止安乐死
- euthanasia to relieve suffering 以安乐死解除痛苦

**例句：**
1. Finally, the original oath prohibits assisted euthanasia to relieve the suffering of patients with incurable conditions.（最后，原始誓言禁止为解除不治之症患者的痛苦而协助安乐死。）
2. Whether euthanasia is healing or killing is exactly the dividing line the oath tries to draw.（安乐死是救治还是杀戮，正是誓言试图划出的那条线。）

✍️ The oath's gold standard is a dividing line between healers and killers; euthanasia sits on that line, which is why the original wording cannot be copied unchanged.（誓言的金标准是医治者与杀手的分界；安乐死就坐在这条线上，所以原文不能原封不动照抄。）
:::

:::derivation{label="confidentiality /ˌkɒnfɪdenʃiˈæləti/ n."}
**书本义：** [U] 保密；机密性（医患关系中的核心义务）

**词形：** confidential（a. 机密的）→ confidentiality（n.）；动词 keep / maintain confidentiality。

**常用搭配：**
- maintain / protect patient confidentiality 维护患者保密
- breach of confidentiality 泄密
- duty of confidentiality 保密义务

**例句：**
1. Maintain patient confidentiality is listed among the main tenets shared by modern versions of the oath.（维护患者保密被列为现代诸版本誓言共有的主要信条。）
2. A breach of confidentiality can destroy the trust on which diagnosis depends.（泄密会摧毁诊断所依赖的信任。）

✍️ Confidentiality is not secrecy for the doctor's convenience; it is the condition that makes the patient willing to speak.（保密不是为了医生方便而保密；它是患者愿意开口的条件。）
:::

:::derivation{label="antiquated /ˈæntɪkweɪtɪd/ a."}
**书本义：** 过时的；陈旧的；不再适用的（含贬义或争议色彩）

**词形：** antique（古董）→ antiquated（过时的）；名词 antiquity 指古代。

**常用搭配：**
- too antiquated to be of use 太过时而无用
- an antiquated code / system 过时的守则/制度

**例句：**
1. Some radicals demand the removal of the Hippocratic Oath at graduation because it is too antiquated to be of use.（一些激进者要求毕业典礼取消希波克拉底誓言，理由是它太过时而无用。）
2. Modification of an antiquated text is not the same as discarding its tenets.（修改过时文本，不等于丢掉它的信条。）

✍️ Call a rule antiquated only after you can name which circumstance it can no longer cover.（只有当你能指出它再也覆盖不了哪种情形时，才配说一条规则过时。）
:::

:::derivation{label="meticulous /məˈtɪkjələs/ a."}
**书本义：** 一丝不苟的；极其仔细的

**词形：** meticulously（ad.）；meticulousness（n.）

**常用搭配：**
- meticulous in scientific methods 在科学方法上一丝不苟
- meticulous observation 细致观察

**例句：**
1. Hippocrates, being meticulous in his scientific methods, would surely have approved of changes that keep up with the world.（希波克拉底在科学方法上一丝不苟，想必会赞成那些为跟上世界发展而作的修改。）
2. Meticulous observation of man and disease is itself an ethical stance: you do not guess when you can look.（对人与疾病的细致观察本身就是伦理立场：能看的时候不要猜。）

✍️ A meticulous clinician revises the protocol without revising away the duty not to harm.（一丝不苟的临床医生会改方案，但不会把「不伤害」的义务改没。）
:::

:::derivation{label="utopian /juːˈtəʊpiən/ a."}
**书本义：** 乌托邦的；理想化而不切实际的

**词形：** Utopia（n. 乌托邦）→ utopian（a.）；反义 dystopian。

**常用搭配：**
- no matter how utopian it sounds 无论听起来多么乌托邦
- a utopian standard 理想化标准

**例句：**
1. A gold standard in moral and medical ethics — no matter how utopian it sounds — is still needed.（道德与医学伦理的金标准——无论听起来多么乌托邦——仍然需要。）
2. Calling an ideal utopian is not an argument for practising without any ideal.（把理想称作乌托邦，并不能论证可以没有任何理想地执业。）

✍️ Keep a utopian standard visible so that compromise remains visible as compromise, not as common sense.（把乌托邦标准留在视野里，妥协才仍然是妥协，而不是被叫成常识。）
:::

## 三、课文精讲 · Text A Hippocratic Oath, The Medical Ideal

课文是一篇医学伦理史论：先定义誓言，再问希波克拉底是谁、誓言是否他写的，然后沿西—东—西的知识路线追到 Percival、AMA、纽伦堡、日内瓦宣言，最后用当代困境（安慰剂、分诊、医保差异、高剂量化疗、安乐死）说明为什么必须改写，却不能丢掉三条信条。

### 段落 1 · 开篇定义

> Perhaps the most enduring — certainly the most quoted — tradition in the history of medicine is the Hippocratic Oath. Named after the famous Greek physician Hippocrates, this oath was written as a guideline for the medical ethics of doctors. Although the exact words have changed over time, the general content is the same — an oath to respect those who have imparted their knowledge upon the science of medicine, and respect to the patients as well as the promise to treat them to the best of the physicians' ability.

**中文翻译：** 或许在医学史上最持久的——当然也是被引用最多的——传统就是希波克拉底誓言。这个以古希腊著名医师希波克拉底命名的誓言，被写下来作为医师医学伦理的指导纲领。虽然准确措辞随时代而变，但主旨始终如一：尊敬那些把知识赋予医学科学的人，尊重病人，并承诺尽医师所能去治疗他们。

**讲解：** 第一句用 perhaps / certainly 的让步把「最持久」和「被引用最多」分开：引用次数可核实，持久性是评价。guideline for medical ethics 说明它一开始就不是药理手册。general content 锁三件事：尊师（imparted knowledge）、尊患者、尽所能治疗。后文所有「修改」都不得把这三件改没。

:::callout{kind="note" label="教材注释：名字不等于作者"}
Named after 只保证冠名，不保证 penmanship。第 2–3 段会立刻拆穿「医学之父亲笔」的传说。
:::

### 段落 2 · 希波克拉底是谁

> For a man considered by many to be the "Father of Medicine", little is known about Hippocrates of Cos. He lived circa 460–380 BC, and was the contemporary of Socrates as well as a practising physician. He was certainly held to be the most famous physician and teacher of medicine in his time. Over 60 treatises of medicine, called the Hippocratic Corpus, have been attributed to him; however, these treatises had conflicting contents and were written some time between 510 and 300 BC, and therefore could not all have been written by him.

**中文翻译：** 作为被许多人视为「医学之父」的人，我们对科斯岛的希波克拉底知之甚少。他生活于约公元前 460–380 年，是苏格拉底的同时代人，也是执业医师。在他的时代，他无疑被视为最著名的医师和医学教师。超过六十篇医学论著——希波克拉底文集——被归于他的名下；然而这些论著内容互相冲突，成文时间约在公元前 510 至 300 年之间，因此不可能全部出自他手。

**讲解：** little is known 与 Father of Medicine 对撞，是全文「理想大于作者」的伏笔。circa 表示约数。Hippocratic Corpus 是文集名，attributed to 是「被归在名下」，不是 proved to be written by。时间窗口 510–300 BC 比他本人寿命宽，所以「不可能都是他写的」是年代学推论，不是文学口味。

### 段落 3 · 作者存疑，观点仍在

> The Oath was named after Hippocrates, certainly, although its penmanship is still in question. According to authorities in medical history, the contents of the oath suggest that it was penned during the 4th Century BC, which makes it possible that Hippocrates had himself written it. Anyway, regardless of whether or not Hippocrates himself had written the Hippocratic Oath, the contents of the oath reflect his views on medical ethics.

**中文翻译：** 这份誓言当然以希波克拉底命名，尽管其手笔仍有疑问。据医学史权威看，誓言内容显示它起草于公元前 4 世纪，这使希波克拉底本人执笔成为可能。无论如何，不管是否他亲笔，誓言内容都反映了他对医学伦理的看法。

**讲解：** penmanship is still in question = 作者未定。penned during the 4th Century BC 与他的生平重叠，所以「有可能是他」。Anyway, regardless of whether or not 把作者问题放下，把 views on medical ethics 抬上来——后文「种子是希波克拉底或他的代笔者播下的」在这里已经埋好。

### 段落 4 · 从不受欢迎到借东方幸存

> The Hippocratic Oath was not very well received when it was first penned, being a representation of only a minor segment of Greek opinion at the time. However, by the end of ancient times, physicians began conforming to the conditions of the oath. It is possible that when scientific medicine suffered a gross decline after the fall of the Roman Empire, this oath, along with the dictates of Hippocratic medicine, was all but forgotten in the West. It was through the perseverance of the spirit of inquiry in the East that the tenets of Hippocratic medicine — and the Hippocratic Oath — survived this period of deterioration, notably through the writings of Arabian authorities in medicine. The knowledge of Greek medicine was later revived in the Christian West through the Latin translations of both these Arabic works and the original Greek texts.

**中文翻译：** 希波克拉底誓言初成文时并不很受欢迎，因为它只代表当时希腊意见中很小的一部分。然而到古代末期，医师们开始遵从誓言的条款。有可能在罗马帝国衰亡后科学医学严重衰退时，这份誓言连同希波克拉底医学的训令在西方几乎被遗忘。正是凭借东方坚持不懈的探索精神，希波克拉底医学的信条——以及誓言——才在这一恶化时期幸存，尤其是通过阿拉伯医学权威的著作。希腊医学知识后来在基督教西方复兴，靠的是这些阿拉伯著作以及希腊原文的拉丁文译本。

**讲解：** 路线是西衰 → 东存 → 拉丁译本回流。all but forgotten = 几乎被遗忘。Arabian authorities 不是点缀，是知识史的中转站。写「西方传统」却漏掉阿拉伯环节，就是没读这一段。

### 段落 5–6 · Percival 与 AMA：理想变成行业法典

> By the late 17th Century, standards of professional behaviour had been set in the Western World. The first code of medical ethics to be adopted by a professional organisation was written by English physician Thomas Percival (1740–1804) in 1794, which was adapted and adopted by the American Medical Association (AMA) in 1846. This code of ethics, which provided a gold standard for professional physicians, dictated the moral authority and independence of physicians in service to others and their responsibility towards the sick, as well as the physician's individual honour.

> The seeds had been sown by Hippocrates — or one of his ghost writers.

**中文翻译：** 到 17 世纪后期，专业行为标准已在西方世界确立。第一部被专业组织采纳的医学伦理法典，由英国内科医生托马斯·珀西瓦尔（1740–1804）于 1794 年写成，并于 1846 年被美国医学会改编并通过。这部为职业医师提供金标准的伦理法典，规定了医师在服务他人时的道德权威与独立性、对病患的责任，以及医师的个人荣誉。

种子是由希波克拉底——或他的某位代笔者——播下的。

**讲解：** 数字要记：Percival 1794，AMA 1846。code of ethics 把誓言从「理想」做成可被组织采纳的 gold standard。ghost writers 与第 3 段 penmanship 呼应，语气故意轻松：作者可以匿名，种子不能匿名。

:::timeline{period="从理想到行业法典"}
- **约前 4 世纪** 誓言成文（作者存疑）
- **罗马帝国衰亡后** 西方几乎遗忘；阿拉伯著作保存信条
- **拉丁译本** 希腊医学在基督教西方复兴
- **1794** Percival 写出第一部被专业组织采纳的医学伦理法典
- **1846** AMA 改编并通过
- **1947** Nuremberg Code：人体试验伦理
- **1948** WMA 通过 Declaration of Geneva
:::

### 段落 7 · 纽伦堡与日内瓦

> After World War II, 23 doctors from Nazi Germany concentration camps were found guilty of breaching the code of medical ethics by performing horrifying medical experiments on prisoners. This incident led to the composition of the Nuremberg Code (1947), which represented the starting point in discussions regarding ethical treatment of human subjects, and outlined the ethics of medical research with regard to the rights of these subjects. This in turn led to the adoption of the Declaration of Geneva oath by the World Medical Association in 1948.

**中文翻译：** 二战后，23 名来自纳粹德国集中营的医生因在囚犯身上进行骇人听闻的医学实验、违反医学伦理准则而被判有罪。这一事件促成 1947 年《纽伦堡法典》的制定，它成为关于人体受试者伦理待遇讨论的起点，并概述了医学研究中这些受试者的权利。这进而促使世界医学协会于 1948 年通过日内瓦宣言誓词。

**讲解：** 23 名医生、1947、1948 三个数字常考。Nuremberg Code 的对象是 human subjects（人体受试者），不是一般门诊病人——研究伦理从这里单独立户。Declaration of Geneva 是 WMA 的现代誓词，后文把它列为当今四版本之一。课文中文稿有一处把 Geneva 写成「维也纳」，那是翻译笔误，英文原文是 Geneva。

:::callout{kind="pitfall" label="日内瓦宣言不是维也纳宣言"}
中文对照把 the Declaration of Geneva 写成「维也纳宣言」，与 World Medical Association 1948 对不上。考试、翻译都写 **Geneva / 日内瓦**。
:::

### 段落 8 · 当代困境：为什么原文会「禁止」必要的医学

> The reintroduction of the oath has been problematic. Medical ethics are complex. They must balance patient expectations, social demands and taboos, economic and political realities, and evolving medical and scientific knowledge. For instance, the original oath required patients to be cured regardless of circumstances. However, using placebos in double-blind trials, considered essential for drug development, means doctors do not attempt a cure. The original oath would also forbid triage. This is used during war or disasters when treatment is prioritised based on patients' survival chances. Different medical care for patients with or without health insurance would be impossible. Some dangerous types of chemotherapy using toxic drugs in high doses would be disallowed. Finally, the original oath prohibits assisted euthanasia to relieve the suffering of patients with incurable conditions.

**中文翻译：** 誓言的重新引入一直成问题。医学伦理相当复杂。它们必须在患者期望、社会需求与禁忌、经济与政治现实、以及不断演进的医学科学知识之间取得平衡。例如，原始誓言要求无论情形如何都要治愈患者。然而，药物研发中必不可少的双盲试验使用安慰剂，意味着医生并非在尝试治愈。原始誓言也会禁止检伤分类——战争或灾害中按生存机会安排治疗优先次序。有无医疗保险的病人接受不同医疗将变得不可能。某些使用高剂量毒性药物的危险化疗会被禁止。最后，原始誓言禁止为解除不治之症患者痛苦而协助安乐死。

**讲解：** 这一段是议论文的「反例清单」，五项都对准 required patients to be cured regardless of circumstances。placebo、triage、insurance difference、high-dose chemotherapy、euthanasia 不是五个无关例子，而是同一条「无条件治愈」在现代被撕开的五个口子。

### 段落 9 · 四个现代版本，三条不变信条

> Therefore people argue that the original Hippocratic Oath is invalid in a society that has seen drastic socio-economic, political and moral changes since the time of Hippocrates. This has led to the modification of the oath to something better suited for our times. Four of the most widely used versions nowadays are the Declaration of Geneva — mentioned previously, the Prayer of Maimonides, the Oath of Lasagna, and the Reinstatement of Hippocratic Oath. Although they differ in wording and content, the main tenets are the same — treat patients to the best of one's abilities, never cause intentional harm, and maintain patient confidentiality — although none of them call upon various deities to punish the physician if he transgresses from the oath, save for the Reinstatement.

**中文翻译：** 因此有人争辩：自希波克拉底以来社会已经历剧烈的社会经济、政治与道德变革，原始誓言已无效。这促使人们把誓言改成更适合当代的文本。当今使用最广的四个版本是：前文已提及的日内瓦宣言、迈蒙尼德祷词、拉萨尼亚誓言，以及「恢复的希波克拉底誓言」。它们措辞与内容不同，但主要信条相同——尽己所能治疗病人、永不故意伤害、保守病人秘密——而且除「恢复本」外，没有哪个版本再召唤神灵来惩罚违约的医师。

**讲解：** invalid 是「作为可逐字执行的古代文本无效」，不是「医学可以没有伦理」。四版本名字要能辨认。save for = except for。deities 这一句说明现代誓言去神圣化，变成职业承诺。

:::compare{title="原始誓言 vs 现代版本"}
| 项目 | 原始誓言 | 现代诸版本 |
|------|----------|------------|
| 无条件治愈 | 要求 regardless of circumstances | 允许安慰剂、分诊、高剂量化疗等 |
| 安乐死 | 禁止 assisted euthanasia | 争议仍在，不再被一句原文锁死 |
| 神灵惩罚 | 违约则遭神罚 | 除 Reinstatement 外不再召唤神灵 |
| 执业资格 | 发誓才可执业（课文后文对照） | 多为 code of practice，不作为执照前提 |
| 不变的三条 | 尽所能、不故意伤害、保密 | 同样三条 |
:::

### 段落 10 · 今天如何宣誓

> Today, most graduating medical students swear to some form of the oath before they go out into the world to practice medicine. However, this is usually a modern version, which bears little resemblance to the original. Some medical schools, particularly in the United States, combine other oaths with modern rules to create a workable ethical model. Unlike the original, the new oath does not require that doctors swear it before they can practise. Instead, it is a code of practice, and a symbol for young doctors entering the profession and their commitment to healing.

**中文翻译：** 如今，多数医学院毕业生在走出校园行医之前，都会宣读某种形式的誓言。然而这通常是与原文相似度很小的现代版本。一些医学院——尤其在美国——把其他誓言与现代规则结合起来，做成可行的伦理模型。与原文不同，新誓言并不要求医生必须宣誓才能执业。相反，它是一份执业守则，也是年轻医生进入这个职业、承诺救治的象征。

**讲解：** bears little resemblance 不是「毫无关系」，是字面不像。workable ethical model 点题：伦理要能用。symbol + commitment to healing 把仪式意义留下，把执照门槛拿掉。

### 段落 11–12 · 为什么这么老的文本仍能影响医学

> The question is — why has such an old bit of writing, descended from ancient times, so profoundly influenced the practice of medicine throughout the history of medical science?

> G. E. R. Lloyd said of Hippocratic medicine: "In the Western world, the name Hippocrates has always stood for an ideal." And this is what the oath is all about — an ideal gold ethics standard representing a clear dividing line separating healers and killers, a commitment that physicians make to protect life, and never to take life away deliberately. In a world where society is always attempting to put the blame on physicians when things go wrong, this oath, when upheld, would protect not only physicians and their patients, but also their families and the society as a whole.

**中文翻译：** 问题是：为何这么一小段来自古代的文字，能在整个医学科学史上如此深刻地影响医学实践？

G. E. R. Lloyd 这样评价希波克拉底医学：「在西方世界，希波克拉底这个名字始终代表着一种理想。」而这正是誓言的全部意义——一条理想的伦理金标准，划出医治者与杀手的明确分界；医师承诺保护生命，永不故意夺走生命。在一个事情出错时社会总想把责任推给医师的世界里，这份被持守的誓言将不仅保护医师与患者，也保护他们的家庭和整个社会。

**讲解：** 课文把 Lloyd 的姓写成 G E R Lloyd（中文对照误作「德国人劳埃德」）。stood for an ideal 把人名做成符号。dividing line separating healers and killers 是全文价值句，写作题应能默写。when upheld 是条件：誓言不自动保护任何人，必须被持守。

### 段落 13 · 收束：可改字，不可改信条

> It is understandable that some radicals may demand the removal of the Hippocratic Oath at graduation with the argument that it is too antiquated to be of use. However, even in this modern age of technological and medical enlightenment, a gold standard in moral and medical ethics — no matter how utopian it sounds — is still needed not only to set an example to those who are inheriting the medical business, but also to protect those at the receiving end of the medical practice. Modification of the original oath is unavoidable — Hippocrates, being a man who was meticulous in his scientific methods and keen in his observations of the world with regard to man and disease, would surely have approved of the changes for the sake of keeping up with the developments of the world — but as long as our physicians and practitioners hold true to the basic tenets of the Hippocratic Oath, it would continue to serve mankind for long after the name of its writer has been forgotten.

**中文翻译：** 可以理解，一些激进者可能以「太过时而无用」为由，要求毕业典礼取消希波克拉底誓言。然而，即使在科技与医学启蒙的当代，道德与医学伦理的金标准——无论听起来多么乌托邦——仍然需要：既为继承这门行业的人树立榜样，也保护处于医疗实践接收端的人。修改原文不可避免——希波克拉底在科学方法上一丝不苟，对人与疾病的观察敏锐，为了跟上世界发展，他想必会赞成这些改动——但只要医师与从业者坚守希波克拉底誓言的基本信条，它就会在作者姓名被遗忘很久以后，继续为人类服务。

**讲解：** 最后一句用破折号把「必须改」和「不能丢 tenets」焊在一起。inheriting the medical business 与 receiving end 是对称的两端：从业者与患者。name of its writer has been forgotten 回到第 2 段 little is known：作者本来就可以被忘，信条不能被忘。

## 四、长难句

:::derivation{label="长难句 1 · 东方保存与拉丁回流"}
It was through the perseverance of the spirit of inquiry in the East that the tenets of Hippocratic medicine — and the Hippocratic Oath — survived this period of deterioration, notably through the writings of Arabian authorities in medicine.

**结构：** It was through X that Y survived Z, notably through W。强调句（it was ... that ...）把途径「东方探索精神」提前；破折号插入 the Hippocratic Oath，与 tenets 并列；notably through 再补一条更具体的途径。

**要点：** 不要把 Arabian authorities 读成「另外一段历史」。它是 survived 的机制。
:::

:::derivation{label="长难句 2 · 改写不可避免，信条必须留下"}
Modification of the original oath is unavoidable — Hippocrates, being a man who was meticulous in his scientific methods and keen in his observations of the world with regard to man and disease, would surely have approved of the changes for the sake of keeping up with the developments of the world — but as long as our physicians and practitioners hold true to the basic tenets of the Hippocratic Oath, it would continue to serve mankind for long after the name of its writer has been forgotten.

**结构：** 主句 1（Modification is unavoidable）+ 破折号插入独立主格 being a man who was A and keen in B，虚拟 would have approved + for the sake of ... + but as long as 条件句 + 时间状语 for long after ...。

**要点：** being a man who... 不是另起一个作者传记，而是给「他会赞成修改」提供性格证据。真正的逻辑重心在 but as long as ... hold true to the basic tenets。
:::

:::keypoint{label="长难句 3 · 金标准那条分界"}
an ideal gold ethics standard representing a clear dividing line separating healers and killers, a commitment that physicians make to protect life, and never to take life away deliberately

**层次：** standard 后接 representing ...；line 后接 separating A and B；随后 a commitment 与 standard 同位，commitment 再接 that 从句 + and never to ...。翻译时先译「金标准 = 分界线」，再译「承诺 = 护命、不故意夺命」。
:::

## 五、主旨、写作手法与本课账本

课文是**历史叙述 + 伦理论辩**。历史线负责证明誓言能活过帝国崩溃；论辩线负责证明活下来的是 tenets 不是原文。修辞上反复使用 gold standard、ideal、dividing line，把职业身份做成「医治者 / 杀手」的二选一，而不是「技术好不好」的量表。

:::keypoint{label="本课必须能默写的账"}
- 词根：cardi/o 心，derm/o 皮肤，nephr/o 肾，-itis 炎，-oma 瘤，-ectomy 切掉，-tomy 切开。
- 连接元音：辅音后缀前留 o，元音后缀前常丢 o；两词根之间常留 o。
- 三条信条：尽所能治疗、不故意伤害、保密。
- 五个现代冲突点：安慰剂双盲、分诊、医保差异、高剂量化疗、协助安乐死。
- 四个现代版本：Geneva / Maimonides / Lasagna / Reinstatement。
- 两个年份：Nuremberg 1947，Declaration of Geneva 1948。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="写作可用的课文句型"}
- Named after X, Y was written as a guideline for ...
