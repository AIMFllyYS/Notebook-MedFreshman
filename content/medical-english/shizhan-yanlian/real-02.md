## 一、I. Choose the correct meaning for each prefix or suffix（8%）

### 第 1 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
masto –

A. breast　　B. womb　　C. menses　　D. ovary
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】A**（同源样题卷面标注）

**mast/o-** = 乳房（mastitis 乳腺炎，mastectomy 乳房切除）。womb = hyster/o- / metr/o- / uter/o-；menses = men/o-；ovary = oophor/o- / ovari/o-。dump 选项 D 作 orary，样题作 ovary，按 ovary 理解。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：mast-"}
mast/o-、mamm/o- 均表乳房。mammography 乳腺摄影。不要和 mastoid（乳突，形似乳头）只记成「乳房病」。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
masto- = **breast**；子宫/月经/卵巢是别的词根。
:::

### 第 2 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
extra –

A. inside　　B. outside　　C. across　　D. backward
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】B**（同源样题卷面标注）

**extra-** = 外、在外（extracellular 细胞外，extrahepatic 肝外）。inside = intra-/endo-；across = trans-；backward = retro-。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：内外前缀"}
| 前缀 | 意义 |
|------|------|
| extra-, exo-, ecto- | 外 |
| intra-, endo- | 内 |
| trans- | 穿过 |
| retro- | 向后 |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
extra- = **outside**。
:::

### 第 3 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
spleno –

A. cell　　B. bone marrow　　C. thymus　　D. spleen
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】D**（同源样题卷面标注）

**splen/o-** = 脾。cell = cyt/o-；bone marrow = myel/o-；thymus = thym/o-。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：淋巴器官词根"}
splen/o- 脾，thym/o- 胸腺，lymph/o- 淋巴，myel/o- 骨髓/脊髓。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
spleno- = **spleen**，不是骨髓或胸腺。
:::

### 第 4 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
– ectomy

A. cutting　　B. stomy　　C. cutting out　　D. outlet-forming
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】C**（同源样题卷面标注）

**-ectomy** = 切除、切掉（cutting **out**）。cutting  alone 更接近 -tomy（切开）。outlet-forming / stomy = **-stomy**（造口）。选项 B 把后缀当词义，不能选。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：-tomy 家族"}
| 后缀 | 意义 | 例 |
|------|------|-----|
| -tomy | 切开 | tracheotomy |
| -ectomy | 切除 | splenectomy |
| -stomy | 造口 | colostomy |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
-ectomy = **cutting out**；造口是 -stomy。
:::

### 第 5 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
dermo –

A. skin　　B. below　　C. under　　D. cuti
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】A**（同源样题卷面标注）

**derm/o-、dermat/o-** = 皮肤。below/under = sub-/hypo-。cuti 来自 cutis（皮肤的拉丁词干），不是英语释义选项。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：皮肤词根"}
derm/o-、dermat/o-、cutane/o- 都表皮肤（subcutaneous 皮下）。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
dermo- = **skin**。
:::

### 第 6 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
proteo –

A. sweet　　B. bile　　C. protein　　D. fat
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】C**（同源样题卷面标注）

**prote/o-** = 蛋白质（proteolysis 蛋白水解，proteinuria）。sweet = glyc/o-；bile = chol/e-；fat = lip/o- / adip/o-。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：生化三大词根"}
proteo- 蛋白，lipo- 脂，glyco- 糖。bile 是 chole-。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
proteo- = **protein**。
:::

### 第 7 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
chromo –

A. time　　B. color　　C. for　　D. disease
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】B**（同源样题卷面标注）

**chrom/o-、chromat/o-** = 颜色（chromosome 染色体，原义「着色小体」；chromaturia 有色尿）。time = chron/o-（chronic）。disease = path/o- / -pathy。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：chromo vs chrono"}
chromo- 色；chrono- 时。chronic 慢性来自 chrono-，与颜色无关。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
chromo- = **color**；time 是 chrono-。
:::

### 第 8 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
necro –

A. life　　B. colour　　C. time　　D. dead
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】D**（同源样题卷面标注）

**necr/o-** = 死、坏死（necrosis, necropsy）。life = bio-；colour = chromo-；time = chrono-。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：死与活"}
necro- 死；bio- 生。necrosis 坏死 vs apoptosis 凋亡（课文 injury 单元常对举）。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
necro- = **dead**；life 是 bio-。
:::

:::memory{label="本卷词根"}
- [ ] masto- 乳房；dermo- 皮肤；spleno- 脾
- [ ] extra- 外；intra-/endo- 内
- [ ] proteo- 蛋白；chromo- 色；chrono- 时；necro- 死
- [ ] -ectomy 切除（cutting out）；-tomy 切开；-stomy 造口
- [ ] 易混：chromo ≠ chrono；-ectomy ≠ -stomy
:::

## 二、II. Filling in the blanks with proper words（8%）

### 第 9 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
We will use the term ________ to refer to the data that can be gathered about an individual patient, namely, symptoms, signs, and laboratory abnormalities.
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】manifestations**（同源样题；样题拼作 menifestations，标准拼写如下）

症状、体征、化验异常合称疾病的 **manifestations**（临床表现）。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：manifestation"}
clinical manifestations = symptoms + signs + lab abnormalities。symptom 主观，sign 客观。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
症状+体征+化验 = **manifestations**。
:::

### 第 10 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
__________________ are those diseases characterized by structural changes within the body as the most basic abnormality.
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】Structural diseases**（同源样题；样题拼作 Structral）

以体内结构改变为最基本异常的疾病 = **structural diseases**（器质性疾病 / 结构性疾病），相对 functional / biochemical 分类。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：结构病"}
structural diseases 的结构改变即 lesions，可为 morphologic，有时也与 biochemical 改变并存。常分三大类，且可跨类。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
以结构改变为最基本异常 → **Structural diseases**。
:::

### 第 11 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
The surgeon should never leave the operating room area until the report is dictated, unless his or her ___________ is required for an emergency elsewhere.
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】attendance**（同源样题）

手术报告口述完成前不得离开手术区域，除非别处急诊需要该外科医生到场（**attendance**）。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：dictate the report"}
dictate = 口述（手术记录）。attendance 到场、出勤。与 Hippocratic 课文 penned/dictated 的「规定」义不同。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
急诊需要医生 **attendance**（到场）才能离开手术室区。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
The function of the ______________ is to protect us from organisms that cause disease, and from other materials that would be harmful to the body.
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】immune system**（同源样题）

保护机体免受病原体及其他有害物 = **immune system**。与 2021 卷 Passage 2 同知识点。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：immune system"}
defends against organisms and harmful materials。AIDS 则损害该系统。

防病原、防有害物 = **immune system**。

### 第 13 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
AIDS, abbreviation of ________________________________________, is a viral disease that impairs the immune system of the human body, leaving it prey to a great variety of infections that would be readily suppressed by a functioning immune system.
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】acquired immune deficiency syndrome**（同源样题）

亦可写 acquired **immunodeficiency** syndrome。要点：acquired（获得性）+ immune deficiency + syndrome。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：AIDS"}

:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
AIDS = **acquired immune deficiency syndrome**。
:::

Approximately 10% of the patients _______ breast cancer present with a history of trauma.

## 三、III. Choose the best answer（8%）

### 第 17 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
Genetic and developmental diseases _______________.

A. cover a wide range of abnormalities
B. are influenced by environment so as to appear early in life
C. are defined as injuries, inflammation, or even neoplasms
D. are caused by biochemical changes present at birth
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】B**（同源样题卷面标注）

遗传/发育性疾病受环境等因素影响，**早年显现**。A 也像正确概括，但样题标 B。C 是损伤/炎/肿瘤等其他结构病类别。D 把范围收窄到先天生化异常（inborn errors），不能概括全部遗传发育病。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：疾病四大结构类别（课文）"}
遗传与发育异常；损伤与炎症；肿瘤；等。一类病可跨类。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
genetic and developmental diseases：**早年出现，可受环境修饰（B）**。
:::

### 第 18 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
Structural changes within the body ________.

A. are the most basic abnormality
B. are usually at the microscopic or electron microscopic level
C. may be either biochemical or morphologic
D. are difficult to classify
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】A**（据第 10 题定义推断；样题未标）

第 10 题：structural diseases 以 **structural changes as the most basic abnormality**。B 过绝对（也可肉眼见）。C 更常用来描述 lesions 的属性，且 biochemical 相对 morphologic。D 不是定义。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：lesion"}
结构改变即 lesion。可微观，可肉眼；分类上「不总是好分」，但题干问的是「结构改变是什么」。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
structural changes = 结构病的 **most basic abnormality（A）**。
:::

### 第 19 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
Which of the following statements are not true?

A. Structural diseases sometimes fall into more than one category.
B. Structural diseases are not always to classify.
C. Structural diseases usually fall into more of the three broad categories.
D. Structural diseases are called lesions that may be biochemical or morphologic.
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】C**（据课文逻辑推断；样题未标）

通常归入三大类之**一**，有时跨类（A 为真）。C「usually fall into **more of** the three」把「有时跨类」说成「通常落入更多类」，**不真**。B 语法残缺，意近「不总是好分类」，可视为拙劣真命题。D 把疾病与 lesion 混称，课文常说 structural **changes/lesions** may be morphologic or biochemical，作为干扰项不如 C 明确为假。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：not true 题"}
先找与课文相反的量化词（usually vs sometimes）。跨类是 sometimes，不是 usually more。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
结构病 **有时** 跨类，不是 usually 落入更多类 → 选 **C**。
:::

### 第 20 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
The author suggests that all of the following means be used to help children with elevated blood cholesterol levels except ________.

A. formal nutrition counseling
B. regular follow-up, including measurement of blood levels
C. dietary interventions as first therapy
D. lipid-lowering medications
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】D**（据 Lesson 4 / NCEP 课文推断）

儿童高胆固醇：**饮食干预为一线**，营养咨询与随访监测；**不把降脂药当常规首选**（except D）。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：DISC / NCEP"}
Dietary Intervention Study in Children。一线 diet，药物谨慎。第 40 题中译也强调不宜轻易给儿童贴「高危」标签。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
儿童高胆固醇 except **lipid-lowering medications**。
:::

### 第 21 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
The one thing that the author does not recommend is that _________.

A. young people should be screened to detect elevated blood cholesterol levels
B. a low-fat, balanced diet should become the norm
C. efforts to lower fat in food should be encouraged
D. dietary education should be promoted
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】A**（据第 40 题中译立场推断）

血胆固醇测定不稳定、给儿童贴「高危」标签的风险、筛查计划收益有限 → 作者**不主张**广泛筛查年轻人。B–D 是人群饮食策略，作者支持。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：筛查 vs 饮食环境"}
个体筛查（labeling）≠ 让低脂均衡饮食成为常态。课文常挺后者、疑前者。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
不推荐：**young people screening（A）**；推荐低脂饮食与宣教。
:::

### 第 22 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
Which of the following is true of the students in the control of the DISC?

A. They showed the same reductions in blood lipid levels.
B. They made changes toward healthier behaviors.
C. They were aware of the high risks of the study.
D. They made information in this trial available to physicians.
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】B**（据 DISC 对照逻辑推断；样题未标）

干预组 3 年 LDL-C 与总胆固醇显著下降（见卷 B / Lesson 4 英译句）。对照组**不会有同等降脂**（A 假）。对照儿童仍可能出现一定健康行为改变（试验污染/霍桑效应），B 相对可成立。C、D 无课文依据。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：干预 vs 对照"}
intervention group：significant LDL-C / TC reductions。control：nonsignificant lipid change。平均效果还会被「不依从者」稀释（15 级样题译句）。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
DISC 对照 ≠ 同等降脂；较可能的「true」= **健康行为有些改变（B）**。
:::

### 第 23 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
How does HIV damages the immune system?

A. By integrating with and taking over RNA of cells.
B. By infiltrating and blood stream.
C. By infecting and damaging helper T cells.
D. By increasing the crucial activities of the killer T cells.
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】C**（据 AIDS 课文推断）

HIV 感染并破坏 **helper T cells（CD4+）**。A 错在 HIV 是 RNA 病毒，逆转录后整合的是宿主 **DNA**。B 语法残缺。D 相反（细胞免疫被削弱）。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：HIV 靶细胞"}
helper T = CD4。机会性感染出现于免疫崩溃之后。killer T 是 CD8，不是 HIV 主要破坏并「增强」的对象。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
HIV 损免疫 = 感染破坏 **helper T cells（C）**。
:::

### 第 24 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
The author believed that the best way to check the spread of the AIDS is __________.

A. to conduct global education about the disease
B. to treat the known cases with AZT
C. to limit the process of the disease
D. to use condoms and other "safe sex" practices
:::

### 第 46 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
Studies aimed at determining the relationship between stress and the immune system have investigated the effects of factors as diverse as examinations, bereavement, divorce, unemployment, mental arithmetic, and looking after a relative with Alzheimer's disease.
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】（参考；与 15 级样题第 80 句同类）**

旨在测定应激与免疫系统之间关系的研究，已调查了多种因素的影响，例如考试、丧亲、离婚、失业、心算以及照料阿尔茨海默病亲属等。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：stress–immune"}
diverse factors：exams, bereavement, divorce, unemployment, mental arithmetic, caregiving。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
应激–免疫研究因素要能列出 **考试/丧亲/离婚/失业/心算/照料痴呆亲属**。
:::

### 第 47 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
As the immune system deteriorates, the patient tends to develop recurrent or chronic opportunistic infections – i.e., infections easily combated by an intact immune system but that can take advantage of a severely weakened system to produce devastating and eventually lethal diseases.
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】（参考）**

随着免疫系统衰退，患者往往出现反复或慢性机会性感染——即健全免疫系统本可轻易抵御、却能利用严重削弱的免疫系统造成毁灭性乃至致命疾病的感染。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：opportunistic infection"}
机会性感染：intact immune system 可控制；AIDS 等削弱后致命。回扣第 13 题 leaving it prey to...
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
免疫垮 → **opportunistic infections**（健全时不足道，削弱后致命）。
:::

It is well known that cancer is a dangerous disease which spares no one, irrespective of age or sex.

**【答案】（参考）**

众所周知，癌症是一种不论年龄或性别、无人能幸免的危险疾病。

spare no one = 不放过任何人；irrespective of = 不论。

irrespective of age or sex = regardless of age or sex。

### 第 50 题

每攻克一个细胞，该细胞就充满成千上万新病毒，自身死亡同时释放出那些病毒，以攻击更多白细胞。（Lesson 9 Translation）
