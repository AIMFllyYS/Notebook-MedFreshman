## 一、Part I Terminology（20%）

### Section A　选词根 / 前缀 / 后缀（10%）

### 第 1 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
vein

A. thrombo-　　B. phlebo-　　C. thymo-　　D. spiro-
:::

::figure{src="/images/medical-english/exam-real-2021/p001-01.jpeg" alt="2021 医学英语试卷第 1 页" caption="第 1 页：Part I Section A 第 1–10 题、Section B 第 11–16 题"}

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】B**（据词根推断）

- **phleb/o-**：静脉（phlebitis 静脉炎，phlebotomy 静脉切开）。
- A thrombo-：血栓。C thymo-：胸腺。D spiro-：呼吸 / 螺旋。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：静脉与血管词根"}
| 词根 | 意义 | 例词 |
|------|------|------|
| phleb/o- | 静脉 | phlebitis, phlebotomy |
| angi/o-, vas/o- | 血管 | angioplasty, vasoconstriction |
| thromb/o- | 血栓 | thrombosis |
| arteri/o- | 动脉 | arteriosclerosis |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
vein = **phlebo-**；不要看成 thrombo-（血块）或 spiro-（呼吸）。
:::

### 第 2 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
joint

A. osteo-　　B. arthro-　　C. nephro-　　D. oculo-
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】B**（据词根推断）

**arthr/o-** = 关节（arthritis 关节炎，arthroscopy 关节镜）。osteo- 骨，nephro- 肾，oculo- 眼。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：骨与关节"}
| 词根 | 意义 | 例词 |
|------|------|------|
| arthr/o- | 关节 | arthritis, arthropathy |
| oste/o- | 骨 | osteomyelitis |
| chondr/o- | 软骨 | chondritis |
| my/o- | 肌 | myalgia |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
joint = **arthro-**；osteo- 是骨不是关节。
:::

### 第 3 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
brain

A. adreno-　　B. myelo-　　C. encephalo-　　D. entero-
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】C**（据词根推断）

**encephal/o-** = 脑（encephalitis 脑炎，electroencephalogram 脑电图）。adreno- 肾上腺，myelo- 脊髓 / 骨髓，entero- 肠。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：神经中枢词根"}
| 词根 | 意义 | 注意 |
|------|------|------|
| encephal/o- | 脑 | encephalitis |
| cerebr/o- | 大脑 | cerebrovascular |
| myel/o- | 脊髓 **或** 骨髓 | 看搭配 |
| neur/o- | 神经 | neuropathy |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
brain = **encephalo-**；myelo- 优先记脊髓/骨髓，不是脑。
:::

### 第 4 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
tooth

A. oro-　　B. dento-　　C. angio-　　D. colo-
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】B**（据词根推断）

**dent/o-**（及 odont/o-）= 牙。oro- 口，angio- 血管，colo- 结肠。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：口齿词根"}
| 词根 | 意义 | 例词 |
|------|------|------|
| dent/o-, odont/o- | 牙 | dental, orthodontics |
| or/o- | 口 | oral |
| stomat/o- | 口 | stomatitis |
| gloss/o-, lingu/o- | 舌 | glossitis |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
tooth = **dento-**；oro- 是口，不是牙。
:::

### 第 5 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
voice box

A. broncho-　　B. gastro-　　C. tracheo-　　D. laryngo-
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】D**（据词根推断）

voice box = larynx（喉）。**laryng/o-**。broncho- 支气管，tracheo- 气管，gastro- 胃。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：呼吸道由上到下"}
喉 larynx（laryngo-）→ 气管 trachea（tracheo-）→ 支气管 bronchus（broncho-）→ 肺 pneumo-/pulmo-。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
voice box = 喉 = **laryngo-**；气管是 tracheo-，支气管是 broncho-。
:::

### 第 6 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
blood vessel

A. angio-　　B. odonto-　　C. dermato-　　D. spiro-
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】A**（据词根推断）

**angi/o-** = 血管（angiogram, angioplasty）。odonto- 牙，dermato- 皮肤，spiro- 呼吸。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：血管三词根"}
angi/o-、vas/o-、vascul/o- 都可表血管；本题选项只有 **angio-**。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
blood vessel = **angio-**。
:::

### 第 7 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
suture

A. -rrhaphy　　B. -plasty　　C. -stomy　　D. -spasm
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】A**（据词根推断）

**-rrhaphy** = 缝合（herniorrhaphy 疝修补缝合）。-plasty 成形，-stomy 造口，-spasm 痉挛。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：手术后缀"}
| 后缀 | 意义 | 例词 |
|------|------|------|
| -rrhaphy | 缝合 | neurorrhaphy |
| -plasty | 成形 / 整形 | rhinoplasty |
| -stomy | 造口 | colostomy |
| -ectomy | 切除 | appendectomy |
| -tomy | 切开 | laparotomy |
| -centesis | 穿刺 | thoracentesis |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
suture = **-rrhaphy**；造口 -stomy，成形 -plasty，穿刺 -centesis。
:::

### 第 8 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
heart

A. vaso-　　B. reno-　　C. cardio-　　D. auro-
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】C**（据词根推断）

**cardi/o-** = 心。vaso- 血管，reno- 肾，auro- 耳（auricle）/ 金。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：心肾不要混"}
cardio- 心，reno-/nephr/o- 肾，vaso- 血管，cor- 也表心（coronary）。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
heart = **cardio-**。
:::

### 第 9 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
puncture

A. -stomy　　B. -ectomy　　C. -sclerosis　　D. -centesis
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】D**（据词根推断）

**-centesis** = 穿刺抽液（amniocentesis 羊膜腔穿刺，paracentesis 腹腔穿刺）。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：-centesis"}
centesis ← 希腊语「刺」。胸穿 thoracentesis，腹穿 paracentesis，关节腔穿刺 arthrocentesis。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
puncture = **-centesis**；-ectomy 是切掉，-stomy 是造口。
:::

### 第 10 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
bad

A. eu-　　B. dys-　　C. endo-　　D. contra-
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】B**（据词根推断）

**dys-** = 坏、困难、异常（dyspnea 呼吸困难，dysfunction）。eu- 好/正常，endo- 内，contra- 反。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：好坏一对前缀"}
| 前缀 | 意义 | 例 |
|------|------|-----|
| eu- | 好、正常 | euthyroid, eupnea |
| dys- | 坏、困难 | dyspnea, dysuria |
| mal- | 坏 | malformation |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
bad = **dys-**；eu- 恰好相反（好/正常）。
:::

### Section B　划线词素义（10%）

### 第 11 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
enteradenitis（划线部分：aden）

A. sac　　B. cell　　C. gland　　D. thymus
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】C**（据词根推断）

enter- 肠 + **aden-** 腺 + -itis 炎 → 肠腺炎。aden/o- = gland。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：aden-"}
aden/o- 腺（adenoma 腺瘤，adenitis 腺炎，lymphadenopathy 淋巴结病）。胸腺是 thymo-，不是 aden- 本身。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
aden = **gland（腺）**；enteradenitis = 肠腺炎。
:::

### 第 12 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
dysmenorrhea（划线部分：-rrhea）

A. pain　　B. occur　　C. pause　　D. discharge
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】D**（据词根推断）

**-rrhea** = 流出 / 溢液（diarrhea, rhinorrhea）。痛经的「痛」在 dys- + men/o（月经）的「异常」，但划线是 -rrhea，义为 discharge。pain 对应 -algia（menalgia）或 dys- 的主观感觉，不是 -rrhea。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：-rrhea / -rrhagia / -algia"}
| 后缀 | 意义 | 例 |
|------|------|-----|
| -rrhea | 流出 | diarrhea, menorrhea |
| -rrhagia | 爆发性出血 | menorrhagia |
| -algia, -dynia | 痛 | neuralgia |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
-rrhea = **discharge（流出）**；痛经的痛不要误选 A。
:::

### 第 13 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
rhinedema（划线部分：rhin-）

A. kidney　　B. vein　　C. nose　　D. throat
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】C**（据词根推断）

**rhin/o-** = 鼻（rhinitis, rhinoplasty）。edema 水肿 → 鼻水肿。肾 nephr/o-，静脉 phleb/o-，咽 laryng/o- / pharyng/o-。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：rhin-"}
rhin/o- 鼻；nas/o- 也是鼻（nasal）。不要和 ren-（肾）形近混淆。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
rhin- = **nose**。
:::

### 第 14 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
myelosis（划线部分：myel-）

A. spinal cord　　B. blood vessel　　C. thymus gland　　D. muscle contraction
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】A**（据词根推断）

**myel/o-** = 脊髓或骨髓。选项中只有 spinal cord。myelosis 可指脊髓病或骨髓增生异常，本卷按「脊髓」选。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：myelo- 双义"}
- 脊髓：poliomyelitis 脊髓灰质炎，myelopathy。
- 骨髓：myeloid, myeloma, myelosuppression。
看搭配：-itis/-pathy 常是脊髓；血细胞/瘤常是骨髓。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
myel- 本卷选项 = **spinal cord**；同时记住「骨髓」义。
:::

### 第 15 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
endocardium（划线部分：endo-）

A. around　　B. outside　　C. through　　D. inside
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】D**（据词根推断）

**endo-** = 内。endocardium 心内膜。around = peri-，outside = exo-/ecto-，through = dia-/per-。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：心壁三层"}
| 词 | 前缀 | 部位 |
|----|------|------|
| endocardium | endo- 内 | 心内膜 |
| myocardium | myo- 肌 | 心肌 |
| epicardium / pericardium | epi-/peri- | 心外膜 / 心包 |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
endo- = **inside**；心内膜 endocardium。
:::

### 第 16 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
hemoptysis（划线部分：-ptysis）

A. clearing　　B. blocking　　C. vomiting　　D. spitting
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】D**（据词根推断）

**-ptysis** = 咯出 / 吐出。hemoptysis 咯血（从呼吸道咳出血液）。vomiting 是 -emesis（hematemesis 呕血，消化道）。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：咯血 vs 呕血"}
| 术语 | 后缀 | 来源 |
|------|------|------|
| hemoptysis | -ptysis 咯出 | 呼吸道 |
| hematemesis | -emesis 呕吐 | 消化道 |
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
-ptysis = **spitting**；hemoptysis = 咯血，不是呕血。
:::

### 第 17 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
gastroduodenal（划线部分：duoden-）

A. twelve　　B. twice　　C. twins　　D. twist
:::

::figure{src="/images/medical-english/exam-real-2021/p002-01.jpeg" alt="2021 医学英语试卷第 2 页" caption="第 2 页：第 17–20 题与 Part II Section A 第 21–30 题"}

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】A**（据词根推断）

duodenum（十二指肠）得名于约 **十二** 横指宽度（拉丁 duo + deni「十二」）。gastroduodenal = 胃十二指肠的。twice/twins 是「二」，不是本题词源。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：十二指肠词源"}
duodenum = twelve finger-breadths。duo- 在其他词可表「二」，但 duodenal 在医学英语考词源时选 **twelve**。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
duoden- 本卷 = **twelve**（十二指肠），不要选 twice。
:::

## 二、Part II Vocabulary（20%）

### Section A　选词填空（10%）

### 第 21 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
Though most would ____ any prejudice, physicians, like all humans, exhibit bias in their behaviors.

A. consent　　B. argue　　C. disavow　　D. derail
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】C**（据语义推断）

though 让步：多数人会 **否认 / 不承认** 自己有偏见，但医生仍会表现偏倚。disavow = 否认、拒绝承认。consent 同意，derail 使脱轨，argue 争论，皆不合。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：disavow"}
disavow = deny responsibility or support。近义：deny, disclaim, repudiate。反义：acknowledge。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
「嘴上否认偏见」= **disavow any prejudice**。
:::

### 第 22 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
Many professors devoted their precious time to ____ their medical knowledge gained from the clinical practice.

A. impart　　B. dictate　　C. breach　　D. antiquate
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】A**（据语义推断）

devote time to **impart** knowledge = 传授从临床获得的医学知识。dictate 口述/命令，breach 违反，antiquate 使过时。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：impart"}
impart knowledge / skills / information = 传授。Unit 1 课文亦有 impart knowledge upon the science of medicine。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
传授知识 = **impart** knowledge。
:::

### 第 23 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
This definition is well accepted because it ____ pinpoints the object and methods of social medicine as a discipline.

A. succinctly　　B. beneficially　　C. consecutively　　D. deliberately
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】A**（据 Unit 7 社会医学课文推断）

### Passage 1（第 41–45 题）

:::callout{kind="note" label="Passage 1 原文"}
Although they are an inexpensive supplier of vitamins, minerals, and high-quality proteins, eggs also contain a high level of blood cholesterol（胆固醇）, one of the major causes of heart disease. This knowledge has caused egg sales to fall in recent years. While egg producers claim that their product has been portrayed unfairly, the fact remains that one egg yolk contains a little more than two-thirds of the suggested daily cholesterol limit.

Because of the problems associated with egg consumption, several alternatives to eating eggs are available. One alternative is to eat substitute eggs. ### Passage 2（第 46–50 题）

:::callout{kind="note" label="Passage 2 原文"}
The immune system is the body's defender. It identifies, tracks down, and destroys troublemakers before they can hurt the body. Those troublemakers may be, for example, bacteria from a cut or splinter, a measles germ, a cold bug, or even a cancer cell.

These invaders try to take over our tissues and feed off our bodies' nutrients. If they succeed, we become sick or, sometimes, even die. It's the job of our immune systems to destroy these invaders before they destroy us.

Say you picked up a flu virus last week. Perhaps it entered your body through a cut, from a drinking glass, or from the air you breathe. Of course, you didn't feel it, but the virus made its way into your bloodstream. As soon as it entered your body, it began to reproduce. Viruses have only one goal: to take over your cells. Once inside your body, viruses try to enter cells and disrupt their normal work. If left alone, these viruses would hurt so many cells that you would weaken, or worse, get a serious illness.

But this flu virus should not be so complacent as it seems to be. As it reproduces in your bloodstream, the virus is met by a certain kind of white blood cell, the lymphocytes. The lymphocytes are the foot soldiers that keep you alive. The number of lymphocytes in your body is hard to imagine. Thousands of them could fit in the period at the end of this sentence. Your body holds about a trillion—that's 1,000,000,000,000 of them, or about 3,000 in every drop of blood. Since you began reading this sentence, over 800,000 of them have been created and destroyed.

Some of these lymphocytes pass through a small walnut（核桃）-sized organ called the thymus（胸腺）. The thymus is the base of the neck. Here, special hormones（荷尔蒙）turn lymphocytes into fighting cells, called T cells. T cells have one terrific talent: They can tell the difference between friend and foe; what should be in our bodies and what shouldn't. They do not affect the body's healthy cells. Yet they attack everything that is foreign to our bodies, such as germs, transplants, and even our own cells which have become abnormal, as in the case of cancer.
:::

### Passage 3（第 51–55 题）

:::callout{kind="note" label="Passage 3 原文"}
Cases of Lyme disease（莱姆关节炎）have been reported in at least thirty-two states and five continents since 1980. The disease, a bacterial infection, is transmitted by tiny deer ticks（扁虱）. The spiral shaped bacteria, or spirochetes, are picked up by the tick from white-tailed deer and white-footed mice. When the tick finds a human host to bite, the bacteria are passed on.

One of the difficulties in diagnosing the ailment is that many victims do not remember the tick bite, and the symptoms are similar to those of several other ailments. In the first stage of infection, the patient may observe a circular rash and experience flu like symptoms. Treatment with antibiotics at this point is very effective.

If the disease is untreated, it may affect the joints, heart, or brain. Many victims may believe they are suffering from arthritis. Fortunately, large doses of antibiotics may succeed in curing the disease even in its later stages.

Lyme disease was named after a town in Connecticut where some of the first cases diagnosed in the United States were found. Although the bacterium that causes the disease was discovered in 1982, many physicians still do not recognize its symptoms. The problem is compounded by the fact that many patients experience only minor symptoms at first and may not consult a physician until almost a month after the bite.

The disease seems to be spreading as herds of white-tailed deer increase in several states. As people travel with pets, particularly from states with a high incidence of the disease, they may unknowingly be transporting tiny ticks with them.

Some precautions can be taken. People who spend time in the woods or tall grass should wear long-sleeved shirts with snug-fitting cuffs（袖口）and should tuck pant legs into their socks to discourage ticks. Humans and animals should be checked for ticks on clothing and the body. The scalp（头皮）and hair should be examined carefully.

If you find an unattached tick, brush it off and burn it with a lighted match. Those that have already bitten should be removed gently and entirely with a pair of tweezers（镊子）. It may help to dab（涂）alcohol on the tick before you attempt to remove it. Wash the bitten area with soap and water or alcohol. A tick bite doesn't always result in Lyme disease, but watch for symptoms and consult a doctor should any appear.

### Passage 4（第 56–60 题）

:::callout{kind="note" label="Passage 4 原文"}
The word "normal", as applied to human body temperature, is a splendidly misleading description. The little arrow on the clinical thermometer, doggedly（顽强地）directed at 98.4F, gives extra support to it and the exactness of that point four of a decimal（小数）emphasizes it even further. The awkward figure is the fault of Gabriel Daniel Fahrenheit, the German physicist. Isaac Newton, a duodecimal enthusiast, had wanted 12 units between water's freezing and boiling. Fahrenheit's new thermometer could do better than that, permitting more units. He liked the idea of the lowest temperature (made with salt and ice) being 0, of water's freezing being 32, of the body being 96, and of water's boiling being 212, all of them nice mathematical numbers. Greater accuracy subsequently, plus an equal determination to keep freezing and boiling 180 degrees apart (another nice number) meant that something had to give—and body temperature did so.

Normally the majority of us do not have a "normal" temperature. Infants have a higher temperature; older people have a lower one. Ordinarily adults wake up with their temperature below normal and go to sleep with it above normal.

Women are 1F hotter after ovulation（排卵）. The armpit（腋窝）(or axilla) is 1F cooler than the mouth and the groin（腹股沟）, and cooler still in thin people. The rectum is almost a degree higher.
:::

not only to set an example to ... but also to protect those at the receiving end。antiquated 过时；utopian 空想的；receiving end = 患者一方。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
激进者要撤誓言 vs 作者：**金标准仍要**，榜样 + 保护患者。
:::

### 第 63 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
The ethical principle of autonomy would suggest that patients should not only always be fully informed, so that they can make the best possible decisions, but also because information helps them to make sense of and cope with illness.（3 分）
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】（参考译文；第 10 页印字淡，题干据 dump OCR 残句补全）**

自主性这一伦理原则提示：患者不仅应始终充分知情，以便做出尽可能好的决策，而且因为信息有助于他们理解并应对疾病。

autonomy → **充分知情**：决策 + 理解/应对疾病。

### 第 64 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
新誓言不再要求医生们执业前必须宣誓。相反，它更是一种行为守则，是年轻医生们进入医疗行业并承诺投身医疗的象征。（3 分）
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】（对齐 Unit 1 Text A 第 10 段）**

Unlike the original, the new oath does not require that doctors swear it before they can practise. Instead, it is a code of practice, and a symbol for young doctors entering the profession and their commitment to healing.

可接受近义：no longer requires physicians to take the oath before practising; rather, it is a code of conduct and a symbol of young doctors' entry into medicine and their commitment to healing.
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：新旧誓言"}
原文 oath：执业前必须宣誓。新誓言：code of practice + symbol of commitment，不强制先宣誓才能执业。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
新誓言 = **code of practice + symbol**，不是执业许可证。

### 第 65 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
对建立一个新的——更民主、较少等级划分——医疗服务模式的渴求并非最近才出现。有社会意识的医生们开始寻找在临床实践中能反映不同社会价值的方法。（3 分）
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】（据 Unit 7 Social Medicine 主题补译；第 10 页淡印）**

The desire for a new — more democratic, less hierarchical — model of health care is not of recent origin. Socially conscious physicians began to look for ways of reflecting different social values in their clinical practice.

关键词：democratic, less hierarchical, not recent, socially conscious, clinical practice。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：social medicine"}
Unit 7：社会医学共同原则；hierarchical 等级制（国会例句：Congress is collegial not hierarchical）。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
更民主、少等级的医疗模式 **并非新近才有**；社会意识医生把价值观带进临床。
:::

### 第 66 题

:::callout{kind="note" label="题目"}
关于患者可以恰当地接受多少信息、如何对患者解释信息的来龙去脉及含义、这些信息如何影响患者及家属应对疾病和选择决策，以及如何避免因提供过多细节而使患者惊慌失措或无所适从，医生常有心照不宣的判断。（4 分）
:::

:::callout{kind="insight" label="解析"}
**【答案】（与第 36 题 tacit judgements 同句族）**

Physicians regularly make tacit judgements about how much information patients can reasonably assimilate, how to explain the context and meaning of that information, how the information affects patients and families in coping with illness and making decisions, and how to avoid overwhelming or confusing patients with too much detail.

**采分**：tacit judgements；assimilate；context and meaning；cope / decision-making；avoid overwhelming。
:::

:::callout{kind="note" label="知识卡片：信息剂量"}
知情同意不是倾倒全部细节。医生对「可吸收量」做 tacit judgement，避免惊慌或无所适从（overwhelm / bewilder）。
:::

:::callout{kind="tip" label="结论速记"}
医生对「说多少、怎么说」做 **tacit judgements**；对应第 36 题 implicit。
:::
