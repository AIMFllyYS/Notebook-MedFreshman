:::example{label="EX02 · Virchow, AIDS and intellectual property — Set 2"}

## 阅读原文

> Rudolf Virchow is considered by many to be the founder of social medicine. He contributed notably to the understanding of disease at the cellular level, and was also keenly aware of the social origins of disease. Investigating a typhus epidemic in 1848, he argued that poverty, lack of education and lack of democracy were key. This experience produced his concept of "artificial epidemics" during social unrest.
>
> When we consider the AIDS pandemic, his words look so prescient. Social inequalities and disruptions have been central to the spread of HIV. Rapid identification of the pathogen and highly effective treatments are a miracle of modern biomedicine. In the United States, AIDS is largely treatable, though still not curable. But it is a great outrage — and also characteristic of modern medicine — that most people who need the medications are denied access to them. The "cocktail" cost about US$10,000 a year, but the U.S. government long used the WTO to limit poor countries' ability to produce and buy generic drugs. The rights of pharmaceutical corporations to their "intellectual property" have trumped public health.
>
> In the late nineteenth century, striking advances in pathology and microbiology made social factors seem less germane in the etiology of disease.

---

**1.** The passage is mainly about __________.

A. Virchow as only a laboratory pathologist with no social theory

B. how a social reading of epidemics, from typhus to AIDS, collides with patent power

C. proving that AIDS is now curable worldwide

D. arguing that microbiology was a mistake

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：细胞病理 + 社会起源；人为流行病；艾滋病可治却不可及；知识产权 trump 公共卫生。

**A 错误**：also keenly aware of the social origins。

**C 错误**：treatable, though still not curable；多数需要者得不到药。

**D 错误**：miracle of modern biomedicine 被承认，问题在可及性。

---

**2.** Virchow's "artificial epidemics" refers to epidemics __________.

A. invented by journalists

B. whose development is driven by social conditions such as poverty and lack of democracy during unrest

C. that exist only in cells, never in populations

D. cured solely by intellectual property law

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：poverty, lack of education and lack of democracy ... "artificial epidemics" during social unrest。

**A 错误**：不是新闻虚构。

**C 错误**：他同时做细胞水平与社会起源。

**D 错误**：专利是后文批判对象。

---

**3.** The word "prescient" is closest in meaning to __________.

A. old-fashioned and wrong

B. showing foresight

C. purely laboratory-based

D. hostile to the poor

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：his words look so prescient 接到艾滋病大流行仍由社会不平等驱动，即有先见。

**A 错误**：look so prescient 是肯定。

**C、D 错误**：脱离 pre- + scient 与语境。

---

**4.** It can be inferred that the author thinks the main barrier to AIDS medicines for the poor is __________.

A. the biological impossibility of generic production

B. not simply price, but political protection of corporate intellectual property

C. Virchow's refusal to identify a pathogen

D. a lack of any effective cocktail

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：The answer is not simply the price ... WTO ... "intellectual property" have trumped public health。

**A 错误**：正是限制生产和购买 generic drugs。

**C 错误**：病原体已被迅速确认。

**D 错误**：cocktail 存在，只是约每年一万美元且可及性被限制。

---

**5.** Calling social factors "less germane" after the rise of microbiology reflects __________.

A. the author's own final view that society does not matter

B. a historical shift the author regrets, because it sidelined social etiology

C. a legal requirement of the WTO

D. Virchow's rejection of the microscope

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**态度 / 推理题**。

**正确答案 B**：seem less germane 是十九世纪末建制医学的观感；全文在反对这种「显微镜让社会退场」。

**A 错误**：作者立场相反。

**C 错误**：WTO 出现在专利段。

**D 错误**：Virchow 对细胞水平有突出贡献。
