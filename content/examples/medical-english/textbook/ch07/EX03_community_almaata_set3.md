:::example{label="EX03 · Community care and Alma-Ata — Set 3"}

## 阅读原文

> Indeed, if politics is medicine writ large, it is also apparent that medicine is politics writ small. Community-oriented primary care included innovations based on the principles of social medicine. Clinical care became a team responsibility: a primary-care doctor, a community nurse, and a health educator from the community. The team served neighbouring families they knew, treating "patients" as families rather than isolated individuals, and systematically considered the broader social context and the epidemiologic meaning of a new diagnosis.
>
> In the 1960s the U.S. Office of Economic Opportunity established two community health centres: Columbia Point in Boston and Mound Bayou, Mississippi. The former was founded by Jack Geiger, who had worked with Sidney Kark in South Africa. Many ideas of the movement are embodied in the Declaration of Alma-Ata (1978), which reaffirmed the WHO definition of health as "a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being, and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity." Existing gross inequality was declared unacceptable; people have the right to participate; primary care should be universal.
>
> Practitioners in Latin America have been involved with community organisations, unions, and political movements; many fell victim to political repression.

---

**1.** The main idea is that social medicine __________.

A. stays in theory and never changes clinics

B. reshapes clinical work as team-based, family- and community-oriented care, later echoed at Alma-Ata

C. requires abandoning epidemiology

D. applies only in Latin America

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：团队、家庭、社会环境与流行病学含义；美国两个中心；阿拉木图定义健康。

**A 错误**：innovations based on the principles 已进入诊所。

**C 错误**：epidemiologic meaning of a new diagnosis 被保留。

**D 错误**：美国中心 + 南非 Kark + 拉丁美洲并列。

---

**2.** According to Alma-Ata as quoted, health is __________.

A. merely the absence of disease or infirmity

B. complete physical, mental and social well-being, not merely the absence of disease or infirmity

C. identical with GDP growth

D. a private good sold only by insurers

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：原文完整定义，not merely 是否定仅仅无病。

**A 错误**：正是被 not merely 排除的窄定义。

**C、D 错误**：无此内容。

---

**3.** "Writ large / writ small" in the opening sentence is used to suggest that __________.

A. handwriting in medical records must be enlarged

B. politics and medicine scale the same issues at different magnitudes

C. only large hospitals count as political

D. Latin is required in every prescription

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：politics is medicine writ large ... medicine is politics writ small = 同一套问题的放大与缩小。

**A 错误**：字面化 handwriting。

**C 错误**：诊所里的交付方式也有 political ramifications（课文原意）。

**D 错误**：与 writ 的法律/修辞义无关。

---

**4.** It can be inferred that a traditional solo family doctor, as contrasted here, would be less likely to __________.

A. see the patient at all

B. systematically read a new diagnosis against neighbourhood epidemiology and household context

C. prescribe any drug

D. work in Boston

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：Unlike the traditional family doctor, the team systematically considered the broader social context and the epidemiologic meaning。

**A、C 错误**：对比点不是「看病/开药」。

**D 错误**：Boston 是社区中心地点，不是传统医生的对立面。

---

**5.** The author's mention of political repression in Latin America is meant to __________.

A. joke about unions

B. underline that praxis in social medicine has carried political risk, not only academic debate

C. claim that Alma-Ata caused the repression

D. deny that community organisations existed

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**写作目的题**。

**正确答案 B**：involved with ... political movements; many fell victim to political repression。

**A 错误**：语气严肃。

**C 错误**：阿拉木图 1978，拉丁美洲实践是二十世纪更长的历史。

**D 错误**：明确写了 community organisations。
