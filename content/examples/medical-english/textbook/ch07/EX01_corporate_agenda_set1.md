:::example{label="EX01 · Corporate agenda and three principles — Set 1"}

## 阅读原文

> The past two decades have seen a rapid expansion of the corporate agenda in the field of health and health care. Rather than moving toward a system of universal access to medical care in the United States, the access to and quality of clinical services is being turned over increasingly to the insurance industry. Clinicians and patients alike are wooed by sophisticated advertising campaigns (often disguised as education) that promote expensive drugs of dubious efficacy. The insertion of "market rationality" into health care has not brought the hoped-for curbing of health care costs. Although the United States spends more per capita on health care than any other country, it still ranks poorly on many health indicators — 27th in the world in life expectancy at birth.
>
> This corporate agenda, however, has not gone unchallenged. The systematic study of society, disease and medicine became known as "social medicine". Certain common principles underlie the term: social and economic conditions profoundly impact health, disease, and the practice of medicine; the health of the population is a matter of social concern; society should promote health through both individual and social means.

---

**1.** The author's main purpose in paragraph 1 is to __________.

A. celebrate U.S. life expectancy as proof that markets work

B. criticise the corporate capture of care, research and cost control despite record spending

C. argue that advertising is the most reliable form of medical education

D. deny that insurance companies influence access

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：corporate agenda、交给保险业、伪装成教育的广告、market rationality 未控费、花得最多却第 27。

**A 错误**：27th 是差，不是庆祝。

**C 错误**：disguised as education 是贬义。

**D 错误**：turned over increasingly to the insurance industry。

---

**2.** "Has not gone unchallenged" implies that __________.

A. nobody has ever criticised the corporate agenda

B. the corporate agenda has in fact been opposed

C. social medicine has been abolished

D. advertising campaigns were illegal

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节 / 词义题**。

**正确答案 B**：双重否定 = 已被挑战，并过渡到 social medicine。

**A 错误**：与字面相反。

**C 错误**：下文正要定义社会医学。

**D 错误**：文中未谈合法性。

---

**3.** The word "dubious" in "drugs of dubious efficacy" is closest in meaning to __________.

A. guaranteed by the NIH

B. doubtful or questionable

C. inexpensive

D. traditional and herbal only

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：与 expensive、advertising disguised as education 连用，疗效可疑。

**A 错误**：NIH 在文中是被制药投资超过的对照。

**C 错误**：drugs 是 expensive。

**D 错误**：无此限制。

---

**4.** It can be inferred that spending more per capita __________.

A. automatically produces the world's best life expectancy

B. is, in this argument, not a sufficient measure of a health system's success

C. has already curbed costs as hoped

D. proves social medicine is unnecessary

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：Although ... spends more ... ranks poorly ... 27th。

**A 错误**：与 27th 矛盾。

**C 错误**：has not brought the hoped-for curbing。

**D 错误**：下段用社会医学挑战公司议程。

---

**5.** The author's attitude toward "market rationality" in health care is __________.

A. enthusiastic

B. disappointed and critical, using quotation marks to distance the phrase

C. purely technical, with no value judgment

D. nostalgic for nineteenth-century advertising

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：insertion of "market rationality" ... has not brought the hoped-for curbing；引号表示距离。

**A、C 错误**：hoped-for 落空是价值判断。

**D 错误**：广告是当代批判对象。
