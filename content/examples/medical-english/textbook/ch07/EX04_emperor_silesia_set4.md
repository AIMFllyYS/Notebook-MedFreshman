## 阅读原文

> Sometimes it is useful to state the obvious. Two decades of "market reform" in U.S. health care have not given all Americans affordable, quality health care, nor is it likely to do so. This emperor has no clothes. HIV treatment for all who need it could be supplied for a tiny fraction of what the U.S. government has spent to pursue Saddam Hussein and his nonexistent weapons of mass destruction. Clinicians know the lives of their patients intimately and thus are uniquely suited to understand the political and social dimensions of their patients' problems. Virchow put it compactly: physicians are the natural attorneys of the poor. This mission has been recognised by the Nobel Peace Prize to Doctors without Borders in 1999 and to International Physicians for the Prevention of Nuclear War in 1985.
>
> More doctors, more drugs, more quality measures, more computers, more audits, and faster discharges will not be enough. A fundamental rethinking of the social role of medicine is required. Virchow's prescription for the typhus epidemic in Upper Silesia is more pertinent than ever: education, with its daughters liberty and prosperity.

---

**1.** The phrase "This emperor has no clothes" is used to __________.

A. discuss a paediatric dermatology case

B. satirise market reform as an exposed failure

C. praise U.S. affordable care for all

D. argue that emperors should run hospitals

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨 / 修辞目的题**。

**正确答案 B**：紧接 market reform have not given ... nor is it likely to do so。安徒生典故 = 讽喻失败。

**A 错误**：不是皮肤科病例。

**C 错误**：与 have not given 相反。

**D 错误**：字面化皇帝。

---

**2.** According to the author, clinicians are "uniquely suited" to social analysis because they __________.

A. earn more than lawyers

B. know patients' lives intimately

C. always agree with pharmaceutical firms

D. avoid politics by definition

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：Clinicians know the lives of their patients intimately and thus are uniquely suited...

**A 错误**：natural attorneys 是比喻，不是收入比较。

**C、D 错误**：与 political and social dimensions 相反。

---

**3.** In the last sentence, "its daughters" refers to daughters of __________.

A. typhus

B. education

C. Saddam Hussein

D. quality measures

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义 / 指代题**。

**正确答案 B**：education, with its daughters liberty and prosperity。所有格 its = education。

**A 错误**：typhus 是要防止的对象。

**C、D 错误**：来自其他句子。

---

**4.** It can be inferred that adding more computers and faster discharges __________.

A. is the author's complete reform package

B. would, without rethinking medicine's social role, still fail to solve U.S. health problems as framed here

C. is condemned as illegal

D. was Virchow's 1848 prescription

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：more doctors, more drugs, more ... will not be enough. A fundamental rethinking ... is required。

**A 错误**：清单被当作不够。

**C 错误**：未谈非法。

**D 错误**：魏尔肖的药方是教育及其女儿。

---

**5.** The author's attitude toward physicians as "attorneys of the poor" is __________.

A. mocking

B. endorsing, citing Virchow and Nobel-recognised missions as models

C. claiming that doctors should replace all lawyers in court

D. arguing that poor patients need no advocates

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：put it compactly 是肯定引用；Nobel 承认 this mission。

**A 错误**：无嘲讽标记。

**C 错误**：attorneys 是比喻。

**D 错误**：与 natural attorneys 相反。
