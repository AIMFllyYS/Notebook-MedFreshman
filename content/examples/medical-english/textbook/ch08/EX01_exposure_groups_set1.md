## 阅读原文

> The September 11, 2001 terrorist attacks exposed many people to horrific events: planes crashing, people falling, the collapse of the WTC towers; the dust cloud; injury and death. Additionally, the collapse exposed many to substances shown to adversely affect health. Routine monitoring equipment in lower Manhattan was destroyed, which made it difficult to quantify exposure to the massive dust cloud and to toxic substances that permeated the clean-up site, residences, and workplaces. The dust contained powdered concrete, gypsum, glass fibres, chrysotile asbestos, PAHs, steel, and metals. It was highly alkaline and rich in particulate matter capable of causing respiratory irritation and damage. PVC-coated cable was incinerated, releasing dioxins and other chlorinated organics.
>
> Studies have generally focused on three populations: directly exposed groups (rescue and recovery workers, volunteers, lower Manhattan residents, area workers, school staff and students, passers-by); those indirectly exposed in New York City; and those indirectly exposed throughout the USA and internationally. This review investigates short- and medium-term health effects to establish what has been learned, and to identify areas in which further research is needed.

---

**1.** The passage is primarily concerned with __________.

A. listing every chemical in Manhattan before 2001

B. framing 9/11 health research as both traumatic and toxic exposure, poorly quantified at first, across three populations

C. proving that dioxins were the only hazard

D. arguing that further research is unnecessary

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：看见/失去 + 碱性尘与毒物；监测被毁故定量难；三组人群；短中期综述目的。

**A 错误**：成分表是倒塌后的尘，不是 2001 年前全城化学品。

**C 错误**：尘是混合物，二噁英只是电缆焚烧一条。

**D 错误**：identify areas in which further research is needed。

---

**2.** Why was exposure hard to quantify in the first days?

A. No dust was generated.

B. Routine environmental monitors were damaged and destroyed, and sampling for several toxins began only from 14–21 September.

C. All residents left the United States.

D. Alkaline dust cannot be sampled in principle.

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：equipment ... destroyed ... difficult to quantify。完整课文写采样 9 月 14–21 日才开始。

**A 错误**：massive dust cloud。

**C、D 错误**：无此信息。

---

**3.** "Permeated" in paragraph 1 is closest in meaning to __________.

A. legally banned

B. spread through and filled

C. scientifically disproved

D. photographed only

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：toxic substances that permeated the clean-up site, residences, and workplaces = 弥漫、渗透这些空间。

**A、C、D 错误**：与地点宾语不搭配。

---

**4.** A commuter who watched the attacks only on television in another state would, in this classification, belong to __________.

A. the directly exposed rescue-worker group

B. the indirectly exposed national/international group

C. a fourth group of unexposed chemists

D. the lower Manhattan resident group

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：第三组 those indirectly exposed throughout the USA and internationally。后文全国媒体暴露走这一组。

**A、D 错误**：直接暴露要在现场或下曼哈顿等名单里。

**C 错误**：综述只有三组。

---

**5.** The author's tone toward the destroyed monitors is __________.

A. triumphant, as if missing data were a scientific success

B. explanatory, treating the gap as a limit on later dose estimates

C. accusing residents of stealing the equipment

D. uninterested in environmental measurement

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：which made it difficult to quantify 是方法学限制，不是胜利。

**A、C、D 错误**：与综述口吻不符。
