:::example{label="EX03 · Firefighters, WTC cough and FEV1 — Set 3"}

## 阅读原文

> Respiratory illness began on a large scale immediately after 9/11. Thousands continue to have long-term problems. Overall incidence is hard to assess because most study populations contain self-selected subsets. Firefighters provide a more complete cohort: a documented roster with mandatory examinations about every 18 months.
>
> In the first year after 9/11, 54% of 10 378 firefighters had a daily cough (vs 4% the year before); it persisted in 17% in the second year. Sore throats were 62% vs 3%; rhinosinusitis 45% vs 4%. Sore throat generally subsided, but rhinosinusitis remained above 40% by year four. Dyspnoea (40% vs 3%) and wheeze (34% vs 1%) rose and stayed high. Arrival time, months at the site, first-year symptoms, current smoking, and older age predicted lower-respiratory symptoms at four years.
>
> Spirometry a median of 3 months after 9/11 in 12 079 FDNY responders showed an average FEV1 drop of 372 mL from the last pre-9/11 test — equal to about 12 years of normal ageing. After the first-year drop, function stabilised; most had normal, albeit diminished, FEV1. Over the next six years, never-smokers' adjusted FEV1 fell 26 mL per year. Earlier arrival predicted the first-year drop but not FEV1 at the end of follow-up, suggesting exposure predicted initial illness while host factors affected the long-term course.

---

**1.** Why do the authors prefer the firefighter cohort?

A. Firefighters never cough.

B. A roster and regular mandatory exams reduce the self-selection problem that plagues other samples.

C. They arrived last and had no dust exposure.

D. Spirometry is illegal in other occupations.

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨 / 细节题**。

**正确答案 B**：self-selected subsets vs documented roster ... mandatory medical examinations。

**A 错误**：54% daily cough。

**C 错误**：到达时间正是暴露代理。

**D 错误**：无此信息。

---

**2.** Which symptom remained above 40% by the fourth year?

A. sore throat in most firefighters

B. reported rhinosinusitis

C. daily cough at the first-year 54% level

D. FEV1 decline of 372 mL per year every year

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：rhinosinusitis remained at more than 40% by the fourth year。咽痛对多数人消退；咳嗽第二年 17%；372 mL 是第一刀，其后从不吸烟者约 26 mL/年。

**A、C、D 错误**：与数字时间窗不符。

---

**3.** "Albeit diminished" describes FEV1 that is __________.

A. far below any normal range for almost all workers

B. still largely in the normal range, though lower than each person's pre-disaster value

C. higher than before 9/11

D. no longer measurable

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：most having normal, albeit diminished, FEV1 compared with pre-disaster levels。

**A 错误**：normal 被保留。

**C 错误**：diminished。

**D 错误**：仍在做肺量计随访。

---

**4.** The finding that early arrival predicted year-one FEV1 loss but not final FEV1 suggests that __________.

A. dust was imaginary

B. dose shaped the acute hit, while later slope depended more on host factors such as genomics or other exposures

C. smoking protected lung function

D. mandatory exams should stop

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：exposure predicted initial illness, but ... host factors such as genomics or non-WTC exposures affected the long-term course。

**A 错误**：与 372 mL 客观下降矛盾。

**C 错误**：smokers ... steeper rate of decline。

**D 错误**：队列价值正来自强制体检。

---

**5.** The authors' attitude toward self-reported symptoms without spirometry is __________.

A. they reject all symptom reports as worthless

B. they treat symptoms as necessary but incomplete, and use FEV1 as complementary objective evidence

C. they claim FEV1 can diagnose PTSD

D. they prefer television surveys to lung-function tests

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：Pulmonary function data provide objective physiological evidence ... and complement symptom reporting（完整课文句）。本段同时大段引用症状率。

**A 错误**：没有抛弃症状。

**C、D 错误**：张冠李戴。
