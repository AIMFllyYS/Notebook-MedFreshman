## 阅读原文

> Most WTC investigators used screening instruments such as the PTSD checklist. High scores are often referred to as probable PTSD because these instruments are not diagnostic. Measurements 3–5 days after 9/11 suggested that 44% of the adult US population experienced substantial stress. Risk factors for post-traumatic stress symptoms up to four years later included pre-event psychopathology, female sex, recent immigration, and increased hours of viewing event-related media. Individuals outside the New York metropolitan area were significantly less likely to have probable PTSD two months later than those inside it. A 2004–05 survey of nearly 35 000 US adults showed that those with indirect experience (e.g., television) had the lowest risk of PTSD (1%) among 32 listed traumatic events.
>
> One to two months after 9/11, among 988 residents of Manhattan south of 110th Street, PTSD prevalence was 7.5% and depression about 9%. Peri-event panic, loss of possessions, and job loss were risk factors for PTSD; low social support was a risk factor for depression. Both prevalences had decreased by six months. Directly exposed groups had higher rates, including chronic and delayed-onset PTSD. Some studies found no rise in suicide; others found a decrease, perhaps because of greater communal support.

---

**1.** The central caution of paragraph 1 is that __________.

A. every high checklist score is a lifetime clinical diagnosis of PTSD

B. screening scores indicate probable PTSD, while national stress and television exposure must not be confused with Manhattan's diagnosed-level burden

C. nobody outside New York felt any stress

D. suicide always rises after terrorism

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：probable PTSD；44% 是实质压力；电视间接 1% 为 32 种创伤最低；曼哈顿 7.5%。

**A 错误**：not diagnostic。

**C 错误**：44% 全国实质压力。

**D 错误**：自杀有的研究无变化、有的下降。

---

**2.** Which factor is listed as a risk for depression rather than for PTSD in the Manhattan study?

A. peri-event panic attacks

B. loss of possessions

C. job loss

D. low social support

**Answer: D**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 D**：low social support was a risk factor for depression。前三项是 PTSD 风险。

**A、B、C 错误**：原文明确归给 PTSD。

---

**3.** "Probable PTSD" means __________.

A. a courtroom verdict of insanity

B. a high screening score, not a clinician's diagnostic confirmation

C. a symptom that never fades

D. the same as substantial stress in 44% of adults

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义题**。

**正确答案 B**：High scores ... probable PTSD because these instruments are not diagnostic。

**A 错误**：不是法律裁决。

**C 错误**：6 个月患病率下降。

**D 错误**：44% 是 substantial stress，不是 PTSD。

---

**4.** It can be inferred that watching many hours of attack coverage __________.

A. had no statistical relationship with later stress symptoms

B. was associated with more post-traumatic stress symptoms, yet television-only exposure still ranked as a low-PTSD event nationally

C. produced a 7.5% PTSD rate in every U.S. county

D. proved that media are more toxic than dust

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：increased hours of viewing 是四年内症状风险因素；同时电视间接经历在 32 种创伤里 PTSD 风险最低（1%）。

**A 错误**：与风险因素句矛盾。

**C 错误**：7.5% 是曼哈顿南段居民。

**D 错误**：课文没有做这种总比较。

---

**5.** The author's attitude toward a post-9/11 suicide "epidemic" claim would most likely be __________.

A. fully supportive, citing a proven nationwide surge

B. cautious or contrary, noting no change or even a decrease possibly tied to communal support

C. sarcastic about all mental-health research

D. focused only on firefighters' cough

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：no significant change ... others note a significant decrease ... communal support。

**A 错误**：没有「已证实全国自杀潮」。

**C、D 错误**：超出本段。
