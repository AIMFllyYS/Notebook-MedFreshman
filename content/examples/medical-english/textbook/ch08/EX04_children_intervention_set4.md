:::example{label="EX04 · Children and disaster interventions — Set 4"}

## 阅读原文

> The mental health effects of 9/11 have not been as well studied in children as in adults. In 8236 New York City students aged 9–21 years, six months after 9/11, 29% had at least one of six anxiety or depressive disorders. The most prevalent were probable agoraphobia (15%), separation anxiety (12%), and PTSD (11%). Higher rates were associated with greater exposure (proximity, injury, dust cloud, family death or injury), female sex, younger age (9–10 years), and previous trauma. Children's reaction severity correlated with parental distress (parental post-traumatic stress and crying in front of the child) and with graphic television images. Children with peri-event panic were more likely to have probable PTSD 6–9 months later. Among 116 children aged 5 and younger in lower Manhattan, those with other trauma history had more anxiety, depression and sleep problems 2–4 years later.
>
> Interventions that might reduce short- and medium-term physical and mental effects include immediate outreach; screening and treatment of high-risk groups with targeted, exposure-specific interventions to prevent long-term mental health sequelae; early and consistent use of appropriate respirators when hazards cannot be controlled; and preparedness training and shift rotations so rescue workers work shorter durations.

---

**1.** What is the main point about children?

A. Child PTSD was the only disorder found, at 29%.

B. A large school sample showed widespread anxiety-spectrum burden, with PTSD not even the most common, and parents and media as additional pathways.

C. Children were unaffected if they were under 5.

D. Graphic television protected children from fear.

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：29% 至少一种；广场恐怖 15% > 分离焦虑 12% > PTSD 11%；父母悲痛与电视画面相关。

**A 错误**：29% 不是 PTSD 单病。

**C 错误**：5 岁及以下仍有 2–4 年后症状，尤其有其他创伤史者。

**D 错误**：graphic images 与严重程度正相关。

---

**2.** Which disorder was the most prevalent in the 8236-student sample?

A. PTSD at 29%

B. probable agoraphobia at 15%

C. schizophrenia

D. COPD

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：The most prevalent were probable agoraphobia (15%)...

**A 错误**：29% 是至少一种障碍；PTSD 是 11%。

**C、D 错误**：未出现。

---

**3.** "Sequelae" in the intervention paragraph refers to __________.

A. opening ceremonies

B. after-effects or later complications of the mental trauma

C. respirators themselves

D. shift rotations as a disease

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：prevent long-term mental health sequelae = 预防长期精神健康后遗症。

**A、C、D 错误**：把干预手段误当成后遗症。

---

**4.** From the correlation with parental crying, one can infer that __________.

A. children's recovery is independent of adult caregivers

B. treating or supporting distressed parents may be part of protecting children

C. children should watch more unfiltered footage with their parents

D. only dust, not family, counts as exposure

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：severity ... positively correlated with parental distress。暴露复合变量已含家人伤亡；父母当面哭泣是第二条通路。

**A 错误**：与相关相反。

**C 错误**：graphic images 是风险，不是处方。

**D 错误**：复合暴露含 family death or injury。

---

**5.** The authors' stance on respirators and shift rotation is __________.

A. optional folklore

B. practical, listing them beside mental-health outreach as measures that might reduce short- and medium-term harm

C. a claim that respirators cure PTSD

D. opposition to any screening

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：Interventions that might reduce ... include ... respirators ... shift rotations。情态是 might，清单并列。

**A 错误**：写进综述建议。

**C 错误**：呼吸器针对环境危害，PTSD 走筛查治疗。

**D 错误**：明确写 screening and treatment。
