:::example{label="EX03 · Relational autonomy and distrust — Set 3"}

## 阅读原文

> The physician should consider whether information will enhance or diminish the patient's sense of autonomy. If information makes illness clearer or more controllable, the patient can decide according to his or her values. Yet more is not always better. Pouring out every available fact may overwhelm patients and paradoxically weaken informed decision-making. Capacity to use information also depends on relationships with physicians, family and others. If patients do not trust the physician, they often seek still more information. If they must sort complex facts alone, they may feel abandoned. The ideal may be "relational autonomy": patients, those close to them, and physicians work together to find information, interpret its context and meaning, and act on it.
>
> Ideally each patient is considered as an individual — capacity, education, values, wish to know. In practice, physicians must watch for conscious or unconscious bias.

---

**1.** "Relational autonomy" in this essay means __________.

A. the patient must decide in isolation after receiving a data dump

B. patients, intimates and clinicians jointly find, interpret and act on information

C. only relatives may hear the diagnosis

D. autonomy has been abolished

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨 / 词义题**。

**正确答案 B**：work together to find ... interpret ... and act on it。

**A 错误**：sort complex facts alone → feel abandoned，正是要避免的。

**C 错误**：三方合作，不是只告诉家属。

**D 错误**：仍在增强或削弱 sense of autonomy 的框架里。

---

**2.** According to the passage, distrust of the physician often leads patients to __________.

A. stop asking questions

B. seek still more information

C. automatically recover

D. refuse all relatives

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：If patients do not trust the physician, they often seek still more information。

**A 错误**：方向相反。

**C、D 错误**：无此信息。

---

**3.** The word "paradoxically" signals that __________.

A. more information always increases autonomy, as expected

B. the outcome (weaker decisions) runs against the naive expectation that more facts mean more autonomy

C. physics has entered the clinic

D. the authors reject all science

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：overwhelm ... paradoxically weaken their ability to make informed decisions。

**A 错误**：与 weaken 相反。

**C、D 错误**：修辞词，不是学科声明。

---

**4.** It can be inferred that handing a patient a complete printout and leaving the room __________.

A. fulfils relational autonomy

B. risks the "abandoned with complex facts" pattern the authors warn against

C. is required by beneficence in every case

D. eliminates bias

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：must sort complex facts alone ... feel abandoned and unable to exercise control。

**A 错误**：relational 需要 together。

**C 错误**：有利原则在本课包含不倾倒信息。

**D 错误**：偏颇是另一段要交叉核对的问题。

---

**5.** The authors' attitude toward "ideal individualised judgment" is __________.

A. they deny that ideals matter

B. they endorse it, while warning that practice is biased unless one watches for prejudice

C. they prefer stereotypes as more efficient

D. they claim education level should be ignored

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：Ideally each patient is considered as an individual ... In practice ... watch for ... bias。教育水平被列为应考虑的个体因素。

**A、C 错误**：与 Ideally 相反。

**D 错误**：education 在个体化清单里。
