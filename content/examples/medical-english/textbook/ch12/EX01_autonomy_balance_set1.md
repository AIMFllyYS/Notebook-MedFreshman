:::example{label="EX01 · Autonomy balanced with benefit and harm — Set 1"}

## 阅读原文

> As clinicians who strongly value truth telling and active patient involvement, we have been reflecting on circumstances in which physicians withhold information about conditions, treatments, and outcomes. The ethical principle of autonomy would suggest that patients should always be fully informed, so that they can make the best possible decisions and make sense of illness. However, since information can sometimes increase cognitive and emotional burden and lead to greater confusion rather than clarity, autonomy needs to be balanced against beneficence and non-maleficence. Physicians also judge whether time spent providing facts will interfere with other important work, such as decision-making or promoting adherence.
>
> Clinicians and ethicists readily accept withholding when patients lack cognitive capacity (someone else is informed); when urgent intervention is needed, as in a ruptured aneurysm; and when patients explicitly or implicitly refuse information (a proxy may be told). But when patients directly request information, or when patients and families disagree, the decision to withhold must be reconsidered.

---

**1.** The authors' main claim is that __________.

A. truth-telling should be abandoned

B. autonomy remains central, yet information can harm, so disclosure is a balanced clinical judgment with specific exceptions and brakes

C. families should always decide instead of patients

D. aneurysms should never be treated until a ten-page consent is signed

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：重视实情相告；autonomy would suggest always → however burden；三种可接受情形 + 两刹车。

**A 错误**：strongly value truth telling。

**C 错误**：患者与家属意见相左时必须重新考虑隐瞒。

**D 错误**：破裂动脉瘤正是早期行动利大于延迟告知。

---

**2.** Which situation requires that withholding be reconsidered?

A. The patient lacks any cognitive capacity and a surrogate is informed.

B. A ruptured aneurysm needs immediate action.

C. The patient directly asks for the information.

D. The patient repeatedly changes the subject and a proxy is informed while reasons are explored.

**Answer: C**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 C**：when patients directly request information ... must be reconsidered。

**A、B、D 错误**：这三项是「容易接受」的隐瞒/延迟情形。

---

**3.** "Non-maleficence" in this passage refers to __________.

A. maximising hospital profit

B. the duty not to harm, including not overloading the patient with confusing information

C. never performing surgery

D. hiding all diagnoses forever

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义题**。

**正确答案 B**：课文括号 doing no harm；与 cognitive and emotional burden 相连。

**A 错误**：无此义。

**C 错误**：急症干预被允许。

**D 错误**：与 truth telling 和两刹车矛盾。

---

**4.** It can be inferred that spending a long time listing facts __________.

A. is always the most ethical use of a visit

B. might, in the authors' view, crowd out decision-making and adherence work

C. is legally required in every aneurysm

D. proves the patient has no autonomy

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：whether time spent providing facts will interfere with ... decision-making or promoting adherence。

**A 错误**：时间被写成有机会成本。

**C 错误**：动脉瘤例是延迟告知代价更大。

**D 错误**：自主仍是原则之一。

---

**5.** The authors' attitude toward autonomy as an absolute "always fully inform" rule is __________.

A. unqualified endorsement

B. respectful but qualified: the principle is real, the "always" is not

C. mockery of all ethics

D. indifference

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：would suggest that patients should always be fully informed ... However ... needs to be balanced。

**A 错误**：However 限制 always。

**C、D 错误**：他们仍 value truth telling。
