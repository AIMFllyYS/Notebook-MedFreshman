## 阅读原文

> Although most people deny their own prejudices, physicians show discrimination in behaviour. Even after accounting for education and socio-economic status, they tend to give less information to Black patients than to white patients. In time-pressured care, "clinical judgment" can become a reason to withhold information in order to avoid time-consuming work. Economic incentives generally reward intervention and discourage talk about the limits of treatment. Some physicians omit alternatives simply to avoid conflict.
>
> When information offers no clear benefit and the patient has not asked, physicians should not disclose what is likely to be unbearable. Excess, especially irrelevant, information can crowd out important discussions. Withholding or delaying potentially important information may be appropriate only as a special case. Simple principles: if the patient asks, tell the truth. If you are anxious that the patient will discover the withholding, reconsider. Ask what a trusted colleague would think, whether you are uneasy, whether you assume the patient shares your values. Know the patient as a person. When uncertain, discuss with a trusted person in the patient's circle, or an experienced colleague, or both.

---

**1.** The last movement of the essay is best summarised as __________.

A. a licence to lie whenever convenient

B. a set of brakes on withholding, plus a warning that bias and payment systems distort "clinical judgment"

C. a claim that Black patients want less information by nature

D. an argument that colleagues should never be consulted

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：种族偏颇、激励、怕吵架是坏的少说；问则告、怕拆穿则重想、拿不准商量。

**A 错误**：special case；if the patient asks, tell。

**C 错误**：less information 是医生行为歧视，不是患者本性。

**D 错误**：uncertain → discuss with colleague。

---

**2.** The racial disparity in information-giving is presented as __________.

A. explained away entirely by education and income

B. persisting even after those factors are accounted for

C. a myth with no empirical edge in the essay

D. a reason to stop talking to white patients

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：even after accounting for education and socio-economic status, ... less information to Black patients。

**A 错误**：even after 表示未被完全解释。

**C 错误**：作者当作实存偏颇。

**D 错误**：目标是克服偏颇，不是报复。

---

**3.** "Crowd out" in "crowd out important discussions" is closest in meaning to __________.

A. financially sponsor

B. push aside by taking up the time and attention

C. translate into Latin

D. legally require

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：Excess information ... can crowd out important discussions = 挤掉。

**A、C、D 错误**：与「过度知情」语境不符。

---

**4.** Anxiety that the patient will discover the secret is, for these authors, a signal to __________.

A. hide the file more carefully

B. reconsider the decision to withhold

C. bill an extra intervention

D. ask only the insurance company

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：If you are anxious about the consequences of the patient discovering that information was withheld, the decision to withhold should be reconsidered。心虚本身是刹车。

**A 错误**：与 tell the truth 原则相反。

**C 错误**：激励干预正是被批评的。

**D 错误**：商量对象是患者圈子或同事。

---

**5.** The authors' attitude toward economic incentives that "reward intervention" is __________.

A. celebratory

B. critical, because they discourage conversation about the limits of treatment

C. irrelevant to ethics

D. aimed only at nurses

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：reward intervention and discourage talk about the limits of treatment。制度被写进伦理失败机制。

**A 错误**：discourage 是贬义。

**C 错误**：放在偏颇段，正是伦理。

**D 错误**：physicians 是主语。
