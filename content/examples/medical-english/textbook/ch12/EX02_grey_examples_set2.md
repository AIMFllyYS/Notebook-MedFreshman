## 阅读原文

> There is a grey area: capable patients who have not clearly refused information, yet physicians withhold it. The choice should maximise benefit and minimise psychological, cognitive and emotional burden. Consider instrumental benefits (decisions, options, resources) and emotional or relational benefits (coping, meaning, quieting guilt, building trust).
>
> Too much information can produce cognitive overload. A rambling list of improbable side-effects can blur relevant facts. Dwelling on an incidental benign liver cyst can obstruct talk of a serious pulmonary nodule. Telling a panic-disorder patient that the QT interval looks possibly prolonged heightens fear of heart disease. A single diagnostically meaningless blast cell on a child's blood count can devastate parents. Explaining to a hospice patient that she has five rather than three metastases is inferior to discussing symptom relief. Rehearsing a technical defect in still-usable mammogram films is a poorer use of time than reinforcing other health behaviours.

---

**1.** The grey-area rule of thumb is to __________.

A. dump every laboratory number to avoid lawsuits

B. weigh instrumental and relational benefits against cognitive-emotional burden

C. always hide cancer diagnoses

D. let only lawyers speak to families

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：maximise benefit ... minimise burden；instrumental / emotional-relational。

**A 错误**：overload 正是要避免的。

**C 错误**：灰色地带不是「凡癌皆瞒」。

**D 错误**：无此内容。

---

**2.** The liver-cyst example is meant to show that __________.

A. benign cysts never exist

B. an incidental finding can steal the agenda from a serious problem

C. pulmonary nodules should be ignored

D. CT scans are unethical

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：incidental ... obstruct discussion of a serious disease。

**A 错误**：囊肿可以是真的良性发现。

**C 错误**：严重的是肺结节。

**D 错误**：问题在谈论议程，不是检查本身。

---

**3.** "Incidental" in "incidental benign liver cyst" means __________.

A. criminal

B. found along the way, not the reason for the test

C. imaginary

D. always more important than the nodule

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：因怀疑肺结节而扫描时发现的肝囊肿 = 附带发现。

**A、C 错误**：脱离影像学语境。

**D 错误**：课文说它会妨碍对严重疾病的讨论。

---

**4.** From the hospice "five rather than three metastases" example, we can infer that __________.

A. numerical precision is always the highest ethical good at the end of life

B. some true details have so little decision value that they cost more, in time and distress, than they give

C. hospice patients must never hear any diagnosis

D. families should be excluded from symptom-relief talks

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：inferior to discussing how to relieve symptoms ... family should take part。真，但不是此时最有用的真。

**A 错误**：作者明确说不如谈症状。

**C 错误**：不是全面封锁诊断。

**D 错误**：家属应参与护理讨论。

---

**5.** The authors' tone toward exhaustive side-effect recitation is __________.

A. admiring, as the gold standard of consent

B. critical, because it can blur what matters and block discussion of purpose and practicalities

C. legally fearful only

D. humorous about pharmacology

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：rambling recitation ... blur more relevant facts and keep both parties from discussing the purpose of treatment。

**A 错误**：不是金标准。

**C 错误**：论证是临床伦理，不是只怕告状。

**D 错误**：语气严肃。
