:::example{label="EX02 · Warming, heatwaves and schistosomiasis — Set 2"}

## 阅读原文

> Modelling studies indicate that a temperature rise of 2°C may cause 5–20% reductions of cereal grain yields in South Asia, South-East Asia and sub-Saharan Africa, significantly exacerbating undernutrition. In many urban populations, higher temperatures are estimated to raise the annual death rate from heatwaves about twofold. In China, a 2°C temperature rise would allow a 50–100% increase in the geographic distribution of water snails, and hence the potential transmission of schistosomiasis, endangering many tens of millions of people. Coastal Alaska shows that a 1°C rise in water temperature can permit summer-long bacterial proliferation in shellfish and increase gastroenteritis among consumers.
>
> In the 2003 European heatwave, female mortality was higher than male mortality. Most European studies show women at higher risk of dying in a heatwave. There may be physiological reasons why older women are at greater risk. Social factors, however, are important determinants. In the United States, elderly men appear more at risk than women — especially in Chicago in July 1995 — a vulnerability that may stem from social isolation. Evidence for social contact as a protective factor comes from case-control studies in the United States. In Paris, unmarried men had higher heatwave-related risk than married men.

**1.** What is the best title for this excerpt?

A. Heat Is Equally Deadly for Every Age and Sex

B. Warming Links Crops, Snails, Shellfish and Heat Deaths — with Gendered Social Patterns

C. Schistosomiasis Has Disappeared from China

D. Alaska's Shellfish Industry Should Relocate to Europe

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：减产、钉螺/血吸虫、贝类肠胃炎、热浪死亡，后段再拆欧洲女高、芝加哥老年男高。

**A 错误**：明确有性别、婚姻与隔离差异。

**C 错误**：钉螺分布可能增加 50–100%，不是消失。

**D 错误**：阿拉斯加是肠胃炎机制例，不是产业建议。

---

**2.** A 2°C rise in China is linked in the passage to __________.

A. a 50–100% increase in the range of water snails and thus potential schistosomiasis transmission

B. a twofold drop in urban heatwave deaths

C. the extinction of Schistosoma

D. a 5–20% increase in cereal yields

**Answer: A**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 A**：50–100% increase in the geographic distribution of water snails, and hence the potential transmission of schistosomiasis。

**B 错误**：热浪死亡是约升高两倍，不是下降；且那句写的是 many urban populations，不是中国钉螺句。

**C 错误**：是潜在传播增加。

**D 错误**：谷物是 5–20% 减产，且地点是南亚、东南亚、撒哈拉以南非洲。

---

**3.** The word "exacerbating" in paragraph 1 is closest in meaning to __________.

A. completely curing

B. making worse

C. hiding from statistics

D. relocating to cities

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：reductions of cereal grain yields ... significantly exacerbating undernutrition = 加剧营养不良。

**A 错误**：方向相反。

**C、D 错误**：无中生有。

---

**4.** From the heatwave paragraphs, it is reasonable to conclude that __________.

A. physiology alone predicts that women always die more in every country

B. social isolation and marital status can reverse or reshape who is at highest risk

C. case-control studies have disproved any role for social contact

D. Chicago in 1995 showed the same female excess as Europe in 2003

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：欧洲女高，但美国老年男高 tied to isolation；巴黎未婚男性风险更高；social contact 是保护因素。

**A 错误**：There may be physiological reasons 之后立刻 however social factors。

**C 错误**：case-control 支持 social contact 的保护作用。

**D 错误**：芝加哥被写成老年男性更危险，与 2003 欧洲女高不同。

---

**5.** The author's attitude toward "physiological reasons" for older women's heat risk is __________.

A. they may exist, but they are not allowed to crowd out social determinants

B. they are the only scientifically respectable explanation

C. they should be mocked as folklore

D. they prove that men need no heatwave planning

**Answer: A**

**解析**：本题为**观点态度题**。

**正确答案 A**：There may be physiological reasons ... Social factors, however, are important determinants。让步 + 转折。

**B 错误**：however 限制了「唯一」。

**C 错误**：没有嘲讽生理。

**D 错误**：芝加哥与巴黎的例子正是男性风险。
