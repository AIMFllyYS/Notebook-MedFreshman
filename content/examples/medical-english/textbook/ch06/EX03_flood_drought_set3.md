:::example{label="EX03 · Flooding, drought and labour — Set 3"}

## 阅读原文

> Flooding is one of the most widespread climatic hazards that pose multiple risks to human health, yet there has been only limited systematic research on the health outcomes of flooding. In southwestern Bangladesh, waterlogging has become urgent. Women are often the family's main caregivers, shouldering the work of managing and cooking food, collecting drinking water, and taking care of household members and livestock. Tube wells are often contaminated, and women are forced to remain and drink unsafe water. Pregnant women, limited in isolated and slippery environments, are often forced to stay indoors. Local health workers have reported a rising trend of gynaecological problems linked to unclean water. Men often leave the area to look for work, and are usually not affected in the same way.
>
> Fuel shortages increase women's workload where they are responsible for collection; an estimated 2.4 billion people currently rely on biomass fuels for cooking and heating, impacting health by increasing the risk of COPD and respiratory symptoms, as well as worsening lung function, while simultaneously exacerbating global warming. Empirical evidence points to a link between drought and suicide among men in Australia, associated with rural masculine norms of stoicism and health-damaging behaviour. Similar patterns have been reported in India after drought in semi-arid regions.

---

**1.** The passage as a whole suggests that climate hazards __________.

A. harm only women, never men

B. distribute harm through gendered labour and norms: water and fuel work for many women, drought-suicide patterns among some rural men

C. have been exhaustively studied in every floodplain

D. can be solved solely by distributing more biomass stoves

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：孟加拉妇女取水做饭与妇科问题；24 亿人生物燃料与 COPD；澳大利亚/印度男性干旱自杀。

**A 错误**：男性外出打工、男性自杀都被写进账。

**C 错误**：only limited systematic research。

**D 错误**：生物燃料本身在加剧 COPD 与变暖。

---

**2.** In the Bangladesh example, men were usually less affected because __________.

A. they are physiologically immune to unclean water

B. they often leave the flooded area to seek work

C. tube wells are reserved for men by law

D. they do all the cooking indoors

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：Men often leave the area to look for work, and are usually not affected in the same way.

**A 错误**：不是生理免疫。

**C 错误**：管井被污染，妇女被迫饮用不洁水。

**D 错误**：做饭是妇女肩负的劳动。

---

**3.** The word "shouldering" in paragraph 1 is closest in meaning to __________.

A. injuring the shoulder while fetching water

B. bearing responsibility for

C. refusing to do

D. supervising men from a distance

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：shouldering the work of managing and cooking food, collecting drinking water, and taking care of ... = 承担这些劳动。

**A 错误**：把 shoulder 身体化。

**C、D 错误**：与并列的劳动清单相反。

---

**4.** From the biomass-fuel sentence, we can infer that switching nothing but the quantity of firewood collected would __________.

A. automatically cancel global warming

B. still leave a double bind: more female labour plus COPD plus climate forcing, unless the fuel and labour structure change

C. cure schistosomiasis in China

D. eliminate drought suicide in Australia

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：workload + COPD + exacerbating global warming 三线并行。只加柴火解决不了结构。

**A 错误**：课文说同时加剧变暖。

**C、D 错误**：张冠李戴到其他段落。

---

**5.** The author's mention of stoicism among Australian farmers is meant to __________.

A. praise rural men as morally superior

B. identify a gendered norm that may channel drought stress into suicide

C. claim that Indian farmers copy Australian culture

D. deny that debt and lost income matter

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**写作目的 / 态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：associated with rural masculine norms of stoicism and health-damaging behaviour，用来解释干旱—自杀链。

**A 错误**：health-damaging 不是赞美。

**C 错误**：India 是 similar patterns，不是文化抄袭。

**D 错误**：前句已写 lost income and related debt。
