:::example{label="EX04 · Equity, data and health systems — Set 4"}

## 阅读原文

> Equity and social justice cannot be achieved without recognising the different vulnerabilities and strengths of women and men, and the many other factors that produce vulnerability. Such recognition is an indispensable part of any attempt to address the different health impacts of environmental change. Gender-sensitive research is necessary for a better overall understanding of climate change, especially the health effects of extreme events. We urgently need to collect, analyse and report data disaggregated by age and sex, and to use other stratifiers that allow thorough gender analysis. We need to build gender-equal, accessible health systems that reach the poorest, so as to address the particular health needs of women and men across the life course.
>
> Even after future agreements to decrease greenhouse-gas emissions, climate change will still have increasing adverse impacts on water systems within several decades. Drought already threatens well-being in arid, semi-arid and sub-humid regions, and is associated with shortages of fuel, food and water, conflict, mass migration, and restricted access to health care.

---

**1.** The author's primary recommendation is to __________.

A. wait for emissions agreements to erase health impacts within a year

B. recognise gendered vulnerabilities and build disaggregated data plus accessible, gender-equal systems

C. treat men and women with identical protocols in every clinic

D. stop studying extreme events and study only average temperature

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：recognising differences；data disaggregated by age and sex；gender-equal, accessible health systems。

**A 错误**：协议之后仍有几十年加重影响。

**C 错误**：particular health needs 不是同一套协议。

**D 错误**：especially the health effects of extreme events。

---

**2.** Data "disaggregated by age and sex" means data that __________.

A. mix all ages and sexes into one national average only

B. are broken down so that age groups and sexes can be analysed separately

C. exclude women over 65

D. are collected only in capital cities

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节 / 词义题**。

**正确答案 B**：disaggregated = 分列；other stratifiers that allow thorough gender analysis 进一步说明要能分层。

**A 错误**：与 disaggregated 相反。

**C、D 错误**：文中要求覆盖最穷人群与整个 life course。

---

**3.** The word "accessible" in "accessible health systems" is closest in meaning to __________.

A. written in simple English textbooks

B. reachable by those who need care, including the poorest

C. open only to urban men

D. digitally fashionable

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：that reach the poorest；across the life course。公共卫生语境的可及。

**A 错误**：不是课文易读性。

**C 错误**：与 gender-equal 相反。

**D 错误**：无中生有。

---

**4.** It can be inferred that signing a future emissions treaty __________.

A. makes water-system planning unnecessary for the next few decades

B. does not remove the need for health adaptation, because impacts will still intensify for decades

C. will immediately equalise male and female death rates

D. contradicts the call for gender-sensitive research

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：Even after future agreements ... still have increasing adverse impacts ... within several decades。

**A 错误**：与 still increasing 相反。

**C 错误**：条约不自动取消性别差异。

**D 错误**：研究与适应仍必要。

---

**5.** The tone of the closing policy paragraph is __________.

A. fatalistic, claiming nothing can be done

B. urgent and programmatic, listing recognition, data, and system design as required steps

C. mocking the idea of social justice

D. purely meteorological, with no interest in health systems

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：urgently need；We need to build；indispensable。祈使与清单，不是放弃。

**A 错误**：列出可做的事。

**C 错误**：equity and social justice 是目标。

**D 错误**：health systems 是最后一句主语。
