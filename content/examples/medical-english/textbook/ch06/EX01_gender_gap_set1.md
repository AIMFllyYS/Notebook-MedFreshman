:::example{label="EX01 · Gendered mortality in disasters — Set 1"}

## 阅读原文

> There is evidence that men and women experience different negative health consequences when facing extreme events such as floods, droughts and heatwaves. While disasters bring suffering to everyone, natural disasters generally kill more women, or kill women at a younger age than men. These differences are in proportion to the severity of the disasters and are also related to women's relative socio-economic status in the affected countries. The effects are strongest in countries where women's socio-economic and political status is low. In countries where women and men have similar status, natural disasters affect men and women almost equally.
>
> A study based on information from 147 countries has highlighted that physical differences between men and women are unlikely to explain these differences and social norms may provide some additional explanation. Natural disasters reduced women's life expectancy more than men's. Because women generally live longer than men, disasters actually narrowed the life-expectancy gap in most countries. The study emphasised the socially constructed, gender-specific vulnerability of women to natural disasters.

---

**1.** The passage is mainly concerned with __________.

A. proving that women are biologically weaker than men in every climate

B. showing that disaster deaths are gendered, and that status and norms — not mere physiology — explain much of the gap

C. arguing that natural disasters do not kill men

D. recommending that meteorology ignore social science

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：不同负面后果 + 与严重程度成正比、与地位相关 + 147 国否定单纯生理解释。

**A 错误**：physical differences are unlikely to explain。

**C 错误**：disasters bring suffering to everyone，只是女性往往死得更多或更早。

**D 错误**：结论走向社会规范与地位，不是排斥社会科学。

---

**2.** According to the 147-country study, disasters narrowed the life-expectancy gap because __________.

A. men started living as long as women after each storm

B. women's life expectancy fell more than men's, cutting into women's usual longevity advantage

C. hospitals began recording age more accurately

D. women migrated to safer countries

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：reduced women's life expectancy more than men's；Because women generally live longer ... disasters actually narrowed the gap。

**A 错误**：不是男性寿命上升。

**C、D 错误**：无中生有。

---

**3.** The phrase "in proportion to" in paragraph 1 means that the gender difference in deaths __________.

A. stays constant in every disaster

B. grows as disasters become more severe

C. is randomly distributed

D. appears only in earthquakes, not floods

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：in proportion to the severity = 随严重程度增大而增大。后文 major disasters hit women's life expectancy harder 同向。

**A 错误**：成比例不是恒定。

**C 错误**：否定规律。

**D 错误**：floods, droughts and heatwaves 都在清单里。

---

**4.** It can be inferred that in a country with near gender-equal political and economic status, a severe flood would __________.

A. still kill vastly more women solely because of physiology

B. be expected to produce a smaller gender gap in deaths than in a low-status-for-women country

C. leave women unaffected

D. invert the pattern so that only men die

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：status similar → affect men and women almost equally；effects strongest where women's status is low。

**A 错误**：与 147 国结论相反。

**C、D 错误**：过度推断。

---

**5.** The author's attitude toward explaining the death gap by "physical differences" is __________.

A. supportive, treating physiology as sufficient

B. sceptical, pointing instead to social norms and constructed vulnerability

C. sarcastic about all epidemiology

D. indifferent to causes, caring only about weather forecasts

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**观点态度题**。

**正确答案 B**：unlikely to explain ... social norms may provide some additional explanation；socially constructed, gender-specific vulnerability。

**A 错误**：直接否定足够性。

**C 错误**：作者在用流行病学研究，不是讽刺流行病学。

**D 错误**：全文在谈原因与公平。
