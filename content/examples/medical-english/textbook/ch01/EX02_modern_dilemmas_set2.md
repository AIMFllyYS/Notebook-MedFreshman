## 阅读原文

> The reintroduction of the oath has been problematic. Medical ethics are complex. They must balance patient expectations, social demands and taboos, economic and political realities, and evolving medical and scientific knowledge. For instance, the original oath required patients to be cured regardless of circumstances. However, using placebos in double-blind trials, considered essential for drug development, means doctors do not attempt a cure. The original oath would also forbid triage. This is used during war or disasters when treatment is prioritised based on patients' survival chances. Different medical care for patients with or without health insurance would be impossible. Some dangerous types of chemotherapy using toxic drugs in high doses would be disallowed. Finally, the original oath prohibits assisted euthanasia to relieve the suffering of patients with incurable conditions.
>
> Therefore people argue that the original Hippocratic Oath is invalid in a society that has seen drastic socio-economic, political and moral changes. This has led to the modification of the oath. Four of the most widely used versions nowadays are the Declaration of Geneva, the Prayer of Maimonides, the Oath of Lasagna, and the Reinstatement of Hippocratic Oath. Although they differ in wording and content, the main tenets are the same — treat patients to the best of one's abilities, never cause intentional harm, and maintain patient confidentiality — although none of them call upon various deities to punish the physician if he transgresses, save for the Reinstatement.
>
> Unlike the original, the new oath does not require that doctors swear it before they can practise. Instead, it is a code of practice, and a symbol for young doctors entering the profession and their commitment to healing.

---

**1.** The author's main point in paragraph 1 is that __________.

A. double-blind trials have made medical ethics unnecessary

B. a literal original oath collides with several necessary modern practices

C. triage should be abolished because it violates patient expectations

D. health insurance differences are required by the original oath

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**（段落主旨）。

**正确答案 B**：required patients to be cured regardless of circumstances 被五项现代做法撕开：安慰剂、分诊、医保差异、高剂量化疗、协助安乐死。

**A 错误**：trials 被当作 essential，不是用来取消伦理。

**C 错误**：作者用 triage 说明原文行不通，不是主张废除分诊。

**D 错误**：原文会使不同医保待遇 impossible，与 D 相反。

---

**2.** According to the passage, which of the following is true of modern versions of the oath?

A. All four still invoke deities to punish transgressors.

B. They share three main tenets despite different wording.

C. They restore the original requirement that one must swear before practising.

D. The Oath of Lasagna is the only version used outside the United States.

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：the main tenets are the same 后列出三条。

**A 错误**：none of them ... save for the Reinstatement，只有恢复本仍召神灵。

**C 错误**：new oath does not require that doctors swear it before they can practise。

**D 错误**：文中未比较四国使用范围。

---

**3.** The phrase "save for" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to __________.

A. in addition to

B. because of

C. except for

D. instead of

**Answer: C**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 C**：none of them call upon deities ... save for the Reinstatement = 除恢复本外都不再召神。except for。

**A 错误**：in addition to 会把恢复本加成「也召神」之外还有别的，逻辑反了。

**B、D 错误**：because of 表原因，instead of 表替代，都不接在 none ... save for 这种排除结构上。

---

**4.** It can be inferred that calling the original oath "invalid" in this context means __________.

A. medical students should stop studying history of ethics

B. the original wording cannot be applied unchanged to present practice

C. the three tenets have been scientifically falsified

D. the Declaration of Geneva has legal force in every country

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：invalid 后立刻接 modification ... better suited for our times，而 tenets 明确还在。

**A 错误**：过激，文中仍讲历史与象征。

**C 错误**：三条信条被说成 the same，不是被证伪。

**D 错误**：Geneva 只是四版本之一，未赋予全球法律效力。

---

**5.** The author's attitude toward modifying the oath is __________.

A. hostile, because any change betrays Hippocrates

B. indifferent, treating versions as a matter of fashion

C. pragmatic, accepting modification while keeping the core tenets and a symbolic code of practice

D. cynical, implying that a code of practice is only public relations

**Answer: C**

**解析**：本题为**观点态度题**。

**正确答案 C**：problematic / invalid as original text → modification；同时 tenets are the same，new oath = code of practice and symbol。

**A 错误**：作者列出必须改的理由，不是卫道士。

**B 错误**：四版本被认真点名，不是时尚。

**D 错误**：commitment to healing 是正面收束，不是公关讽刺。
