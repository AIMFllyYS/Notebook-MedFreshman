## 阅读原文

> The Hippocratic Oath was not very well received when it was first penned, being a representation of only a minor segment of Greek opinion at the time. However, by the end of ancient times, physicians began conforming to the conditions of the oath. It is possible that when scientific medicine suffered a gross decline after the fall of the Roman Empire, this oath, along with the dictates of Hippocratic medicine, was all but forgotten in the West. It was through the perseverance of the spirit of inquiry in the East that the tenets survived this period of deterioration, notably through the writings of Arabian authorities in medicine. Greek medicine was later revived in the Christian West through Latin translations of both these Arabic works and the original Greek texts.
>
> The first code of medical ethics to be adopted by a professional organisation was written by English physician Thomas Percival in 1794, which was adapted and adopted by the American Medical Association (AMA) in 1846. After World War II, 23 doctors from Nazi Germany concentration camps were found guilty of breaching the code of medical ethics by performing horrifying medical experiments on prisoners. This incident led to the composition of the Nuremberg Code (1947), which represented the starting point in discussions regarding ethical treatment of human subjects. This in turn led to the adoption of the Declaration of Geneva oath by the World Medical Association in 1948.
>
> Modification of the original oath is unavoidable — Hippocrates, being meticulous in his scientific methods, would surely have approved of the changes for the sake of keeping up with the developments of the world — but as long as our physicians hold true to the basic tenets, it would continue to serve mankind for long after the name of its writer has been forgotten.

---

**1.** Which of the following best states the main idea of the whole excerpt?

A. The Oath died with the Roman Empire and was never recovered.

B. The Oath's survival and later codes show a living standard that can be rewritten without dropping its tenets.

C. Percival copied the Nuremberg Code in 1794.

D. Arabian authorities rejected Greek medicine as unscientific.

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：东传西回 → Percival/AMA → 纽伦堡/日内瓦 → 结尾 modification unavoidable but tenets remain。

**A 错误**：all but forgotten 之后立刻 survived / revived。

**C 错误**：年代反了：Percival 1794，Nuremberg 1947。

**D 错误**：阿拉伯著作是幸存机制，不是拒绝希腊医学。

---

**2.** The Nuremberg Code (1947) was composed primarily in response to __________.

A. AMA's 1846 adoption of Percival's code

B. the conviction of 23 camp doctors who experimented on prisoners

C. the Latin translation movement in medieval Europe

D. Hippocrates' own demand for a research protocol

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：23 doctors ... horrifying medical experiments on prisoners. This incident led to ... Nuremberg Code (1947)。对象是 human subjects。

**A 错误**：AMA 1846 是前史，不是纽伦堡的直接起因。

**C、D 错误**：中世纪译本与希波克拉底本人都不是 1947 的触发事件。

---

**3.** In paragraph 1, "all but forgotten" is closest in meaning to __________.

A. completely destroyed by law

B. almost forgotten

C. remembered only by patients

D. deliberately erased by Arabian writers

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：all but = almost。后文 survived / revived 也证明不是绝对消失。

**A 错误**：没有法律消灭。

**C 错误**：无此信息。

**D 错误**：Arabian authorities 是保存者。

---

**4.** From the last paragraph, we can infer that the writer of the Oath __________.

A. must be identified before the tenets can be used

B. would, in the author's view, have accepted updates that keep pace with knowledge

C. forbade any later code such as Percival's

D. is known to have opposed the Declaration of Geneva

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：being meticulous ... would surely have approved of the changes for the sake of keeping up with the developments of the world。

**A 错误**：for long after the name of its writer has been forgotten 直接否定「必须先确认作者」。

**C、D 错误**：文中无禁止 Percival 或反对日内瓦的内容。

---

**5.** The author's tone toward Arabian medical authorities is __________.

A. grateful, presenting them as the channel through which tenets survived a Western decline

B. sarcastic, implying their writings delayed the Renaissance

C. hostile, blaming them for deterioration of scientific medicine

D. indifferent, mentioning them only as a date marker

**Answer: A**

**解析**：本题为**观点态度题**。

**正确答案 A**：through the perseverance of the spirit of inquiry in the East ... notably through the writings of Arabian authorities；随后拉丁译本回流。

**B、C 错误**：deterioration 发生在西罗马帝国衰亡之后的西方，不是阿拉伯作者造成的。

**D 错误**：notably 表示被当作关键机制，不是日期装饰。
