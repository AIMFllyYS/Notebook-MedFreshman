## 阅读原文

> The majority of medical terms in current use are composed of Greek and Latin word parts that maintain the same meaning wherever they appear. A combining vowel links a root to a suffix or to another root to ease pronunciation. Use a combining vowel to connect the root and the suffix, but drop the combining vowel before a suffix beginning with a vowel: rhin/o/scopy, but mamm/itis. Keep the combining vowel between two roots even if the second root begins with a vowel: gastr/o/enter/itis.
>
> A suffix is attached to the end of a root. Noun suffixes meaning "condition" include -ia, -iasis, -ism, -sis. Noun suffixes meaning specialty or specialist include -logy, -ist, -ian. Adjective suffixes meaning "pertaining to" include -ac (cardiac), -ic (hepatic), -al (bronchial). Negative prefixes include a-/an- (without), anti- (against), contra- (against, opposite).
>
> Competing forms arise because both Greek and Latin supply roots: dent/ist beside odont/o/logy; uter/o/plasty beside hyster/ectomy. Do not pronounce a long term by stressing each element separately: peri/card/itis is /ˌperikɑːˈdaɪtɪs/, not a chain of three stressed pieces. -itis is always pronounced /ˈaɪtɪs/; -oma /ˈəʊmə/; -osis /ˈəʊsɪs/.
>
> Surgical endings must be kept apart: -tomy is an incision into an organ; -ectomy is excision (removal); -stomy is a surgical opening to the outside of the body. Nephrectomy removes a kidney; cystotomy opens the bladder.

---

**1.** What is the main idea of the passage?

A. Medical terms should be memorised as unanalysable wholes.

B. Stable Greek and Latin parts, plus a few linking and stress rules, unlock medical vocabulary.

C. Only Latin roots are acceptable in modern hospitals.

D. Pronunciation always follows the stress of each word element.

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：开篇 maintain the same meaning；中段连接元音、前后缀、竞争形式；末段手术后缀与重音。整篇是「零件 + 规则」。

**A 错误**：与 word parts 分析相反。

**C 错误**：希腊、拉丁并用，还有 competing forms。

**D 错误**：末段明确不要按零件重音去念。

---

**2.** According to the passage, which form is constructed correctly?

A. mammoitis, because a combining vowel is always kept

B. rhinscopy, because -o- must be dropped before every suffix

C. gastrenteritis, because the second root begins with a vowel

D. gastr/o/enter/itis, because the combining vowel is kept between two roots

**Answer: D**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 D**：Keep the combining vowel between two roots even if the second root begins with a vowel: gastr/o/enter/itis。

**A 错误**：mamm/itis 是丢掉连接元音的例（后缀以元音开头）。

**B 错误**：rhin/o/scopy 保留 o，因为 -scopy 以辅音开头。

**C 错误**：两词根之间要留 o。

---

**3.** The word "competing" in "competing forms" most nearly means __________.

A. hostile rival schools that refuse to use each other's words

B. two historically different roots or spellings that cover the same meaning

C. incorrect forms that students must never write

D. trademarks owned by Greek and Latin dictionaries

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：dent/ist beside odont/o/logy；uter/o/plasty beside hyster/ectomy —— 同一意义、两套零件。

**A 错误**：不是学派吵架。

**C 错误**：它们都是合法医学用词。

**D 错误**：无中生有的商标说。

---

**4.** It can be inferred that a student who pronounces pericarditis as three equally stressed chunks __________.

A. is following the Latin accent system correctly

B. has confused etymological cuts with syllabic stress

C. will automatically be understood by all clinicians

D. is applying the rule for plural -i pronounced /aɪ/

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：Do not pronounce a long term by stressing each element separately。词素切分 ≠ 重音切分。

**A 错误**：课文说医学词大体走拉丁重音，但禁止按零件重读。

**C 错误**：错误重音正是课件要改的。

**D 错误**：-i 读 /aɪ/ 是复数词尾规则，与 pericarditis 无关。

---

**5.** The author's purpose in contrasting -tomy, -ectomy and -stomy is __________.

A. to warn that a slide glossing -ectomy as "incision" would mix up two operations

B. to claim that all three suffixes mean "inflammation"

C. to argue that nephrectomy is merely opening the kidney

D. to show that surgical suffixes are no longer used

**Answer: A**

**解析**：本题为**写作目的 / 态度题**。

**正确答案 A**：-tomy incision vs -ectomy excision 被特意分开，nephrectomy removes a kidney。课件曾把 -ectomy 误标为 Incision，对照题意就是防这种混账。

**B 错误**：炎症是 -itis。

**C 错误**：与 removes a kidney 相反。

**D 错误**：例词都在用。
