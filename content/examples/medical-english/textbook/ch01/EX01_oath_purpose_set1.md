## 阅读原文

> Perhaps the most enduring — certainly the most quoted — tradition in the history of medicine is the Hippocratic Oath. Named after the famous Greek physician Hippocrates, this oath was written as a guideline for the medical ethics of doctors. Although the exact words have changed over time, the general content is the same — an oath to respect those who have imparted their knowledge upon the science of medicine, and respect to the patients as well as the promise to treat them to the best of the physicians' ability.
>
> For a man considered by many to be the "Father of Medicine", little is known about Hippocrates of Cos. Over 60 treatises of the Hippocratic Corpus have been attributed to him; however, these treatises had conflicting contents and were written some time between 510 and 300 BC, and therefore could not all have been written by him. The Oath was named after Hippocrates, certainly, although its penmanship is still in question. Anyway, regardless of whether or not Hippocrates himself had written the Oath, the contents of the oath reflect his views on medical ethics.
>
> G. E. R. Lloyd said of Hippocratic medicine: "In the Western world, the name Hippocrates has always stood for an ideal." And this is what the oath is all about — an ideal gold ethics standard representing a clear dividing line separating healers and killers, a commitment that physicians make to protect life, and never to take life away deliberately.

---

**1.** What is the primary purpose of this passage?

A. To prove that Hippocrates personally drafted every treatise in the Hippocratic Corpus.

B. To explain why the Oath remains an ethical ideal even though its wording and authorship are uncertain.

C. To argue that modern medical schools should restore the original Greek wording of the Oath.

D. To show that the Oath was already the majority view among ancient Greek physicians.

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**主旨大意题**。

**正确答案 B**：开篇锁定 enduring / most quoted tradition；中段承认 little is known、penmanship in question、文集不可能全是他写的；收束用 Lloyd 的 ideal 与 dividing line。全文要证明的是「理想仍在」，不是作者已核实。

**A 错误**：第 2 段明确 could not all have been written by him，与 A 相反。

**C 错误**：文中说 exact words have changed，并未主张恢复希腊原文。

**D 错误**：完整课文第 4 段写它只代表 a minor segment of Greek opinion，开篇也未把它写成当时主流。

---

**2.** According to the passage, why could the Hippocratic Corpus not all have been written by Hippocrates?

A. The treatises were all composed after the fall of the Roman Empire.

B. The treatises contradict one another and span a period longer than one lifetime.

C. Arabian authorities later forged the Corpus in Latin.

D. Socrates denied that Hippocrates was a practising physician.

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**细节理解题**。

**正确答案 B**：原文 conflicting contents and were written some time between 510 and 300 BC, and therefore could not all have been written by him。冲突 + 时间窗过宽 = 不可能一人完成。

**A 错误**：罗马帝国衰亡是后文知识史，不是文集年代。

**C 错误**：阿拉伯著作保存的是信条，不是伪造 Corpus。

**D 错误**：课文只说他是苏格拉底同时代人，没有苏格拉底的否认。

---

**3.** The word "penmanship" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to __________.

A. beautiful handwriting used in medieval manuscripts

B. the identity of the person who actually wrote the text

C. the legal right to practise medicine after graduation

D. the ceremony at which students swear the Oath

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**词义猜测题**。

**正确答案 B**：named after Hippocrates, certainly, although its penmanship is still in question 与后文 whether or not Hippocrates himself had written 对举，penmanship = 执笔身份，不是书法课。

**A 错误**：把 -manship 字面化成「书法」，脱离语境。

**C、D 错误**：把后文毕业宣誓、执业资格提前塞进这个词。

---

**4.** What can be inferred from Lloyd's remark that the name Hippocrates "has always stood for an ideal"?

A. Historians have finally verified every biographical detail of Hippocrates of Cos.

B. The cultural function of the name matters more, in this argument, than settling authorship.

C. Only Western medicine is entitled to use the Oath.

D. Killers and healers can no longer be distinguished in modern hospitals.

**Answer: B**

**解析**：本题为**推理判断题**。

**正确答案 B**：前文刚说 little is known、作者存疑，紧接着用 stood for an ideal 收口——名字的符号功能大于传记核实。

**A 错误**：与 little is known 矛盾。

**C 错误**：Lloyd 说的是 Western world 里这个名字的含义，不是排他执照。

**D 错误**：誓言正是要划 dividing line，不是宣布分界消失。

---

**5.** What is the author's attitude toward the Hippocratic Oath in these paragraphs?

A. Dismissive, treating it as a sentimental antique.

B. Uncritical, claiming the original words must never change.

C. Respectful of its ethical core while remaining sceptical about authorship and exact wording.

D. Ironic, implying that "Father of Medicine" is only a marketing title.

**Answer: C**

**解析**：本题为**观点态度题**。

**正确答案 C**：尊重 general content / gold ethics standard，同时承认 words have changed、penmanship in question。

**A 错误**：most enduring / most quoted / ideal 不是轻蔑。

**B 错误**：Although the exact words have changed 已经允许改字。

**D 错误**：Father of Medicine 加引号是传说需核实，不是整段反讽。
